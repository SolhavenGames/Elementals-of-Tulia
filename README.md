# ELEMENTALS!
## The Timeline will be stored here!
## Timeline of the story begins in the Satan's Era of Tulia, i.e. the March on Tramalen.
- March on Tramalen establishes Firen Knightsdale / Irision (T-Year 900)
- Seige of Set establishes Poisi Knightsdale / Tixolam (T-Year 901)
- The Great Massacre in Icik/Snai capitals establishes Fraklen and Swailen (Icik/Snai Knightsdales). (T-Year 902)
- Rutt (Naturine) & Wavalo (Watrish) form the Phlanax of Freedom, a military alliance against Irision. (T-Year 905 - Present)
- Poisi-Assisted Windstorm Protests coup in Airren Nation establishes Knightsdale / Wrenao (Airren). (T-Year 905)
- Remaining Free States (Roccian Delekt, Ballad Ralia, Sakai Bunnis) join the Phlanax. (T-Year 905)
- Tixolam (Poisi) begins investigations and tests revolving around Forbidden Elements. (T-Year 906)
- Knightsdale Nations form the Independent Coven, a "defensive" military alliance. (T-Year 906 - Present)
- Tixolam begins human tests, starting a "Perfect-Warrior" program with Bane being given to ***Warrior-000 "Zero"***. (T-Year 907)
- Dal yon Tulia starts, a deadly global war between the Coven and Phlanax, displacing and killing millions within the first months. (T-Year 908 - Present)
- First refugees flee to Earth, most die in space. (T-Year 908)
- ***Rai*** & ***Arete*** flee in the first refugee group. (T-Year 908) (E-Year 2020)
- Coven nations begin confescating and stealing Space equipment, heavily hindering attempts to flee to Earth (T-Year 909). 
- **_Zero_**, along with the 6 other soldiers in his division, are deployed to their first missions, assassinations across Ralia (Ballad Nation)
and Wavalo (Watrish Nation). (T-Year 909)
- **_Zero_** reaches Bane Gate II, the first to do so. (T-Year 909)
- **_Spara_** defects from the Icik Army, hiding out deep in The Far South. (T-Year 909)
- The Forbidden Element Death is born from the mass murders in Dal yon Tulia, catching the God's attention for a moment. (T-Year 909)
- **_Dera_** is drafted, splitting him from his sister **_Teira_**, who flees to Sharlin, taking refuge in refugee camps. (T-Year 909)
- **_Aeri_** meets **_Dera_** briefly, listening to her trauma as a guard of the refugee camp, changing their perspective on
the poisi population. (T-Year 909)
- **_Adva_** dodges the Airren draft, living in ruined cities called "No Man's Land". (T-Year 910)
- **_Kael_** watches his family get massacred by Icik soldiers for protesting. (T-Year 910)
- Knightsdale Youth programs are exposed, forcing the Phlanax to treat every non-Phlanax born with caution. (T-Year 910)
- **_Ember_** and **_Adva_** meet in Wrenao, sharing information and growing to trust each other. (T-Year 910).
- **_Aeri_** is discharged from the Sharlin Military and sentenced to "Godly Intervention", sent to the countryside to live off the land and
grow closer to the Light element. (T-910)
- **_Aeri_** is given Warshine by Light, who keeps this a secret from the rest of the gods. (T-Year 911)
- **_Kael_** is taken as a POW by Phlanax costal raids and sent to Rutt. (T-Year 911)
- Nature realizes that **_Ember_** has Hellfire sleeping within her, building suspicion around the supposedly locked element. (T-Year 911)
- **_Spara_** is found by Snai soldiers, being forced to fight her way out and fleeing to Bunnis. (T-Year 912)
- Fire element swears to know nothing about **_Ember's_** Hellfire, telling the truth. Suspicion and distrust rises among the gods. (T-Year 912)
- ***Ember*** & ***Adva*** find and recruit ***Kael***, who calls himself ***Vael***. (T-Year 918)
- Gods discover **_Aeri's_** Warshine, beginning the Great God War as they scramble to ensure their people survive. (T-Year 918)
- ***Ember***, ***Adva***, and ***Kael*** arrive to Earth. (T-Year 918)
- **_Ember_** awakens, being visited by the Fire god in an attempt to indoctrinate her. (T-Year 918) (E-Year 2033)
- **_Kael_** saves **_Ember's_** life, nearly sacrificing his own, resulting in her reaching Threshold III in Gate I for the first time.
- ***Kael*** is returned to Tulia by Coven soldeirs, infected with Death as a test subject in Tixolam (T-Year 918)
- Accusasions of Tixolam using Forbidden Elements or banned technology rise, which Tixolam does not respond to. (T-Year 918)
- Coven and Phlanax nations alike begin their own Forbidden Element experiments. (T-Year 919 - Present)
