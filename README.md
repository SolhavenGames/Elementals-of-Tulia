# ELEMENTALS OF TULIA / VALORAN UNIVERSE | THE TIMELINE
# TULIA ARC
<sub> Events that affect all characters will be listed without names. <sub>
## T-Year 900
- **March on Tramalen establishes Firen Knightsdale / Irision, causing an international crisis.**
- [IS'ALE](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/IsaleArcTulia.md) | Is'ale and his group pushes for more Anti-Knightsdale reform in Rutt (Naturine Nation). They are mostly ignored.
- [KTERLA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md) | Kterla is sent to Gaea for safety after surviving multiple assassination attempts.
- [K'ZORAK](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KzorakArcTulia.md) | K'zorak and other journalists are targetted when Irision is established, an open attack on Free Speech in Ralia (Ballad Nation).
## T-Year 901
- [KTERLA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md), [TERKOU](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md) | The two, now being residents of Gaea, cooperate with the local governments. Together, they form Endsight, an organization made for Elementals by Elementals on Gaea. (E-YEAR 2008)
- [ARETE](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/AreteArcTulia.md) | Arete and her family ramp up their charity efforts, becoming very publicly Anti-Knightsdale.
- **The Seige of Set begins, and the Poisi Nation falls to Knightsdale, becoming Tixolam.**
- [SAMAE](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) | Samae is born on Gaea. (E-YEAR 2010)
- [KTERLA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md), [TERKOU](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md) | Kterla stops radicalizations of Endsight when she discovers Terkou's actions. He still continues them behind her back. (E-YEAR 2010)
- [DERA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/DeraArcTulia.md), [TEIRA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TeiraArcTulia.md) | The Kni-Soa siblings, being residents of Set, struggle under the new regime. Their formerly powerful family loses a major chunk of their influence.
- **The two Knightsdales block international investigations surrounding the health, freedom, and safety of its citizens.**
## T-Year 902
- [TERKOU](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md) | Terkou ramps up his radicalizations of Endsight. (E-YEAR 2011)
- [IMBRA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/ImbraArcTulia.md) | Imbra is forced to return home after higher education in Irision is abolished.
- **The Great Massacre causes the Icik and Snai to fall to Knightsdales, forming Fraklen (Icik Nation) and Swalien (Snai Nation).**
- [KAEL](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KaelArcTulia.md) | Kael returns home from higher education when his nation falls to Knightsdale.
- **Swailen falls into a civil war when protesters kill the nations leader, forcing Fraklen to intervene.**
- [SPARA](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SparaArcTulia.md) | Out of fear, Spara cooperates with her new leaders. She is sent to the Snai nation to assist in stabilizing the nation.
- [ARETE](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/AreteArcTulia.md) | Arete and her family halt all operations around the globe due to spreading Knightsdales and fear of their safety. Arete does not agree with this choice.
- **Knightsdale Movements temporarily succeed in Rutt democratically, though they don't have enough representation to have influence. Though, they do manage to get Anti-Knightsdale groups locked out of the government.**
- [IS'ALE](https://github.com/RosemaryKai/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/IsaleArcTulia.md) | Upon the rising Knightsdale movement in Rutt, Is'ale and his group begin a staunch public media push against them, exposing their corruption and evil.
## T-Year 903
- **International Knightsdale plans are derailed by the civil war in Swailen.**
