# Aeri "Warsight" Glem | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_War always shines when I'm there! Get it? Cause.. Warshine..._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A young unit of Endsight under Terkou, being trained in combat with dual swords that connect at the handles.

**Ending State**: A feared and respected warrior & rival of Paradox (Dera). 

**Inciting Moments**: Slaughtering the military of an Anti-Elemental nation, as per orders.

**Irreversable Actions**: Killing the leader of a nation on public broadcast.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the most public figure of Endsight's task force, building further cross-species resentment.

**Ending Alignment**: Endsight (As Warsight).

## Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Aeri spends the short antarctica nights staring up at the stars. Teira accompanies them every once in a while.
- Terkou pushes Aeri to try and reach new top speeds, building a large circular track for them. Aeri manages to reach 1% the SoL, but Terkou consistently urges them to go further. 
- Is'ale presses Aeri after a team coordination training session, urging them to quit being so reckless. Aeri brushes it off and ignores him, saying he doesn't know anything about being as fast as they are, then claims it's impossible for them to be reckless. 
- Teira loses to Aeri in a training simulation. They laugh it off afterwards, but Terkou urges Aeri to stop holding back in training simulations, something they didn't even realize they were doing. 
- Aeri tries pushing themself further, but can't seem to break their top speed. Terkou keeps pushing them.
- Terkou gives Aeri two one-handed curved swords to compliment their speed and increase their effectiveness against other Forbidden Users. Aeri, as ordered, begins to train with them to prepare to use them in combat.
- Ordered by Terkou, Aeri and Teira practice illusions with Teira's dimensional alterations to later plan for potential dual-team attacks. 
### APRIL
- After long training, Aeri (and the others) are sent out on solo missions. Aeri is ordered to destroy multiple military forces in Africa. They begin in Cairo (Hyper City), where they destroy the army in mere moments. Riding the high, they speed across the northern coast looking for more.
- Aeri eventually reaches Casablanca (Hyper City), where they devastate another Human army. At this point, they seem to be somewhat enjoying themselves.
- Aeri reaches Lagos. At this point, less than 20 minutes has passed. Aeri devastates another human army, and indirectly causes an Elemental uprising in the city that swiftly succeeds and fights to spread further inland. Aeri begins to run for Cape City (Hyper City).
- Aeri meets Adva | Adva ambushes Aeri at Cape City, where they fight her while also dealing damage to the army attacking them both. While they lose this battle, they did achieve a good portion of their objective.
- When they return to Terkou, Terkou is impressed and praises them. Then, Aeri is the one of two given the green-light to move in with quick hit-and-run missions to dodge Adva as Terkou makes contingency plans for her.
- With this new directive, Aeri quickly hits militaries of states like Northern South East Asia, Korea's Empire, State of Japan, and TUNA (Terra Union North Atlantic Command), who gets hit particularly hard as Aeri deals massive damage to their naval force.
- Aeri returns to the Endsight base damn near a hero. Is'ale notably stays out of the praising, as he's busy focused on studying Terkou's plans. Zero doesn't do celebrations.
- Aeri is called off of their missions as Terkou thinks they could be better with their swords. So, Terkou trains them in swordsmanship.
