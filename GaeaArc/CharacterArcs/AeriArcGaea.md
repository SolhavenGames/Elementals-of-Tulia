# Aeri "Warsight" Glem | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_War always shines when I'm there! Get it? Cause.. Warshine..._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A young unit of Endsight under Terkou, being trained in combat with dual swords that connect at the handles.

**Ending State**: A feared and respected warrior & rival of Paradox (Dera). 

**Inciting Moments**: Slaughtering the military of an Anti-Elemental nation, as per orders.

**Irreversable Actions**: Killing the leader of a nation on public broadcast.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the most public figure of Endsight's task force, building further cross-species resentment.

**Ending Alignment**: Endsight (As Warsight). 
## Summary 

## Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Aeri spends the short antarctica nights staring up at the stars. Teira accompanies them every once in a while.
- Terkou pushes Aeri to try and reach new top speeds, building a large circular track for them. Aeri manages to reach 1% the SoL, but Terkou consistently urges them to go further. 
- Is'ale presses Aeri after a team coordination training session, urging them to quit being so reckless. Aeri brushes it off and ignores him, saying he doesn't know anything about being as fast as they are, then claims it's impossible for them to be reckless. 
- Teira loses to Aeri in a training simulation. They laugh it off afterwards, but Terkou urges Aeri to stop holding back in training simulations, something they didn't even realize they were doing. 
- Aeri tries pushing themself further, but can't seem to break their top speed. Terkou keeps pushing them.
- Terkou gives Aeri two one-handed curved swords to compliment their speed and increase their effectiveness against other Forbidden Users. Aeri, as ordered, begins to train with them to prepare to use them in combat.
- Ordered by Terkou, Aeri and Teira practice illusions with Teira's dimensional alterations to later plan for potential dual-team attacks. 
### APRIL
- After long training, Aeri (and the others) are sent out on solo missions. Aeri is ordered to destroy multiple military forces in Africa. They begin in Cairo (Hyper City), where they destroy the army in mere moments. Riding the high, they speed across the northern coast looking for more.
- Aeri eventually reaches Casablanca (Hyper City), where they devastate another Human army. At this point, they seem to be somewhat enjoying themselves.
- Aeri reaches Lagos. At this point, less than 20 minutes has passed. Aeri devastates another human army, and indirectly causes an Elemental uprising in the city that swiftly succeeds and fights to spread further inland. Aeri begins to run for Cape City (Hyper City).
- Aeri meets Adva | Adva ambushes Aeri at Cape City, where they fight her while also dealing damage to the army attacking them both. While they lose this battle, they did achieve a good portion of their objective.
- When they return to Terkou, Terkou is impressed and praises them. Then, Aeri is the one of two given the green-light to move in with quick hit-and-run missions to dodge Adva as Terkou makes contingency plans for her.
- With this new directive, Aeri quickly hits militaries of states like Northern South East Asia, Korea's Empire, State of Japan, and TUNA (Terra Union North Atlantic Command), who gets hit particularly hard as Aeri deals massive damage to their naval force.
- Aeri returns to the Endsight base damn near a hero. Is'ale notably stays out of the praising, as he's busy focused on studying Terkou's plans. Zero doesn't do celebrations.
- Aeri is called off of their missions as Terkou thinks they could be better with their swords. So, Terkou trains them in swordsmanship.
- Aeri keeps spending times looking at the stars, specifically when Tulia is visible in the sky. 
### MAY
- Terkou moves Aeri from open frontline offensives to a bit of espionage as their speed allows for them to get in and get out very quickly. With Zero, they often participate in quick acquisition missions to take capital for Endsight to continue to function.
- Aeri spends time with Teira after she asks. Teira admits that Terkou makes her feel a little weird. Aeri oddly hasn't formed an opinion on him yet. When Teira asks, they just claim to agree.
- Aeri trains with the rest of the team in the first team deployment training to prepare for future deployments. They can't seem to fully get behind Is'ale's field leadership. 
- Aeri pulls off a massive heist in Londina (Hyper City), securing massive amounts of gold bars that removes the need for future heists for about 3 months. Terkou again praises Aeri for this development.
- Aeri prays to their God for the first time in a while, asking if they're doing the right thing, and looking for directions. They get no answer.
- Warshine begins to bolster Aeri's pride further, causing them to be more reckless in training. Terkou praises it, but Is'ale tells them to stop. The conflicting order confuses them.
- Aeri doesn't respond to an invitation to join the Alaskan military, but they do consider how life would be different.
- Aeri finds Zero's collection of IDs, something only Teira had seen at this point. Zero doesn't get protective, and instead struggles to justify why he collects them. Aeri doesn't really seem to care, though. They just think it's odd... but a little cool. The two go over some of the ID's after Aeri picks one up to take a closer look.
- Aeri finally asks Teira why she abandoned them so suddenly on Tulia. Teira claims not to remember abandoning them, just ending up in space suddenly. Aeri doesn't know if they should believe her or not.
- Terkou tells the team to prepare for an upcoming joint Endsight attack, with all four of them, something they had never done yet, but had been training for.
### JUNE
- The day finally comes, and all four members lead an attack on Instantinople (Hyper City). Fighting through the SFP Peacekeeping Force, Knights of Gaea, and Union of Mankind military all at once, they sweep through them in mere minutes.
- However, when Adva arrives (who NONE of them have gotten the name for yet, they literally just call her "Bitch"), they all try to jump her. However, she claims an aspect of Squall: Aspect: Liberation, allowing her to, shockingly, keep up with Aeri's speedy strikes. Aeri is the second one out of the battle, but does the most damage after wounding her side with a quick, running strike.
- The team retreats after Adva stands strong and takes them out. When they return, Terkou is enraged, but not at them, instead at who he finally identifies to them: Adva. He declares her the most valuable target of Endsight, right behind someone they have yet to meet: Imbra. Aeri doesn't seem to be too worried, despite having just lost to one for second time with a numerical advantage.
- Aeri begins to train harder in preparation for more battles with other Forbidden Users, pushing even harder to push past their top speed. They finally do, and they reach Warshine Gate IV. They peak at 19.4% the SoL.
- Aeri is put to focus on quelling the suddenly growing Knights of Gaea by attacking logistics and striking their largest known hubs/bases. Notably, other Forbidden Users don't try to stop them...
- Aeri meets Samae | Spoke too soon. The human Forbidden User, which was a myth, pops up during one of their objectives. In a very high difficulty fight, Aeri eventually routes Samae through use of their superior speed.
- In another battle, Samae hardly trips Aeri up, forcing them to retreat as more KoG reinforcements arrive. Aeri returns heavily wounded... and it doesn't help that they weren't fully recovered from the last battle.
- Terkou puts off Aeri's operations for now, allowing them to recover.
- Teira and Zero visit Aeri sparsely as they recover. Is'ale only visits once, but when he visits, he claims it's not out of love, and more for his own conscious.
