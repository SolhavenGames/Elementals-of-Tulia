# Kael "Morrow" Voris | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_"Saddened by my own power; I miss my element."_** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A prisoner of Troy, to be used as bait.

**Ending State**: Morally gray, globally renowned symbol of mystery and fear.

**Inciting Moments**: Emancipation, allowing him to operate as a normal creature again as he chooses to join The Symbols.

**Irreversable Actions**: Killing both Humans and Elementals as a show of strength for The Symbols (he chose to do this himself.)

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being a symbol of fear for The Symbols.

**Ending Alignment**: The Symbols (as Morrow).
