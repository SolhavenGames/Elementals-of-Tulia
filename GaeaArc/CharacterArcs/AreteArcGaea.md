# Arete "Sentinel" Ishel | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_I serve the law, even if the law doesn't serve me._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Living in the Amazon Rainforest alongside Rai, Adva, and Imbra.

**Ending State**: Grouped with Zezri, Zrili, and K'zorak, aiming to enforce peace and lawfulness by protecting citizens and popularizing coexistence. 

**Inciting Moments**: Joining the Peace & Cooperation Guild after Kterla called for her.

**Irreversable Actions**: Letting a war criminal get away to save civilians.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being a major grounding point for Imbra and Adva.

**Ending Alignment**: Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC) (as Sentinel). 
## Summary 

## Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Arete has been struggling to cope with her inaction. She wants to help people, but the urge feels different. Usually, her urge to help is entirely positive. Now, after getting Bastion, it's always felt derogatory, belittling herself for not being on the field. She worries that if she *does* help at some point, it'll change her drastically.
- Arete witnesses Kael corrupted by death. That internal voice screams at her that much louder for being unable to help him.
- Arete goes on walks frequently, trying to silence the voice. She doesn't want to tell anyone about it, so when it gets real bad, she just leaves their home for a while.
- With the voice only getting louder, Arete tries to silence it by spending more time near Kael. She still can't really help him, but the voice quiets when she takes care of him.
- The voice also pushes her to be more controlling of Imbra, Adva, and Rai, pushing them to be safer in their sparse sparring encounters. 
### APRIL
- Her controlling nature pushes her into an argument with Adva, which pushes Adva to leave soon after. Arete spends the entire time worrying.
- Arete spends time with Kael and Imbra, as Imbra speaks of worries of caving to the voices of Hellfire and Kael keeps pushing through the gates of Death.
- Rai expresses concern when Arete tells him not to help people. "That's... unlike you," He says. Arete has no good response, but Rai stays anyway.
- When Adva returns after being gone for 26 hours, Arete and Adva get into an argument once again, where Arete attempts to push her to be safer. The argument has to be broken up by Rai.
- Adva still goes out despite Arete's wishes, stressing her out further. She nearly has a panic attack just thinking about it, but Rai and Imbra calm her down.
### MAY
- Rai pushes Arete and Adva to reconcile, pointing out Imbra's growing internal conflicts. After he begs for them to build a better environment, Arete and Adva come to an agreement. The two apologize to one another for their own grievances. 
- Arete reluctantly agrees to let Adva go out and fight, though she still despises it and worries for her safety.
- Arete begins sleeping to try and ignore the voice, but it appears in her dreams now, as she's forced to watch helpless people dying.
- Arete coerces Adva to rest, finally.
- She has discovered that helping small animals in the wild helps silence the voice. So, she begins doing that.
- Arete learns more about Bastion through Rai and his studies...
### JUNE
- Arete begins trying to self-diagnos why the urge to help feels so different. After a few days, she can only confidently conclude that it's something to do with Bastion. 
- She decides she'll keep helping small animals to silence the urge instead of giving into it. 
- When Arete finds out Adva went out to contest a full-force Endsight, she's horrified. She's even more horrified when she finds out that both Rai and Imbra trust her to quote "kick some ass". Arete has a panic attack due to this.
- When Adva returns injured (but alive) proclaiming victory, Arete resist the urge to blow up and beats around the bush, trying to urge her to be more careful without triggering an argument. 
- Adva continues to go out and risk her life. Arete's internal urge pushes and asks why she isn't doing the same. 
- Arete has multiple sleepless nights due to this urge. 
### JULY
- Arete and Rai have been drifting apart noticeably. Rai reaches out to try and fix that, and the two spends a few days together.
- When Kael reaches the final gate of Death before he can be emancipated, Arete joins the other three in investing most of their energy in attempting to emancipate him. 
- Arete silently blames herself for not emancipating Kael before it got to this point. 
- Through the joint focus on Kael, Arete is able to rebuild friendlier relationships with Adva, Imbra, and Rai. 
### AUGUST
- Kael's emancipation is such a huge event for the group that the urge to help people is actually silenced for a few days and Arete enjoys herself for the first time in a long time.
- ... it returns shortly after.
- When the rest of the group begins sparring for fun again, Arete declines to join. She worries that the urge will grow stronger during it, though it's also one of the things telling her not to. Though, she does watch sometimes, staying close for medical needs.
- Arete is admittedly impressed by Kael's combat prowess.
- Arete scares an animal away after trying to help it. She later finds it dead. The urge ridicules her for it, pushing her to self-isolate.
###  SEPTEMBER
- Arete, still isolating herself somewhat, urges Adva, Imbra, and Kael after they took a risk against the Nile Corporation.
- When the trio goes again and returns, she blows up, causing them to leave the Amazon Rainforest. Rai remains with Arete, claiming to be worried about her.
- Despite that, the two continue to grow apart as Rai grows increasingly interested with the global politics and Arete continues to try to isolate herself to silence her urges.
- They do spend some time together, though, but it's very short, and Arete is often not too focused on the task at hand.
- Arete grows increasingly worried as it becomes more obvious that Rai is gearing up to leave the Amazon, too.
- The day finally comes. Rai leaves the Amazon, apologizing to Arete, saying that the Queen in Frei-Se inspired him to lead the Watrish back to their culture and into a better, more peaceful life. Arete is now entirely alone.
- Suddenly, she gets a visit...
- Arete meets Kterla | In the Amazon, Kterla comes in person to visit Arete after hearing about her from Adva, Imbra, Kael, and Rai. Kterla actually knows Arete from Tulia, from her family's charity organization (The Ishel Foundation). For the first time since the urge appeared, Arete is telling someone about it. Kterla promises to keep Arete in check, and Arete agrees to join the Peace & Cooperation Guild, moving to Lima, Peru.
- Arete meets K'zorak | In the very first PAC debriefing (centered around meeting the rest of the team), Arete meets K'zorak first. They hit it off really easily, as K'zorak also knows Arete, and Arete actually knows K'zorak as she read a few of his news reports on her visits to Ralia (Ballad Nation; his nation, though K'zorak doesn't remember that it was his nation...)
- Arete meets Zezri | Arete and Zezri also hit it off. Although Zezri doesn't know Arete due to her more secluded life in Dalino (Darrekin Nation), she admires what Arete used to do on Tulia. Arete is actually kind of afraid of Zezri's power, and Zezri reassures her, telling Arete that she's afraid of it, too. They have a good laugh over it.
- Arete meets Zrili | Right after meeting Zezri, Zrili approaches Arete. Zrili has heard of the Ishel Foundation, but not Arete herself. No matter, Arete didn't do it for the fame. The two chat for a little bit and Arete learns a lot about Zrili's desires for a quiet life, and they even call their power a cruel joke. The two laugh about it together.
- Arete meets Dera | Another person who knows who Arete is, this time someone who's directly benefited from it (not the first person she's met like this; Rai benefited from her charity, too). Dera and Arete share stories about his hometown Set (the name of the city is Set) in Tix (the Poisi Nation, now Tixolam). Afterwards, they talk about how the new Knightsdale government has destroyed the nation.
- Arete meets Wykoi | After meeting the entire team, Wykoi reveals himself to them. He doesn't take time to meet them all individually just yet, but reassures them that their mission is pure and good, also reminding them what they're fighting for: Coexistence and peace on Gaea.
- Finally, the actual debriefing can begin. Multiple requests are thrown around, mainly for evacuations, repairs, or aid deliveries. Arete is sent on a mission to deliver aid through the Alpine Mountains, from Germania to the Mediterranean Command. She does it in only a few hours, liberating multiple cities from hunger.
- Immediately after, Kterla sends her to do repairs in North-Eastern Gaul, a region rocked by Londina Elemental warfare.
- Arete returns, being given some praise by humans for the assistance she gave. Yet somehow, the urge to help people has only grown stronger. Louder. More demanding. More demeaning.
- Without orders, Arete goes out to Sao Paulo to commence repairs there. Kterla warns her not to go without orders, as they only go where they are sanctioned. Arete apologizes, but the urge pushes her to keep doing what she can.
- Arete goes sleepless, the insomnia only worsening. She can't sleep for days, and doesn't leave her room.
- The third day of her self isolation, Zrili comes in. They claim they can hear Arete's distress, and push Arete to try to spend more time with the team to try and ignore it.
- Arete tries that, attending some team building activities, and it does kind of silence the urge... but only for a little.
- Arete begs Kterla for more missions. Kterla gives her a long list of reconstruction requests, counting 55 cities across all continents (barring Antarctica). Arete doesn't return for 8 days, going sleepless for all of them (Forbidden Users can operate longer without sleep, for about 300 hours. She's been awake for just about that, so she's visibly tired).
- Kterla and Wykoi push Arete to sleep, and she finally does, collapsing from sheer exhaustion.
- As soon as she wakes up, she finds out that PAC is being deployed to Busan to stop a looming Endsight attack. Kterla reminds the team that despite there only being three visible, the task force is known to have four members. Dera does not go, claiming a non-combatant role. 
- Arete meets Is'ale | The first person she clashes with. Arete immediately pushes Is'ale back, overwhelming him with sheer firepower.
- Arete meets Aeri | Well... hardly. Aeri came in to support Is'ale. Arete hardly saw them, only a flash of light and a sudden pain in her throat and chest.
- Arete meets Zero | While down and injured, Zero failed to attack her, getting pushed back. Zezri came to support the weakened and vulnerable Arete.
- Arete meets Teira | ... and Zezri brought her enemy. Now alongside Zezri, Arete tries to fight the teleporting dimension wielder back.
- The battle of Busan continues on. Arete, being harshly injured, struggles to keep stand her ground, and ends up overcompensating for her injury, getting in the way of her teammates while only injuring herself further. PAC is forced to retreat, and Endsight achieves their goals.
- The team returns to Lima demoralized. Arete is given medicine and bandages to heal from the cuts and poisons. Kterla tells her to sit out for a while so she can properly heal. Wykoi tries to remoralize the team, telling them there's always tomorrow. Arete utters, "Not for the citizens who died".
