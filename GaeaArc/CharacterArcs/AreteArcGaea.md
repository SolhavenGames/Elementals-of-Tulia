# Arete "Sentinel" Ishel | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_I serve the law, even if the law doesn't serve me._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Living in the Amazon Rainforest alongside Rai, Adva, and Imbra.

**Ending State**: Grouped with Zezri, Zrili, and K'zorak, aiming to enforce peace and lawfulness by protecting citizens and popularizing coexistence. 

**Inciting Moments**: Joining the Peace & Cooperation Guild after Kterla called for her.

**Irreversable Actions**: Letting a war criminal get away to save civilians.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being a major grounding point for Imbra and Adva.

**Ending Alignment**: Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC) (as Sentinel). 
## Summary 

## Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Arete has been struggling to cope with her inaction. She wants to help people, but the urge feels different. Usually, her urge to help is entirely positive. Now, after getting Bastion, it's always felt derogatory, belittling herself for not being on the field. She worries that if she *does* help at some point, it'll change her drastically.
- Arete witnesses Kael corrupted by death. That internal voice screams at her that much louder for being unable to help him.
- Arete goes on walks frequently, trying to silence the voice. She doesn't want to tell anyone about it, so when it gets real bad, she just leaves their home for a while.
- With the voice only getting louder, Arete tries to silence it by spending more time near Kael. She still can't really help him, but the voice quiets when she takes care of him.
- The voice also pushes her to be more controlling of Imbra, Adva, and Rai, pushing them to be safer in their sparse sparring encounters. 
### APRIL
- Her controlling nature pushes her into an argument with Adva, which pushes Adva to leave soon after. Arete spends the entire time worrying.
- Arete spends time with Kael and Imbra, as Imbra speaks of worries of caving to the voices of Hellfire and Kael keeps pushing through the gates of Death.
- Rai expresses concern when Arete tells him not to help people. "That's... unlike you," He says. Arete has no good response, but Rai stays anyway.
- When Adva returns after being gone for 26 hours, Arete and Adva get into an argument once again, where Arete attempts to push her to be safer. The argument has to be broken up by Rai.
- Adva still goes out despite Arete's wishes, stressing her out further. She nearly has a panic attack just thinking about it, but Rai and Imbra calm her down.
### MAY
- Rai pushes Arete and Adva to reconcile, pointing out Imbra's growing internal conflicts. After he begs for them to build a better environment, Arete and Adva come to an agreement. The two apologize to one another for their own grievances. 
- Arete reluctantly agrees to let Adva go out and fight, though she still despises it and worries for her safety.
- Arete begins sleeping to try and ignore the voice, but it appears in her dreams now, as she's forced to watch helpless people dying.
- Arete coerces Adva to rest, finally.
- She has discovered that helping small animals in the wild helps silence the voice. So, she begins doing that.
- Arete learns more about Bastion through Rai and his studies...
### JUNE
- Arete begins trying to self-diagnos why the urge to help feels so different. After a few days, she can only confidently conclude that it's something to do with Bastion. 
- She decides she'll keep helping small animals to silence the urge instead of giving into it. 
- When Arete finds out Adva went out to contest a full-force Endsight, she's horrified. She's even more horrified when she finds out that both Rai and Imbra trust her to quote "kick some ass". Arete has a panic attack due to this.
- When Adva returns injured (but alive) proclaiming victory, Arete resist the urge to blow up and beats around the bush, trying to urge her to be more careful.
