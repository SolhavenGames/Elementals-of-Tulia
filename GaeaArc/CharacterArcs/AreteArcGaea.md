# Arete "Sentinel" Ishel | Gaea Arc Journey
<sub> **_I serve the law, even if the law doesn't serve me._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Living in the Amazon Rainforest alongside Rai, Adva, and Imbra.

**Ending State**: Grouped with Jhu, Nixis, and E'rix, aiming to enforce peace and lawfulness by protecting citizens and popularizing coexistence. 

**Inciting Moments**: Joining the Peace & Cooperation Guild after Kterla called for her.

**Irreversable Actions**: Letting a war criminal get away to save civilians.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being a major grounding point for Imbra and Adva.

**Ending Alignment**: Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC) (as Sentinel). 
## Summary 

## Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Arete has been struggling to cope with her inaction. She wants to help people, but the urge feels different. Usually, her urge to help is entirely positive. Now, after getting Bastion, it's always felt derogatory, belittling herself for not being on the field. She worries that if she *does* help at some point, it'll change her drastically.
- Arete witnesses Kael corrupted by death. That internal voice screams at her that much louder for being unable to help him.
- Arete goes on walks frequently, trying to silence the voice. She doesn't want to tell anyone about it, so when it gets real bad, she just leaves their home for a while.
- With the voice only getting louder, Arete tries to silence it by spending more time near Kael. She still can't really help him, but the voice quiets when she takes care of him.
- The voice also pushes her to be more controlling of Imbra, Adva, and Rai, pushing them to be safer in their sparse sparring encounters. 
### APRIL
- Her controlling nature pushes her into an argument with Adva, which pushes Adva to leave soon after. Arete spends the entire time worrying.
- Arete spends time with Kael and Imbra, as Imbra speaks of worries of caving to the voices of Hellfire and Kael keeps pushing through the gates of Death.
- Rai expresses concern when Arete tells him not to help people. "That's... unlike you," He says. Arete has no good response, but Rai stays anyway.
- When Adva returns after being gone for 26 hours, Arete and Adva get into an argument once again, where Arete attempts to push her to be safer. The argument has to be broken up by Rai.
- Adva still goes out despite Arete's wishes, stressing her out further. She nearly has a panic attack just thinking about it, but Rai and Imbra calm her down.
### MAY
- Rai pushes Arete and Adva to reconcile, pointing out Imbra's growing internal conflicts. After he begs for them to build a better environment, Arete and Adva come to an agreement. The two apologize to one another for their own grievances. 
- Arete reluctantly agrees to let Adva go out and fight, though she still despises it and worries for her safety.
- Arete begins sleeping to try and ignore the voice, but it appears in her dreams now, as she's forced to watch helpless people dying.
- Arete coerces Adva to rest, finally.
- She has discovered that helping small animals in the wild helps silence the voice. So, she begins doing that.
- Arete learns more about Bastion through Rai and his studies...
### JUNE
- Arete begins trying to self-diagnos why the urge to help feels so different. After a few days, she can only confidently conclude that it's something to do with Bastion. 
- She decides she'll keep helping small animals to silence the urge instead of giving into it. 
- When Arete finds out Adva went out to contest a full-force Endsight, she's horrified. She's even more horrified when she finds out that both Rai and Imbra trust her to quote "kick some ass". Arete has a panic attack due to this.
- When Adva returns injured (but alive) proclaiming victory, Arete resist the urge to blow up and beats around the bush, trying to urge her to be more careful without triggering an argument. 
- Adva continues to go out and risk her life. Arete's internal urge pushes and asks why she isn't doing the same. 
- Arete has multiple sleepless nights due to this urge. 
### JULY
- Arete and Rai have been drifting apart noticeably. Rai reaches out to try and fix that, and the two spends a few days together.
- When Kael reaches the final gate of Death before he can be emancipated, Arete joins the other three in investing most of their energy in attempting to emancipate him. 
- Arete silently blames herself for not emancipating Kael before it got to this point. 
- Through the joint focus on Kael, Arete is able to rebuild friendlier relationships with Adva, Imbra, and Rai. 
### AUGUST
- Kael's emancipation is such a huge event for the group that the urge to help people is actually silenced for a few days and Arete enjoys herself for the first time in a long time.
- ... it returns shortly after.
- When the rest of the group begins sparring for fun again, Arete declines to join. She worries that the urge will grow stronger during it, though it's also one of the things telling her not to. Though, she does watch sometimes, staying close for medical needs.
- Arete is admittedly impressed by Kael's combat prowess.
- Arete scares an animal away after trying to help it. She later finds it dead. The urge ridicules her for it, pushing her to self-isolate.
###  SEPTEMBER
- Arete, still isolating herself somewhat, urges Adva, Imbra, and Kael after they took a risk against the Nile Corporation.
- When the trio goes again and returns, she blows up, causing them to leave the Amazon Rainforest. Rai remains with Arete, claiming to be worried about her.
- Despite that, the two continue to grow apart as Rai grows increasingly interested with the global politics and Arete continues to try to isolate herself to silence her urges.
- They do spend some time together, though, but it's very short, and Arete is often not too focused on the task at hand.
- Arete grows increasingly worried as it becomes more obvious that Rai is gearing up to leave the Amazon, too.
- The day finally comes. Rai leaves the Amazon, apologizing to Arete, saying that the Queen in Frei-Se inspired him to lead the Watrish back to their culture and into a better, more peaceful life. Arete is now entirely alone.
- Suddenly, she gets a visit...
- Arete meets Kterla | In the Amazon, Kterla comes in person to visit Arete after hearing about her from Adva, Imbra, Kael, and Rai. Kterla actually knows Arete from Tulia, from her family's charity organization (The Ishel Foundation). For the first time since the urge appeared, Arete is telling someone about it. Kterla promises to keep Arete in check, and Arete agrees to join the Peace & Cooperation Guild, moving to Lima, Peru.
- Arete meets E'rix | In the very first PAC debriefing (centered around meeting the rest of the team), Arete meets E'rix first. They hit it off really easily, as E'rix also knows Arete, and Arete actually knows E'rix as she read a few of his news reports on her visits to Ralia (Ballad Nation; his nation, though E'rix doesn't remember that it was his nation...)
- Arete meets Jhu | Arete and Jhu also hit it off. Although Jhu doesn't know Arete due to her more secluded life in Dalino (Darrekin Nation), she admires what Arete used to do on Tulia. Arete is actually kind of afraid of Jhu's power, and Jhu reassures her, telling Arete that she's afraid of it, too. They have a good laugh over it.
- Arete meets Nixis | Right after meeting Jhu, Nixis approaches Arete. Nixis has heard of the Ishel Foundation, but not Arete herself. No matter, Arete didn't do it for the fame. The two chat for a little bit and Arete learns a lot about Nixis's desires for a quiet life, and they even call their power a cruel joke. The two laugh about it together.
- Arete meets Dera | Another person who knows who Arete is, this time someone who's directly benefited from it (not the first person she's met like this; Rai benefited from her charity, too). Dera and Arete share stories about his hometown Set (the name of the city is Set) in Tix (the Poisi Nation, now Tixolam). Afterwards, they talk about how the new Knightsdale government has destroyed the nation.
- Arete meets Wykoi | After meeting the entire team, Wykoi reveals himself to them. He doesn't take time to meet them all individually just yet, but reassures them that their mission is pure and good, also reminding them what they're fighting for: Coexistence and peace on Gaea.
- Finally, the actual debriefing can begin. Multiple requests are thrown around, mainly for evacuations, repairs, or aid deliveries. Arete is sent on a mission to deliver aid through the Alpine Mountains, from Germania to the Mediterranean Command. She does it in only a few hours, liberating multiple cities from hunger.
- Immediately after, Kterla sends her to do repairs in North-Eastern Gaul, a region rocked by Londina Elemental warfare.
- Arete returns, being given some praise by humans for the assistance she gave. Yet somehow, the urge to help people has only grown stronger. Louder. More demanding. More demeaning.
- Without orders, Arete goes out to Sao Paulo to commence repairs there. Kterla warns her not to go without orders, as they only go where they are sanctioned. Arete apologizes, but the urge pushes her to keep doing what she can.
- Arete goes sleepless, the insomnia only worsening. She can't sleep for days, and doesn't leave her room.
- The third day of her self isolation, Nixis comes in. They claim they can hear Arete's distress, and push Arete to try to spend more time with the team to try and ignore it.
- Arete tries that, attending some team building activities, and it does kind of silence the urge... but only for a little.
- Arete begs Wykoi for more missions. Wykoi gives her a long list of reconstruction requests, counting 55 cities across all continents (barring Antarctica). Arete doesn't return for 8 days, going sleepless for all of them (Forbidden Users can operate longer without sleep, for about 300 hours. She's been awake for just about that, so she's visibly tired).
- Kterla and Wykoi push Arete to sleep, and she finally does, collapsing from sheer exhaustion.
- As soon as she wakes up, she finds out that PAC is being deployed to Busan to stop a looming Endsight attack. Kterla reminds the team that despite there only being three visible, the task force is known to have four members. Dera does not go, claiming a non-combatant role. 
- Arete meets Is'ale | The first person she clashes with. Arete immediately pushes Rootsight (Is'ale) back, overwhelming him with sheer firepower.
- Arete meets Aeri | Well... hardly. Warsight (Aeri) came in to support Rootsight. Arete hardly saw them, only a flash of light and a sudden pain in her throat and chest.
- Arete meets Zero | While down and injured, Gravesight (Zero) failed to attack her, getting pushed back. Jhu came to support the weakened and vulnerable Arete.
- Arete meets Teira | ... and Jhu brought her enemy. Now alongside Jhu, Arete tries to fight the teleporting dimension wielder Riftsight (Teira) back.
- The battle of Busan continues on. Arete, being harshly injured, struggles to keep stand her ground, and ends up overcompensating for her injury, getting in the way of her teammates while only injuring herself further. PAC is forced to retreat, and Endsight achieves their goals.
- The team returns to Lima demoralized. Arete is given medicine and bandages to heal from the cuts and poisons. Kterla tells her to sit out for a while so she can properly heal. Wykoi tries to remoralize the team, telling them there's always tomorrow. Arete utters, "Not for the citizens who died".  
### OCTOBER
- Arete hears that Rai has migrated the Watrish to Frei-Se. She honestly couldn't be prouder of him... she realizes how much she misses him.
- She considers visiting Frei-Se to see Rai, but decides against it after she remembers she can't deploy anywhere without approval.
- Arete goes on sanctioned reconstruction missions with Dera for a few days. They become slightly renowned for their work.
- Arete continues to struggle with intense insomnia and goes sleepless for weeks on end. She tries to distract herself with work.
- Nations have become so accustomed to Arete's work that they greenlight laws known as the Sentinel Programs, allowing Arete to show up unsanctioned to do reconstruction. Though Kterla tried to resist it, Arete and Wykoi advocated for it heavily. The Sentinel Programs is what solidifies her field name as Sentinel, though many news stations had long been calling her that.
- Arete runs reconstruction programs in The Nile Corporation and India alongside some light humanitarian aid. She's gone for multiple days.
- Kterla and Jhu speak of worries about Arete's health. She swears she's fine... but they don't believe her.
- After hearing that Frei-Se is harboring Adva, Imbra, and Kael (who have been recognized as terrorists internationally), Arete is silently happy that they're safe. Kterla reassures her that the SFP nor PAC will be attempting to capture them despite this new information.
- Arete's exhaustion begins causing her to blunder, making some of her work sub-optimal and leading to injuries after the fact.
- Kterla forces Arete to take a break. The team visits her often to keep her company.
- After her break is over, she is deployed to Havana, where she actually fights Imbra, Adva, and Kael for the first time alongside the rest of her team.
- PAC loses the battle, to no one's surprise. The deployment was more of a publicity stunt.
- After the battle, Arete battles Adva, with neither of them holding back. Despite this, they continue to reassure each other that they still care for each other, and Arete even takes the opportunity to ask about Rai.
- When Imbra attacks Pertha, Arete is on the ground with the rest of PAC (still minus Dera). She witnesses first hand an unrelenting Hellfire.
- The aftermath changes her perspective of Imbra. Despite knowing that it was all against her will, she still carries the end of the world within her body. Arete begins to question if Imbra should remain free.
- Arete and E'rix together clash twice with Warsight and Rootsight in The Levant. While losing the first encounter, they use more defensive measures to win the second one, forcing the duo to retreat. 
### NOVEMBER
- The Moon Disaster. PAC scrambles to look for solutions as the Moon slowly falls towards Gaea.
- Dera comes up with an idea- deliver Arete's val to overpower whatever force is pulling it, then holding the Moon in position until they can find a permanent solution. Arete goes along with this plan.
- She waits on Gaea for Dera to deliver her val to The Moon. He eventually does, though he comes back somewhat wounded and frustrated, claiming he had an encounter with Warsight. Due to this, her Val wasn't spread well enough to push the moon back, but it was enough to hold it in place.
- Suddenly, the connection was severed. Arete is able to push the moon back to where it belongs, and The Moon Disaster ends.
- Dera defects from PAC suddenly, going to join The Renegades. E'rix uses this to push his ideology of anti-stateism and PAC independence. Nixis seems to be beginning to agree with him.
- After PAC and SFP discovers that the extinctionist cult known as "The Whispers" was responsible for The Moon Disaster, Arete leads the initiative into re-focusing PAC to take them down before they cause more harm. Kterla initially disagrees, citing team morale after Dera's recent defection and Arete's diminishing health, but gives in after Arete persists.
- The team begins leading investigations globally alongside SFP. Arete herself oversees incursions into isolated mountain cities like Thimphu and Cordova, where she actually makes some successes in finding names of local leaders tied to The Whispers. She even manages to plant a few informants in the regions! 
- Using information that Nixis and Arete have discovered, PAC successfully concludes that The Whispers have a minor stronghold outside of Kunming in China. When they go to assault it, they nearly capture a war criminal, but Arete leads the decision to intercept forces sent to terrorize and raze the city instead, saving civilians. The Whispers member gets away.
- Nixis and Arete argue about what Nixis is calling 'The Stronghold Blunder'. Arete insists that civilians come first, no matter who they're after. Nixis argues that the whispers leader will do more harm than they prevented. Jhu and E'rix notably don't engage in this conversation.
- The next day, Jhu tries to reconcile relations between the two, which fails as both Arete and Nixis seem to have let the disagreement grow to something bitter. When Kterla and Wykoi get involved, the two shake hands... but it's clear that the conflict is not yet over.
- Arete continues her missions to subdue The Whispers while also delivering humanitarian aid to starved cities. In these missions, she also attempts to help calm protests, as the world has seemed to explode in recent weeks.
- PAC is deployed to Nagpur after reports of suspicious movements outside of the city. The team arrives to find The Whispers, led by the same man they let get away in Kunming. This time, they sweep the forces easily and grab hold of him.
- However, he was nothing more than a distraction. A massive explosion goes off in the city of Nagpur, leaving the entire team stunned. Buildings collapse. The shockwave nearly knocks them over. Ash and debris flies into the sky like they've never seen before. Arete immediately orders the entire team into the city, letting him escape once again.
- The team focuses on trying to minimize destruction and death. Arete focuses on halting the collapsing buildings and liberating civilians, though most of them near the blast have been turned to nothing more than a shadow.
- Adva and Dera eventually arrive on-scene, attempting to assist in the disaster.
- A val storm suddenly appears- an Ash Blizzard. The team can hardly see or breathe, and citizens are getting cut by the high-speed ash particles in the air.
- Another bomb goes off. Arete begins trying to stretch herself thin, but E'rix *demands* she focuses on what she's already doing, reassuring her that he's got her back. Nixis and Jhu are injured by the explosion.
- Another bomb goes off, but Adva manages to contain it. Adva then manages to push most of the Val in the air out of the city, ending the storm.
- Once it was confirmed that there were no more bombs, the team remained to commence reconstruction and humanitarian aid, saving civilians trapped inside buildings or under rubble. Emergency services came soon after.
- Once they finished, Adva and Dera left. Soon after they did, the Government of India delivered an official, silent thanks to them for saving Nagpur.
- Upon returning to Lima, another argument, this time louder and more intense, broke out between Nixis and Arete. The same man they let escape caused this attack, and they let him go again. Arete continues to defend her actions, claiming that defending civilians will always be her utmost priority. Nixis insults her, saying "Civilians die when your incompetence lets their murderer free". Nixis is put on temporary leave for the comment, but they don't seem to care.
- The team's morale is almost entirely fractured. In fact, only Arete goes out on missions afterwards, namely reconstruction and humanitarian aid, as usual. Though, she does sprinkle in a bit of hunting for The Whispers. She also sprinkles in some diplomacy missions, as many same-species wars have recently begun. 
### DECEMBER
- Arete attends Rai and Spara's wedding, deciding what she needs is to cool down. There, she speaks with Adva and Imbra (choosing to hide her new outlook on Imbra instead of pressing it) peacefully, talking about life.
- After the ceremony, she speaks with Rai. She nearly cries seeing him become a king. She tells him she is so proud of him, and reassures him that his parents would be, too.
- Suddenly, Frei-Se is the victim of an aerial attack. Arete quickly mobilizes with the rest of the Forbidden Users present and the Frei-Se military. She mainly protects civilians and infrastructure from the incoming damage, being unable to reach the attackers herself.
- Arete meets Spara | Despite wanting to meet her *before* her nation was brutally attacked, Arete meets her after, being given an award for defending the people of Frei-Se. She accepts it, but reassures that she does not condone any future Frei-Se actions in response. She then leaves.
- None of PAC knew Arete went to Frei-Se for the wedding. She manages to keep it a secret from the rest of her team.
- Nixis returns suddenly after sneaking out, holding the man from Kunming and Nagpur in their hands. They throw him on the ground like trash, before giving an annoyed look and returning to their room without a word. Nobody knows how long they were gone. The man is turned over to authorities in India. Nixis is finally allowed back to sanctioned work after this feat proves their worth. 
- Arete meets Samae | The two meet in Berlin after Arete arrives to help quell protests. Though Samae doesn't fight Arete (due to Arete's status, it would look very bad for the KoG), Samae does reassure her that they are enemies, and urges her to forward the same message to PAC.
- Arete is forced to take time off after Kterla realized she hadn't slept in a while. 
- During this rest, Arete simply tries to track down The Whispers, forwarding her progress to both the SFP and Frei-Se.
- Arete sneaks out to assist an Elemental Nation in securing its borders from hateful, rage-filled humans. She returns after the mission quickly. Her actions allowed the nation of Strono Lorik to form.
