## Structure 
**Starting State**: Living in the Amazon Rainforest alongside Arete, Imbra, and Adva, continuing to study Forbidden Elements and even participating in combat training with Imbra and Adva.

**Ending State**: A king of a young nation, the husband to a powerful Queen, and hellbent on avenging his fallen soldiers.

**Inciting Moments**: The Frei-Se Invasion, where he fought and still lost some of his citizens to the bombing.

**Irreversable Actions**: Choosing to murder pilots instead of giving them due process as his wife commanded.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being king of Frei-Se, alongside Spara.

**Ending Alignment**: King of Frei-Se.
