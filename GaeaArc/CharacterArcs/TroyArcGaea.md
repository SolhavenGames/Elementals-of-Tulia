## Structure 
**Starting State**: The leader of the Elemental Research Initiative, slowly gaining more and more power.

**Ending State**: A man with near infinite power over the global government, creating loyalists and disgruntled followers as Humans inch closer to civil war as many distrust him.

**Inciting Moments**: Elemental Factions taking super cities causing him to consider more extreme solutions.

**Irreversable Actions**: Initiating a massive power grab of the ADS to secure more power for himself.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Pushing human resistance tech to its limits.

**Ending Alignment**: Association of Democratic States (as Sir Aegis).
