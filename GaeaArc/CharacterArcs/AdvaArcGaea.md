# Adva "Zephyr" Anil | Gaea Saga Journey
<sub> **_I do what I know is right, without hesitation._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Training for combat alongside Imbra, living with Rai, Arete, and Imbra in the Amazon Rainforest, isolated from the world and silently regretting her past actions.

**Ending State**: A popular symbol of graceful strength for Elementals across the planet.

**Inciting Moments**: Choosing to save Humans, despite them attacking her.

**Irreversable Actions**: Choosing more dangerous but more efficient options, causing loss of life.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: The first person to use a Forbidden Element's aspect ever in her 1v3 against Endsight's Task Force.

**Ending Alignment**: The Renegades (As Zephyr).

# Timeline
## ⦿ **YEAR 2039**
### MARCH
- Adva and Imbra have been training with one another for months, optimizing the use of both of their Forbidden Elements. Despite it being the reason her Windsi died a second time, Adva slowly grows to love her Forbidden Element.
- Adva witnesses a Kael corrupted by Death after Imbra saves him from Berlin. Seeing her friend broken like this, she stays in the forest for a while with him. Though she doesn't realize it now, she's partially responsible for emancipating Kael.
- Since Imbra is not feeling the best as her romantic interest is sitting before her infected with something clearly terminal, she often trains with Rai instead, and the two become friends. Adva wins more often than not.
- Adva is growing sick of Arete's controlling nature, but has yet to speak up against it.
### APRIL
- Adva and Arete get into an argument, causing Adva to leave for a few hours, though she doesn't go too far. While away, she resents not being able to kill Terkou.
- Adva continues to train with Imbra to get her mind off of Kael. She's beginning to worry that she may not be able to soften the blow if Kael actually, genuinely dies.
- Imbra stops training as much as she begins to worry about Kael's potential death causing her to succumb to the voices of Hellfire. She tells Adva to kill her if she ever needs to, something Adva promises to do, but she knows she wouldn't be able to if required.
- With the home front not doing so well, Adva begins to fight publicly again. She reappears in Mexico City to combat an invader...
- Adva meets Is'ale | Adva and Is'ale clash in Mexico City, where Adva finds her signature hat: A cybernetic, metal fitting black cap with cat-ears! She takes it off of a civilian she saves from being crushed under rubble. Adva wins this battle, then immediately goes elsewhere...
- Adva meets Aeri | After chasing Aeri, being just to late to catch them in Cairo, Casablanca, or Lagos, Adva ambushes Aeri in Cape City before they can do reasonable harm to the armed forces there. Adva notably has to fight Aeri while resisting the attacks from the same armed forces she's protecting. She wins this battle, too. 
- Adva meets Teira | While only temporarily, Adva, running off of her streak, immediately goes to meet Teira in Port Terra (Singapore). Though Teira has long heard of Adva's actions, and instead of fighting her, simply taunts her before fleeing the battle entirely.
- In between those three battles, she'd sometimes stop at open Elemental rebellions to save civilians from looming harm and danger. 
- After about 26 hours straight of flight and conflict, Adva returns to the Amazon. She remains relatively unexhausted due to the improvements that Divine Val gives her biological body. She immediately argues with Arete upon her return, who is upset that she got herself into risky and dangerous situations. Rai breaks them up.
- Adva, despite Arete's (and sometimes, Imbra's) protests, continues to go out and fight for what she believes in.
- Adva clashes with Teira for for the first time in Southern Germania outside of Munich (Mega City). Teira gives her a hard time due to her unique and difficult powers, but Adva eventually lands a good hit, pushing her to retreat.
- Adva clashes with Samae right after that battle, who tries to catch her off-guard. She defeats Samae after a long, drawn-out battle, as she was having fun...
### MAY
- Arete and Adva reconcile, as Rai points out the conflict Imbra's currently going through, and beg them to build a better environment for her at the very least.
- After some deliberation, Arete ends up apologizing for her tense control over Adva, and Adva apologizes for fighting so hard despite Arete's anxiety. They make a few mutual promises to keep one another in check.
- Now with Arete's approval, Adva doesn't feel forced to go out to prove herself. Yet, she still does, because it's beyond that now. She's realized she _enjoys_ being on the frontlines.
- Adva finds herself fighting alongside the new Strategists for Peace peacekeepers forces in Wellington, a relatively small city (by this world's standards) which had been captured by Humans who began to try and enslave weaker Elementals.
- Adva rejects an Alaskan Invitation to join their army.
- Arete coerces Adva into resting, as she had been going out for 12 days straight at this point with little sleep... she definitely needed it, but she was addicted to the rush. Plus, Endsight hadn't been as active anymore. Perhaps she really could rest.
- Finally settling down, Adva gets to hang out with Imbra a little more, raising Imbra's spirits upon the still looming Kael crisis.
- Rai and Adva continue to train, eventually learning about Forbidden Element Aspects through Rai's Forbidden Element books, specifically the one on Squall.
### JUNE
- Upon hearing of a looming full-force Endsight attack on a city, Adva finds her way there to stop them. Luckily, her group at home was asleep, allowing her to sneak out without them saying much to stop her.
- When she arrives at Instantinople (Hyper City), she is forced to confront all four members at once. Yes, four! There was an extra one she was unaware of. Through a battle that lasts almost an hour, Adva eventually comes out on top after she claims Aspect: Liberation in the middle of the battle, pushing her that extra mile further.
- Due to her saving Instantinople from sure demise, the government there decides to change her title from "Tempest" to "Zephyr", using their sway as the leader of the Union of Mankind to get it done.
- Adva clashes with Samae again after an ambush in Madagascar. Samae has become stronger, but Adva doesn't let it get to her. She still ends up winning the battle, but the fight is closer than the last one.
- When an injured Adva arrives back home, Arete and Imbra are distraught. Rai believes in and trusts Adva's judgement, pulling Imbra to do so, too. Arete is still weary, and urges Adva to be more careful, intentionally beating around the bush to avoid starting an argument.
- Adva, for the first time in a while, takes a moment to sit down with Kael. While he's... lost, she still admits how much he means to her and Imbra. She doesn't know if it helps, but she felt the need to get it off of his chest just in case it was the last time she ever saw him.
- Just a day later, she sits with Imbra and Kael, chatting like the days before the war. Though Kael isn't entirely himself, it reminds her of a peaceful, simpler time...
- Adva's quickly back on the field as a bunch of terrorist attacks destabilize the world further, and she feels required to help.
- Adva meets Dera | While only temporarily, Dera and Adva cross paths. They don't exchange much information about each other as they're actively trying to save people, but they do notice that the other is a Forbidden User.
### JULY
- Adva meets Zezri | Just passing by one another, similar to the situation with Dera. However, they do exchange a bit more about one another, as Zezri oddly seemed to be a bit of a fan.
- Adva, after flip-flopping between stances, finally decides to spend more time at home after Kael reaches Gate VIII of Death... the final gate before the Gate IX - The point of no return.
- The entire group - Adva, Imbra, Rai, and Arete (despite the latter two not knowing Kael too well) divert all of their energy, attention, and love to Kael in a last-ditch attempt to trigger emancipation and save him.
- Adva finds herself sharing sleepless nights alongside Imbra, refusing to let her suffer alone.
### AUGUST
- Kael is finally emancipated from Death. While he doesn't retain many memories from his life before the element was thrown upon him, he does recall his deep love and affection for Imbra.
- Imbra and Kael get married in the rainforest with Adva, Arete, and Rai all watching.
- Imbra pulls Adva to remain longer with her and Kael before going back in the field, promising to join her if she did. So she does. They spend the rest of the month reconnecting and growing into a family, including Rai and Arete. They spend the rest of August this way.
- Imbra and Adva begin training with Kael and Rai (Arete is still hesitant to participate in combat). Kael quickly proves that he and his element are stronger than they look...
###  SEPTEMBER
- After Kael stresses how ready he is multiple days in a row, Adva leads Imbra and Kael to Cairo (Rai and Arete refused to go), a city in the tight grip of a rich warlord named Khali Aziz who not only seized the city from the government and used it as blackmail, but turned the citizens inside against each other so they wouldn't realize he was exploiting them.
- The first day there, the trio slips into the city undetected. They find a FOB (Forward Operating Base) in a small, relatively neglected apartment complex in a poorer district of the Hyper City. There, they gather info from locals: guards, curfews, active conflict zones...
- Adva is the first to interrogate and execute a guard she catches alone. While a risky move that the other two condemn, she stands by her choice, as it gave her lots more info than the civilians did.
- By the second day, they have important names. Kael goes out on his own to assassinate them while Imbra and Adva map out the surrounding area and document guard patrols.
- Kael returns with sweeping success. Adva begins preparing for a full-on assault, desperate for some action and bored of the covert ops. Imbra urges her to be patient.
- During a curfew, Adva gets into combat with a group of patrolling guards while Imbra and Kael sleep. She kills 13, and captures the last remaining one. She brings him to the FOB.
- When Kael and Imbra awake on the third day, they all interrogate this one. They get the names of commanders. Administrators. When Kael offers to assassinate them, Imbra says they should be captured instead. Adva agrees. They execute the captured guard.
- They spend the third day attempting to find a way to sneak into the human sectors to capture key administrators. When it's deemed near impossible without all out conflict, Kael is sent alone. He returns in the night with one of the named administrators who refuses to speak under any circumstance, instead taunting the trio. Adva kills her out of frustration.
- On day four, Kael captures another one, but it's a repeat of the last one. Adva begins to try and push the idea of an all-out attack.
- Nearby civilians have grown suspicious of the three, and some begin to recognize Adva through her disguises. Adva stays in-doors more often.
- Day five, there's finally a breakthrough: A commander that breaks. He tells them where Khali stays. Where higher-up administrators stay. He even tells them that security forces have long suspected an infiltration, and with his disappearance, Khali may leave soon. So the trio decide to strike immediately.
- They move through the streets of Cairo quickly, fighting through guards and regular civilians who mistake them for normal Elemental invaders. They reach Khali's tower, but he's already began an escape attempt through a tunnel. Adva, being the quickest, chases his vehicle down and captures him, bringing him before the people, where they execute him.
- The trio leaves the city shortly after this all, celebrating it as a major victory.
- Arete is very worried for them, and begs them to stay. Rai seems... indifferent, but he congratulates them on their victory.
- When they hear that the government and military of the Nile Corporation has been using their military and power in the city in similar ways, they go back to break that up, too, but this time in an operation that only takes a few hours.
- This time, when they come back, Arete seems almost enraged. She starts another argument with Adva, who takes Imbra and Kael out of there, which she claims is for good. She leads them to the young nation of Frei-Se.

- The trio takes time to adjust to their new home before continuing more operations.
- After some short downtime, the trio go together to Freecity (previously Freetown, Megacity) to assist Elementals in revolting against a violently oppressive government. It gets minimal news coverage as the media is covering other Forbidden User movements and clashes.
- Adva goes alone to an Anarchy Zone in the Arabian Desert to defend Sakai Elementals from nearby nations and internal human factions after they let out an SOS call. Kael and Imbra chose to stay back to help Spara, who they had been growing closer with, in governing and organizing her admittedly small, military-guard force.
