**TIMELINE 

# MARCH - Imbra Attacks Berlin, Liberates Kael. Notably there are only 2 casualties and minimal damage to the city.

Imbra, after catching wind of Kael being used in advertisements, sees him in his broken, death-ridden state. Being unaware that the power he wields is what causes him to look that way, she immediately assumes Troy and humans are to blame for this. So, while Adva, Arete, and Rai actually encourage her to remain with them, denouncing Troy’s flaunting of Kael as clear bait, she immediately goes to Berlin, full of rage. Rage she manages to contain when she arrives. She seamlessly coasts through the field of armed guards and vehicles, not even raising a finger to assault. She eventually makes her way to the main road of Berlin, where Troy stands at the end on an elevated podium before a monument, weirdly confident. Imbra remains surrounded by enemy forces, but she continues to step forward, until she’s about 20 meters in front of Troy. The two do not speak a language in common, but Troy taunts her in English anyways, knowing she won’t understand. Unordered, a guard charges at Imbra with his spear cannon (spear cannons are more effective at close range). Before he can even get close enough, Imbra lets her hand up, turning the guard to nothing more than ash in the wind. This is when Troy’s smile falters. Guards begin to hesitate. Comms fill with confusion. Troy finally orders them to display another video of Kael in captivity, clearly dazed and confused. The taunt clearly enrages Imbra, who still manages to remain merciful. However, her rage causes her flames to flare, which sends nearby guards into a bit of a panic. Troy’s defenses are fading just from Imbra’s aura. Her dead cold stare piercing through Troy’s soul begins to intimidate him as he realizes the gravity of what he’s done— he’s brought one of the most dangerous Elementals right before his face. He’s lucky he’s still alive. So, he finally executes his plan- Capture Imbra. He orders his guards to attack, as multiple Tethered Spear Cannons launch at her… only for the metal to completely evaporate before they even touch her, and she has yet to even flinch. Troy’s lost, and he knows it. So, he brings out Kael and returns him to Imbra, and while she’s distracted by him, he silently exits the area. After getting him, Imbra can’t help but hold him for a few minutes. The two stand in a ring of molten metal as guards all across the plaza aim weapons at them in a one-sided embrace, as Kael has little to no recollection of her. Hearing this, she brings him back to the Amazon with her, hoping to help him remember there.

# Immediately after the attack, Whispers and rumors of an underground death cult begin spreading. The public and media give them the name “The Whispers” at this time due to their enigmatic nature.

Deep in long abandoned Soviet Work Camps in Siberia, a small yet fanatical group of Elementals observe the footage of Kael in Berlin. One clocks it immediately- one named Surein, a Poisi Elemental that studied Forbidden Elements as a scientist for the Tixolam, the Poisi Knightsdale. That’s Death, something rumored to come for the mortal realm when the end times are near. It just so happened that his daughter, Yori (who is aligned with him), happened to hear news of the spreading of the Death-adjacent pathogen on Tulia. With this news, Surein and Yori together decided to create their death cult in the camp they resided in, beginning what would later be known as The Whispers. Through their silent recruitment attempts, many would find out about the cult through word of mouth from those who were invited. The original Soviet Work Camp became the cult’s secret base of operations, with another one being slightly further south. These camps would be where their brightest and most important reside, though they would have international bases elsewhere across the globe.

# An uprising of Naturine Elementals in South Japan leads to immense infighting in Tokyo, which leaks over to North Japan. The rival nations find themselves working together to try and restore order to the city.

Two formerly heavily Elemental-Friendly nations in a jointly administered city (politics are messy. Long story short: Tokyo, like Japan, is split between North and South. However, being such an economic asset, the two would rather share it than risk warfare there. So, a treaty is in place that basically allows joint administration over the city) are faced with conflict after the 92 Attack (The attack on Wuhan-Nanjing), namely do to how close it was to Tokyo. Due to this, attitude shifts rapidly. South Japan immediately shifts its opinions on Elementals, trying to deport the ones it brought to South Tokyo, while North Japan is forced to hold special elections as public opinion shifts in the opposite direction of its current politicians. South Japan, being a dictatorial & authoritarian nation, was able to respond faster, and its deportations of Elementals ramped up quickly. However, most Elementals were sent to North Tokyo, which helped nobody. South Tokyo citizens felt no safer with Elementals still being a mere drive away, and now North Tokyo citizens were feeling the pressure as more and more elementals moved into their city. Tensions rose when North Japan tried to build a wall, and South Japan blew it up. Eventually, a small group of Elementals began a small-scale attack on military bases in South Tokyo, which slowly turned into a fully fledged rebellion in the city. A retreating party of wounded Elementals accidentally crossed into North Tokyo, which sent the fighting there, too. The two rival nations found themselves trying to restore order to and save their most valuable asset. 

# APRIL - A few days after, the human nation Europa officially creates an alliance called the Union of Mankind. Many nations funnel into it almost immediately, specifically European and North African nations.

Europe, after watching growing unrest around the world tilt to all out conflict, felt the pressure to prepare preemptively. The nation known as Europa, also widely considered the direct heir to the Terra Union, took initiative and created the largest military alliance the world had ever seen, uniting all of Europe—even nations that were still considered Elemental-Friendly or politically isolated—to defend one another in case of a large, threatening Elemental Uprising. This alliance alone, solidified by Imbra's Berlin Attack, stood strong enough to pacify Elemental uprisings in places like Italy, Iberia, and Scandinavia swiftly, making the idea of anymore seem like a far shot, making Europe a bastion of peace, and even some places, coexistance—a prime target for terrorist organizations. 

# Spear Cannons reach a new peak of effectiveness, and when paired with innovations in aircraft technology to better compete with flying Elementals, humanity begins its first large-scale offensive against Elemental Factions.

With scientists in vulnerable nations like North Japan and China scrambling, the Spear Cannon got an innovation almost every other day. One day they're trying to make it an automatic firing weapon, though that fails. Then they repurpose some construction drones to wield and fire them—success. Then they make the spear cannon easier tk carry, giving them to every soldier. Then they perfect its propulsion, giving it the maximum momentum it has ever reached. Then, while only briefly, nations begin considering restarting production of androids or cyborgs, which many nations shoot down due to masses still being against their use. Troy notably seems to be interested in the idea. Some nations do still consider cybernetics, enhancing their troops with cybernetic enhancements, which many call “The Knight's Method”, courtesy to Samae and the Knights of Gaea. These new soldiers are deployed on the most desperate front—North America—where the city state governing Toronto has been holding out in a brutal defense against Elemental invasions, day and night. These new advancements are so effective, they reach and liberate the nearby cities of Buffalo and Rochester.

# Three new exceptional Elementals show their faces. One with speeds never measured before, sweeping Human military forces (namely in Cairo), one with ambiguous positioning / potential omnipresence, and it seems to supply Elementals for uprisings, and one that causes massive overgrowth of nature in major cities it visits. Rumors of a fourth, silent assassin  roaming around have been flying, but there’s little to no proof of its existence beyond missing ID’s of the suspected assassination targets.

Terkou officially sends out his new Endsight Task Force on their own solo missions, now believing that he has rid of the hole that allowed Imbra and Adva to escape his grasp. Now, beginning step two of his plan—By sending them out to commit atrocities on Endsight's behalf, humanity will hate them. And when humanity hates them, if everything goes right, they will hate Humanity right back. Then and only then will he truly pair them together for tougher tasks that could require them to work together. Regardless, the world watches as these superpowered individuals come out of seemingly nowhere, unleashing an unknown will. This further pushes nations and populations against Elementals, and with recent technological advancements, encourages stronger offensives elsewhere—mainly to send a message to these new individuals. They are strong, but that doesn't mean Humanity will kneel. Furthermore, some other superpowered individuals come out to stop them. Namely, the Knights of Gaea, and another superpowered individual—The Airren One, marked already as a hostile Elemental, yet she's defending humans? Some declare it a ploy to improve her image. Others genuinely think she's trying to turn her reputation around. One thing is for sure, though: they'd rather she keep doing it, because she's buying them time. 

# MAY - Icik and Snai Elementals migrate en masse to Antarctica, forming the nation of Frei-Se

Upon the formation of the Union of Mankind, many Elementals, despite its own political advertising, believe that the alliance is made as an anti-elemental defense initiative, and they have evidence to believe so. 20/21 of its recent military actions have been against Elementals. Member nations were encouraged to surveille their Elemental residents. Violence against Elementals was often overlooked, or not treated with the same severity as violence against humans. This mainly bothered and affected Icik and Snai elementals, who lived in Scandinavia—the region owned by The Republic of The North. Another Terra Union descendant, but a fairly anti-Elemental nation, with the coldest regions in the continent… exactly why it was the largest concentration of the two elemental types on the planet. However, as The Republic of The North became more anti-Elemental, they decided to flee to a continent that, among the groups, was referred to as a land of hope and future. Tens of millions of elementals in one of the largest mass migrations the globe had ever seen from the region created makeshift ships to journey to the continent. Some didn’t make it. Most who did didn’t make it peacefully. They fought through anti-Elemental naval patrols, aerial patrols, and human ambushes from nations that discovered their plans. Yet, they made it. Many from other cold regions, including Siberia and Canada, found their way down, too. They created the nation of Frei-Se— a fragile nation with an unclear future. Democratic processes continually failed as the nation constantly devolved into corruption scandals and snap elections to heal what previous leaders had caused. It didn’t help that human nations were sending assassins to prevent the nation from stabilizing. The nation desperately needed a figure not only capable, but willing. 

# Other super-powered Elementals are officially classified after ADS deliberation

While the other three (possibly four) Forbidden Element Users were quickly classified after their first appearances, it was long known to the ADS that many more existed. They measure these uniquely powerful Elementals in magnitudes of power expression and/or drastic difference in appearance or behavior. Three came from recent incarceration (though they've all since escaped): one with a deeply dark element, with the ability to teleport. No given alias yet, but her followers called her “Karai”, something that was translated to be something roughly relating to the English word “Guidance”. One with an entirely unique element, never seen before. It seems to revolve around vibrations, as neural signals were tracked through their mind when sounds that should have been inaudible to them were played. No given alias yet. Another, with the alias Kael, known to be his name. Previously known to be Snai (Snow), his element changed after what the ADS assumed to be a return to his home planet. HIs snow is discolored to be gray, and he notably can no longer use his element. He seems disconnected from reality, requiring much more stimulation to grab his attention. Two from notable planetary events: An elemental with what seems to be time capabilities. While reports are sparse, the public of Londina has many images of him on social media, where they refer to him simply as ‘Paradox’. Alongside him is another, who seems to have bridged the gap between ice and snow. No known or main alias either, as the social media calls her a multitude of different names. Then, there’s others from unrelated events appearing in the wild: a watrish elemental, with his powers vastly surpassing the usual watrish scales, with simulations suggesting he could conjure an oceans worth of water if required. There is no main alias for him, either, as the public has no knowledge of him, and he hasn’t done any major actions that would push governments to care enough about him. Another, a familiar face. Samae Herba seemingly has conquered a Forbidden Element, though she refuses to tell us more. Reports are spotty, but the ADS still classifies her. Her alias is known to be “Valor”. With these figures appearing en masse, the ADS begins to search for motives to suppress, contain, and/or eliminate these threats, alongside the other Forbidden Users that have appeared. 

# Official quick response forces are created by the Strategists for Peace

An organization that was turned silent due to being owned by an Elemental, the Strategists for Peace introduced a massive force of volunteer forces—both human and Elemental—to fight on the frontlines in case of any conflict. The task force, sharing the name of the organization it was founded under, grew quickly as people who supported coexistence flocked to join its ranks, and it ended up becoming the final bastion for the argument that it was even possible at all. When it was founded, it had a small force—20 thousand total units, about half Elemental and half Human. Those numbers would grow as more brave souls opted to join the ranks. This force, while promising to protect humans and Elementals alike, was forced to bend to the will of the nations—of which still predominantly Human—causing many people to criticize them as just another pro-human force wearing a coexistence cloak. Though its actions on the field were notably true to its proclaimed task, as it fought both Humans and Elementals if it meant saving the life of either, while refusing to take up arms in times where they deemed it unnecessary. They commonly found themselves clashing with the Knights of Gaea as the two groups basically have a stance being polar opposites of one another, striking a near instant rivalry. 

# The Empire of Alaska officially takes the side of Elemental Revolters—the first human nation to ever do so

The Empire of Alaska—a historically fickle and aggressive nation with a rather enigmatic monarch officially announces its sympathies for Elemental revolters, mobilizing for potential with war with its neighboring countries and cities. It is unknown what pushed the Empire to do such a thing, but many cite its winter warfare tactics paired with a formerly high population of recently migrated Snai elementals as a potential reason, as if they're trying to get those Elementals to come back. Unfortunately for the Empire, it doesn't work. Instead, they gain the loyalties of many other Elementals ready to fight for their freedoms and rights, preparing potential invasions of neighboring Toronto and nearby anarchist lands. The military of the nation surges immediately, from 1.1 million personnel to over 3 million. Many humans are let go from their fighting positions to be replaced by Elementals, and those who remain use weaponry created by Elementals. This “betrayal” is taken heavily across the globe, as both the ADS and many nations sanction and declare war on the Empire. Its people, who notoriously follow the monarch without question, seem fearful of the decision, but still trust the monarch wholeheartedly. The nation becomes the target of the Knights of Gaea, to punish them and send a message—but new Elemental residents manage to fight off the Knights, causing some to question if Alaska made the right decision. 

# JUNE - First Ever Endsight Full-Task-Force Deployment on the outskirts of Instantinople, Europa.

The previously noted three elementals, now confirmed to be four, were spotted in an attack on the outskirts of Instantinople Mega City, the secondary capital of Europa and the location of the Union of Mankind’s Headquarters. It was the nightmare of any human nation. Four united Forbidden Element users approaching an economically and politically important city, devastating defenses in their wake. SFP, UM, and KoG—Even with all of their ideological differences—deployed together in an attempt to halt the advances of the task force. It is well known that one member of said invasion force can teleport, bringing the others with them. It’s unclear why they didn’t use this ability in the attack, perhaps to spread fear. When the initial defense forces fell—and they fell swiftly—nothing stood in between the task force and Instantinople. The defensive force lasted just under an hour. Evacuations were rushed. Anti-Elemental weapons were imported quickly. Luckily, they were never needed, as the same Airren Elemental that was fighting them when they were solo arrived to fight them again. Despite being outnumbered 4-to-1, she still managed to fight off their attack after a long battle. She was injured, and a second battle ensued as the KoG tried to capture her and she was defended by the SFP, successfully. While this event was hidden from the public (though rumors do surf), the government of Europa is incredibly grateful for the Airren One, coining her new alias—”Zephyr”. 

# International ADS Representative Elections are suspended, justified by the claim “The immediate conflicts could bring an end to Humanity”.

In a somewhat controversial move, the ADS opts to suspend its incoming elections to replace the seats of the representatives from nations including The Thirteen Sectors, The Democratic Republic of Iberia, The Nile Corporation, State Alpha, Sector Iota, the Military Democracy of Oceania, The Collective of Persia, and Quebec. Troy Talona, the former head of the Elemental Wing and now head of the ADS itself, claims that a shift in leadership could be detrimental to the stability that the organization desperately needs. Protests in the Thirteen Sectors, a state that prides itself on democratic representation, and Sector Iota, a highly law-centric state, believed that these measures were too far, and even threatened to stop supporting the war effort (though they were likely empty threats). The people of those states, however, remained silent. Trust in authority was at an all-time high in these trying times, allowing this to slip nearly undetected through the media. In fact, most people only knew about it when the day of elections came, and no polls were put up, only to find that the decision was already made a while ago. At that point, there was nothing they could do. Regardless, Troy Talona and the rest of his ADS council, having gotten away with that, set a dangerous sentiment. The ADS was becoming the face of humanity and its resistance of what they called “The Elemental Plague". 

# The ADS silently rolls back some of its harsher sentiment against the Knights of Gaea and adopt the organization as its official armed forces

For a while, the ADS had been searching for official armed forces. However it was tucked under the rug, as not only did every other nation already have armed forces sufficient enough to defend themselves with the anti-Elemental weapons being produced by the ADS, but they felt the extra administration responsibilities would be undesirable when their attention was needed elsewhere. However, as time went on, and they witnessed the SFP adopt its own armed forces, the problem arose once more. Nobody could decide on what they would do. The Knights of Gaea was mentioned in passing by the representative from the Imperial Military State of China, a nation that was already sympathetic with the group, but it was quickly shot down when others mentioned how the group was classified as a terrorist organization when it began attacking humans sympathetic to Elementals earlier in the conflict. When the attack on Instantinople happened, however, they realized their hands were tied. If Zephyr (Adva) had not arrived to stop the attack, a major administrative and economic city would’ve fallen to the hands of Forbidden Users- or worse. Thus, they decided they had no choice but to adopt an armed force of their own, and they had to do it fast, resulting in them adopting the Knights of Gaea under harsh terms. The leader, Samae, demanded that they be allowed to continue their own unsanctioned operations, payment in vast amounts, secrecy of their research and sciences, and ADS sponsored residence in return for their service, which the desperate council reluctantly agreed to. Troy and Samae would still not be friends, as Samae would remember how Troy declared them terrorists, and Troy would remember how Samae began ruthlessly attacking civilians. However, the delicate relationship between the organizations was born, and the Knights of Gaea would be deployed on behalf of the ADS when necessary. 

# Multiple nations and factions, both Human and Elemental, report assassinations, bombings, cyberattacks, and sabotage throughout their territory. They blame each other, but evidence is still unsure.

Throughout the conflict, espionage has rarely been used. Elementals struggle to blend in with Humans, and Humans could hardly find valuable targets worth risking an infiltration for. Likewise, Elementals hardly ever had the large-scale cyber infrastructure to be targeted or do the targeting, holding only small subsections of the planet’s internet that would likely do more damage to humanity if it were the target of a cyberattack. Imagine the horror of both sides when they suddenly got reports of terrorist attacks across their nations. Bombings, infiltrations & assassinations, kidnappings, cyberattacks, shootings, all you could imagine. The loss of life is extreme, and both species blame the other. These actions not only result in more Elemental uprisings, mainly in places like Mexico Super City and Catalonia, but now sides are fighting harder. Elementals use tactics that endanger civilians, and humans use chemical weapons that care not for their target. The loss of life in the global conflict rises exponentially, resulting in some new Forbidden Users showing their faces again in attempts to minimize the damage. Alongside them, the growing SFP force and the now ADS-Sponsored Knights of Gaea fight tooth and nail to push their own agendas in this now terrified world. Military membership also skyrockets in response to these terrorist attacks. To anyone anywhere, it feels like the world was a blaze of flames, and it somehow lit itself on fire once more.

# Global Trade breaks down as more organized Elemental Factions dominate the seas & skies

Elemental factions, being invigorated by the terrorist attacks, move into methods they were previously not using: Shutting down the planet. It has been known to them how important trade is for humanity, and even though it was already on a much lower scale, desperate shipments were allowed through as most Elementals still didn’t want to harm civilians. However, due to these terror attacks, factions have begun to throw this sentiment out the window. Watrish Elementals began to patrol the oceans, introducing the possibility that every meter of water could host one of them, making shipments a death wish. Elementals that can fly, mainly Airren and Firen Elementals, would patrol the skies, raiding aerial shipments. This pushed humanity to deploy their experimental airforces, which was a major failure as they were overrun by the much more air-experienced force. Drones, however, proved to work as they could match the numbers in the sky. Regardless, global trade had come to a grinding, sudden halt, causing famines & disease to begin to spring up across the world for humanity. Notably, it also halted the shipment of weaponry, forcing some nations to ration their remaining anti-Elemental equipment. Some Elementals, who believed their brothers and sisters were going too far, would go out of their way to assist human civilians—something that the media notably never reported. The ADS took new measures to try and counteract these tactics. Drone Submarines were used to detect and attack Watrish Elementals, and as mentioned previously, drones were used in aerial combat alongside aircraft to match Elemental Numbers in the sky. A true fight for humanity’s survival had begun, and if they lost, it could spell doom for entire swathes of the planet. 

# JULY - Cairo falls under the control of a wealthy billionaire turned warlord with a personal army

Using the distraction of the armed forces of the governing nation, a wealthy man opts to use his own personal army and government ties to turn Cairo into his own pseudo-independent nation, where he keeps civilians oblivious by breeding conflict between Humans and Elementals and keeps the government away by threatening to damage the city massively. Under his rule, Cairo was turned into a warzone-work camp hybrid, where civilians who worked were promised protection from the other side. Using this tactic, this man enriched himself through Naturine architecture and other trade to nearby struggling nations, specifically European ones like Europa and Germany. Many nations, who were well aware of his illegal, human rights violating tactics, simply turned a blind eye as he became their main source of supplies to keep their citizens fed.

# The struggling Arctic nation of Frei-Se, formerly ignored on the global stage, elects and crowns a queen- Spara Dei-Sor, a Forbidden User.

Frei-Se was formed a while ago by Icik and Snai refugees who heard of the continent of Antarctica and wanted to settle there. However, under its lack of stability in its democratic processes due to foreign intervention, and even with the recent terror attacks hitting it very hard, it was on the brink of collapse. That is until Spara Dei-Sor—The Subzero User—appeared in the nation. She had heard of its troubles and wanted to come assist. However, when civilians saw her, they knew she was the one who had to rule them. A Subzero user can use both Ice and Snow, a signature of their unity. Many had also seen her heroic acts in response to the terror attacks and believed she’d keep the nation stable and peaceful effortlessly. Even though Spara had turned down the offer many times, when she kept on getting elected, and the democratic processes she tried to set up kept conceding their power to her, she conceded, becoming Queen Dei-Sor officially. The world was ecstatic. Media across the globe reported the first elemental nation rising in humanity’s own backyard, further pushing anti-Elemental sentiment as Queen Dei-Sor became known as “Frozen Hades” to many humans. She cared little for what humanity had to say about her, though, as she took her new role as Queen and Guardian of Frei-Se very seriously, building massive ice walls around the entire continent of Antarctica and building a city known simply as Darsk, a reference to the capitals of the Icik and Snai nations back on their home planet. The nation had never seen such prosperity, and her actions would only make her more popular among the civilians, with many becoming devout followers of her, causing the nation of Frei-Se to quickly gain more civilians and military personnel. Though nobody really knew this, Endsight was formerly based in Antarctica, and had to flee under fears that the now self-proclaimed peaceful Elemental nation would snitch on their location. So, they relocated to the Sahara Desert, a barren wasteland where very few passed. 

# The first Alaskan Offensives begin through Quebec, with the end goal of reaching Toronto

Alaska had long been organizing its military for its first offensives. Its main neighbor, Quebec, and self-proclaimed defender of the Toronto City-State, had long been preparing for the conflict. With help from the neighboring nation of the Thirteen Sectors, also considered to be the main Superpower of North America, multiple defensive posts, walls, and forts were set up with massive anti-personnel weaponry and minefields (Alaska is known to rarely use vehicles). When the days of the offensives finally came, Quebec was still caught off-guard. Spearheaded offensives were sent directly through weaker points of the walls, allowing near instant encirclements. The already small Quebecian Air Force stood no chance against the Flying Elementals in the sky, causing immediate Alaskan air superiority. The defenses collapsed within hours, and Quebec had fallen back to its own cities, preparing to mount a defense. Toronto was on its own, barring some volunteer forces from neighboring nations desperate to prevent the Alaskan Menace from pushing into the rest of the continent. Even the Socialist Federal States of the Pacific East, a Democratic-Socialist nation that previously had a non-aggression pact with the Alaskan Empire, began making moves to break the pact due to fears that its own mega city Seattle would fall if the Empire went unchecked. North America, while suffering from mass Elemental Protests/Uprising and instability from recent terror attacks, was now uniting against another threat. The media reported it sparsely, but to them, Alaska had lost its title as a human nation. It was now known as a “Hybrid Nation”, as Elementals and Humans both resided in it, though many argued it was still a human nation, as no Elementals had a role in any governance due to its autocratic government. The recently empowered Frei-Se had become the main example of an Elemental Nation- governed by Elementals.

# Human currencies begin to collapse as nations resort to bartering and free aid to keep allies afloat

Elementals are fairly good at sustaining themselves. Naturine Elementals are capable of growing food. Firen Elementals are known to be good hunters, also being able to cook the food. Every elemental is capable of architecture, it just takes substantial education to build better buildings. Elementals are even capable of making their own weapons, having had a holiday back on their home planet for the entire purpose of making their own weaponry. Humans, however, do not have that luxury. They fight tooth and nail everyday for resource hubs and trade routes, everyday risking a collapse. Due to this, confidence in currencies has fallen to an all-time low, and many citizens and nations alike simply resort to bartering or lend-leasing treaties to avoid the headache of using the currencies to begin with. The thin trade routes pushing through heavily defended areas and mountains try to naturally protect convoys, which Elementals had to fight unconventional guerrilla-like tactics to breach, where they were still fighting through armored convoys and anti-Elemental weaponry to disrupt. Luckily for humanity, some trade routes do survive.

# AUGUST - Multiple super cities fall to Elemental Factions and armies simultaneously, a major strike to human morale across the globe.

For a long time, humanity had managed to hold their highly fortified and important Super Cities. A handful of mega cities had long fallen to Elemental factions, which was to be expected. But a super city falling is the equivalent to the city of Troy falling through conventional warfare—And three have just fallen. Firstly, in the bloodiest, longest battle, Singapore. Also known as Port Terra due to its historical size and trade importance, Singapore was always diverse and work-oriented. When Elementals arrived, they were the first to claim many to join their workforce. However, when the first attacks on Wuhan-Nanjing happened, a nearby super city, the governing nation known as North South-East Asia (Or North Sea for short) began to crack down on those populations. Elementals within the city, particularly inspired by the attack, began an uprising immediately, and Port Terra had been in long conflict since then, one of the main reasons trade had been collapsing. In the face of a monsoon, however, Watrish Elementals in the city used the environmental attack to push through defenses, allowing for the city to fall after months of conflict. North Sea felt it immediately, as its capital had collapsed and its government was compromised, a massive power vacuum had opened up—something neighboring, stronger nations had to discuss. Next was Londina, also known as London Super City. Londina had long been feeling tensions as Elementals within the city were treated particularly poorly by the city due to its direct ties to the ADS. Forbidden Element users (Spara and Dera, long before past Meso Arc Events listed here) had come to try and keep the peace. However, when they left after realizing it was hopeless, the city collapsed into armed conflict where the Union of Mankind had been present since its inception. A long, drawn out and cold rainstorm eventually befell Londina, where Watrish Elementals used similar tactics as those that were used in Singapore to push through and finally fully conquer the city. The governing body, an Atlantic Naval Command from the left over northern naval forces of the long collapsed Terra Union, felt it immediately. Londina was not only their connection to the rest of Europe, but it was a cultural staple that they swore they would never let fall. Their morale took a major hit. Finally, Toronto. While not technically being an Elemental Faction, the fall of Toronto to Alaska came quickly. Celebrations across the nation shook the rest of the continent, yet the monarch realized something quickly. She was at dire risk of the Elementals who had come to serve her empire revolting due to their democratic outlook. So, she turned Toronto into a puppet state where Elementals would be housed in their own, small democracy that had to answer to her, temporarily solving that issue—but humans in her nation who wanted democracy grew bitter and jealous. Regardless, her nation stood strong. Its long objective of conquering Toronto, the gate to the rest of the continent, had finally succeeded. Now she was preparing to strike her long rival: The Thirteen Sectors. These losses hit the morale of humanity hard, and nations were strengthening their Super Cities in fear that theirs could be next.

# North Sea collapses into internal political chaos as the fate of many of its political leaders are unknown

North Sea, a representative democratic state, collapsed after the sudden fall of its capital. Its government remained in Port Terra (also known as Singapore), its capital city, despite ongoing fighting with Elemental rebels inside due to beliefs that the fall of the city was impossible. Furthermore, moving the entire administration of the nation was more costly for what they were willing to pay, and they considered remaining in the city a show of strength to push the morale of their troops and citizens alike forward. So, they remained in the city, though distant from the sectors with active war. However, when the city’s defenses surrendered to the assaulting Watrish Elementals, the government was still inside of it, thus they were captured by the rebels.
