# EARTH
## ⦿ Berlin Attack
An attack on Berlin by Imbra alone- but it's her smallest, least damaging attack. She comes for one purpose: To recieve Kael. Troy and the ADS try to fight her, causing multiple quick casualties- but notably, Imbra allows retreat. She leaves with Kael, having little blood on her hands. The attack humiliates humanity, as the world government only survived due to her mercy, and invigorates Elementals who already believed in their superiority.
## ⦿ Formation of The Gray Lives
A breakaway faction from Endsight, The Gray Lives is an extinctionist group hellbent on ending all life on Gaea after declaring neither species worthy of its beauty. Though the group starts out as purely Elementals, Humans begin to join as well. The group waits before they attack, building strength and recruiting others using online forums and underground networks. 
## ⦿ Icik & Snai Migration to Antarctica; Formation of Frei-Se (Polar Union)
After hearing os a similar continent on this planet as to their home & disgruntled by the Burning of Gaea, Icik and Snai Elementals migrate in massive droves towards Antarctica. Though, leadership struggles to form and the nation is very decentrallized and messy. The nation nears collapse multiple times in its youth as political leaders are inexperienced, have little public support, and government systems fail.
## ⦿ First Endsight Task Force Deployment
Terkou's new group of Forbidden Users have their first deployments. Aeri, codenamed Warsight, Zero, codenamed Gravesight, Teira, codenamed Riftsight, and Is'ale, codenamed Thornsight, are deployed to Mexico City to cause havoc and distrupt North American supply lines to support nearby Elemental Factions. Later deployments are distrupted by Adva (who they know as Tempest), who prevents them from causing further harm- but causes much harm herself.
## ⦿ First Gray Lives Terror Attacks
The Gray Lives begin massive and unrelenting terror attacks on cities held by both Humans and Elementals, building further hatred and drive for conflict by destroying major capitals and economic hubs. Thousands are killed and many more wounded by these attacks, and victims blame the opposing species. New wars break out with larger loss of life as both Elementals and Humans begin ignoring usual unspoken war rules; people like Spara, Zezri, and Zrili intervene to prevent more death.
## ⦿ Queening of Spara Dei-Sor, The Polar Queen/Frozen Hades
Frei-Se, the still struggling nation, finds out about Spara Dei-Sor and immediately vote her as their leader, despite her not even being in the nation. Once she arrives, the democratic processes of the nation dissolve itself to crown her Queen. Even with Spara's attempts to reinstante Democracy, the people of Frei-Se continually vote for her to become Queen for life. Reluctantly, Spara gives in, and becomes known as Frozen Hades among Humans, The Polar Queen among her citizens, and Queen Dei-Sor among her friends. The nation under her popular leadership immediately stabilizes and flies to Superpower status. 
## ⦿ Super Cities fall to Elemental Factions
The first super cities fall to organized Elemental factions, being Toronto during the winter, London during a large rainstorm, and Singapore during a monsoon. These cultural and economic losses further drive humanity as 57% of the remaining Human GDP is spent on development of new weapons and technology to fight Elementals. The war peaks at this point as fully fledged factions & Frei-Se ignite rage and fear in human citizens.
## ⦿ Failure to cause Val Pollution
The Gray Lives tries to extract val from the air, but notice that some underlying force is beating them to it. Their technology is abandoned, but they begin to look deeper into it, trying to discover what force could possibly be preventing val from lingering that Tulia lacked. Though most investigations lead to nothing, some suggest a more powerful underlying being is behind it.
## ⦿ Rai & Watrish Migration to Frei-Se
Rai, after hearing of the prosperity of Frei-Se, gathers and leads Watrish Elementals to the nation, where they live on and defend the coasts. Under Spara and Rai's leadership, three Elemental races have been united- Watrish, Snai, and Icik, all under one banner. It's around this time that Frei-Se begins to build its own military after fearing potential Human invasion.
## ⦿ Formation of the Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC)
Formed by the Strategist for Peace (Kterla & Wykoi), The Peace & Coordination Guild, commonly refered to as PAC, is a group of four Forbidden Users aiming to prevent loss of *all* life. With Zezri under the alias Umbriss, Zrili as Echo, Arete as Sentinel, and K'zorak as Axis, and sometimes Dera as Paradox, they work against aggressive factions like Endsight and The Knights of Gaea whilst participating in diplomatic missions, becoming symbols of peace.
## ⦿ Mass Assassinations by The Gray Lives; World Destabilizes & First Same-Species Wars
The Gray Lives watching PAC begin undoing their work caused a slight panic. So, they began to order their hidden members to begin what they called Operation Upside-Down, which was to assassinate multiple strong figures while painting blame onto others. Hundreds of high-ranking figures, even some in Frei-Se, lose their lives. Kterla narrowly dodges an attempt against her own life, painted to be from Endsight. These assassinations sparks Same-Species wars as long standing Human tensions finally explode (Mainly in Asia, North America, and Europe) and Elemental Factions fight over land claims.
## ⦿ Kael is Emancipated; The Symbols unofficially form
Finally being Emancipated by Imbra, the pair join Adva on her vigilantism. They become known as The Symbols due to mystery but popularity, with Imbra and Adva still under the name Phoenix and Tempest, and Kael joining as Morrow. Together, they act as a less regulated and more extreme version of PAC, fighting exclusively for what they believe in. Though, they are known to lean towards Elemental Superiority, and often condemn Human Actions that Elementals get away with in their moral code.
## ⦿ The Knights of Gaea crowned the Greatest Elemental Fighters
With expanded Tech augments and unique tactics of ambush and surprise attack, the Knights of Gaea begin to regularly defeat low-tier and mid-tier Elementals. With this development, multiple Human governments begin to funnel money and power into them, much to the dismay of Troy and the ADS, who still dislike the Knights hostility to humans who don't align with them. 
## ⦿ The Moon Disaster
The Gray Lives, using Roccian Val placed all across the moon, begin to attract the moon towards the Earth, aiming to have it strike the planet and wipe out all life within 12 hours after they began to pull. With such a disasterous development, the Burning of Gaea is put on a temporary pause as the world unites to try and stop the disaster. Frei-Se's Watrish population commanded by Rai work to stabilize the tides while Dera representing both The Symbols & PAC and Aeri representing Endsight are sent to the moon, both aiming to stop the moon, but Aeri aiming to stop it later. The two duel on the Moon, starting their rivalry. The moon is eventually stopped once Dera spreads Arete's val across the moon, allowing her to override the pull of The Gray Lives and return the moon to where it belongs, and the entire planet breathes a sigh of relief. 
## ⦿ Rai & Spara Marriage; Dei-Sor Royal Dynasty
Having been allies and symbols of their people, Rai and Spara marry, forming the Dei-Sor Royal Dynasty as Rai takes Spara's last name. They hold a marital ceremony that slightly unites their souls, and slightly merging their abilities as Rai's Water becomes colder as his rain turns into hail, and Spara gains the ability to cast heavy blizzards in her area. Recordings of the marriage cause outrage among humans as Elemental customs spook them. 
## ⦿ HUMAN Units improve, reaching 50% win rate against mid-tier Elementals.
Extensive investments into the ADS military causes HUMAN units to be optimized for warfare against mid-tier Elementals, though high-tier Elementals continue to cause problems. Alongside this, Spear Cannons become far stronger and foot infantry also become more effective against Elementals. This event brings hope to humans who previously thought the war was hopeless, consequently raising the manpower and enlistment of the ADS. 
## ⦿ Frei-Se Great War | Humans invade Antarctica
After long planning, Nations in South Africa, Oceania, and South America launch a massive Aerial Invasion of Frei-Sa with Jets flying over to bomb major cities and attempting to destroy the nation economically. Luckily for Frei-Se, The Symbols happened to be visiting their Queen, so the attack wasn't as bad as it could've been- but the damage wasn't negligible. Hundreds of jets were taken down, but multiple cities were flattened. This attack radicalized the formerly peaceful Frei-Se, but also further boosted human morale.
## ⦿ Death Pandemic; Death Disease spreads thanks to The Gray Lives.
The final move of The Gray Lives: Releasing the Death Forbidden into major cities across the globe. All around the world, including Frei-Se, citizens began to suffer from immense internal pain, gray skin/elements, and paralysis as the Death Disease purged through towns and massacred millions. This is the first time The Gray Lives made themselves noticed as they publicly claimed responsibility for this, once again uniting the world against them.
## ⦿ Val Suction is detected; ADS Begins Val Suction
The ADS, after noticing a distinct lack of Val Pollution, noticed inexplicable Val Suction- the same Val Suction that The Gray Lives found years ago. This discovery is major, because it proves that extracting val from a non-elemental source is possible. The ADS immediately begins trying Val Suction themselves, aiming to empower future weapons.
## ⦿ The Gray Lives are defeated.
The Gray Lives are defeated in Siberia by PAC, its leader being captured and put on public trial in Berlin. Humans and Elementals alike watched the trial, both unanimously despising The Gray Lives and the pain they caused. The leader and its members are executed, but small splinter factions of The Gray Lives escape and continue to cause havoc throughout minor cities in the world.
## ⦿ New HUMAN units known as Valdroids act in unexpected ways on the battlefield.
After successfully harvesting Val from the air, the ADS deploys their new weapon- Valdroids, combat robots with val capabilities. These robots, while few and far-between, are exceptional in combat against even high-tier Elementals, though they have limited val. Later innovations allow them to suck val out of the air, which is useful as their enemies will now commonly feed them more power.
