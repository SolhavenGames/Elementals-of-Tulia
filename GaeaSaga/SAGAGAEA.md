# EARTH
## ⦿ Berlin Attack
An attack on Berlin by Imbra alone- but it's her smallest, least damaging attack. She comes for one purpose: To receive Kael. Troy and the ADS try to fight her, causing multiple quick casualties- but notably, Imbra allows retreat. She leaves with Kael, having little blood on her hands. The attack humiliates humanity, as the world government only survived due to her mercy, and invigorates Elementals who already believed in their superiority.
## ⦿ Formation of The Whispers
A breakaway faction from Endsight, The Whispers is an extinctionist group hellbent on ending all life on Gaea after declaring neither species worthy of its beauty. Though the group starts out as purely Elementals, Humans begin to join as well. The group waits before they attack, building strength and recruiting others using online forums and underground networks. 
## ⦿ Icik & Snai Migration to Antarctica; Formation of Frei-Se (Polar Union)
After hearing os a similar continent on this planet as to their home & disgruntled by the Burning of Gaea, Icik and Snai Elementals migrate in massive droves towards Antarctica. Though, leadership struggles to form and the nation is very decentralized and messy. The nation nears collapse multiple times in its youth as political leaders are inexperienced, have little public support, and government systems fail.
## ⦿ First Endsight Task Force Deployment
Terkou's new group of Forbidden Users have their first deployments. Aeri, codenamed Warsight, Zero, codenamed Gravesight, Teira, codenamed Riftsight, and Is'ale, codenamed Thornsight, are deployed to Mexico City to cause havoc and disrupt North American supply lines to support nearby Elemental Factions. Later deployments are disrupted by Adva (who they know as Tempest), who prevents them from causing further harm- but causes much harm herself.

## ⦿ First Gray Lives Terror Attacks
The Whispers begin massive and unrelenting terror attacks on cities held by both Humans and Elementals, building further hatred and drive for conflict by destroying major capitals and economic hubs. Thousands are killed and many more wounded by these attacks, and victims blame the opposing species. New wars break out with larger loss of life as both Elementals and Humans begin ignoring usual unspoken war rules; people like Spara, Zezri, and Zrili intervene to prevent more death.
## ⦿ Queening of Spara Dei-Sor, The Polar Queen/Frozen Hades
Frei-Se, the still struggling nation, finds out about Spara Dei-Sor and immediately votes her as their leader, despite her not even being in the nation. Once she arrives, the democratic processes of the nation dissolve itself to crown her Queen. Even with Spara's attempts to reinstate Democracy, the people of Frei-Se continually vote for her to become Queen for life. Reluctantly, Spara gives in, and becomes known as Frozen Hades among Humans, The Polar Queen among her citizens, and Queen Dei-Sor among her friends. The nation under her popular leadership immediately stabilizes and flies to Superpower status. 
##  ⦿ Super Cities fall to Elemental Factions
The first super cities fall to organized Elemental factions, being Toronto during the winter, London during a large rainstorm, and Singapore during a monsoon. These cultural and economic losses further drive humanity as 57% of the remaining Human GDP is spent on development of new weapons and technology to fight Elementals. The war peaks at this point as fully fledged factions & Frei-Se ignite rage and fear in human citizens.
##  ⦿ Failure to cause Val Pollution
The Whispers tries to extract val from the air, but notice that some underlying force is beating them to it. Their technology is abandoned, but they begin to look deeper into it, trying to discover what force could possibly be preventing val from lingering that Tulia lacked. Though most investigations lead to nothing, some suggest a more powerful underlying being is behind it.


## ⦿ Rai & Watrish Migration to Frei-Se
Rai, after hearing of the prosperity of Frei-Se, gathers and leads Watrish Elementals to the nation, where they live on and defend the coasts. Under Spara and Rai's leadership, three Elemental races have been united- Watrish, Snai, and Icik, all under one banner. It's around this time that Frei-Se begins to build its own military after fearing potential Human invasion.

## ⦿ Formation of the Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC)
Formed by the Strategist for Peace (Kterla & Wykoi), The Peace & Coordination Guild, commonly referred to as PAC, is a group of four Forbidden Users aiming to prevent loss of *all* life. With Zezri under the alias Umbriss, Zrili as Echo, Arete as Sentinel, and K'zorak as Axis, and sometimes Dera as Paradox, they work against aggressive factions like Endsight and The Knights of Gaea whilst participating in diplomatic missions, becoming symbols of peace.
## ⦿ The Whispers Terrorist Attacks, World Destabilizes
The Whispers watching PAC begin undoing their work caused a slight panic. So, they began to order their hidden members to begin what they called Operation Strawmen, which was to assassinate multiple strong figures while painting blame onto others, while running many terrorist attacks across the planet. Hundreds of high-ranking figures, even some in Frei-Se, lose their lives. Kterla narrowly dodges an attempt against her own life, painted to be from Endsight. These assassinations spark Same-Species wars as long standing Human tensions finally explode (Mainly in Asia, North America, and Europe) and Elemental Factions fight over land claims. In their terrorist attacks, they use a unique type of bomb known as a Val Bomb. These are the most dangerous weapon that any human or elemental has ever seen, a single bomb with the power to wipe small cities off the map. They plant many, but only three manage to go off- causing hundreds of thousands of deaths and a race to find the technology used to create the bomb.
## ⦿ Kael is Emancipated; The Symbols unofficially form
Finally being Emancipated by Imbra, the pair join Adva on her vigilantism. They become known as The Symbols due to mystery but popularity, with Imbra still under the name Phoenix, Adva newly as Zephyr, and Kael joining as Morrow. Together, they act as a less regulated and more extreme version of PAC, fighting exclusively for what they believe in. Though, they are known to lean towards Elemental Superiority, and often condemn Human Actions that Elementals get away with in their moral code.
## ⦿ The Knights of Gaea crowned the Greatest Elemental Fighters
With expanded Tech augments and unique tactics of ambush and surprise attack, the Knights of Gaea begin to regularly defeat low-tier and mid-tier Elementals. With this development, multiple Human governments begin to funnel money and power into them, much to the dismay of Troy and the ADS, who still dislike the Knights hostility to humans who don't align with them. 
## ⦿ The Moon Disaster
The Whispers, using Roccian Val placed all across the moon, begin to attract the moon towards the Earth, aiming to have it strike the planet and wipe out all life within 12 hours after they begin to pull. With such a disastrous development, the Burning of Gaea is put on a temporary pause as the world unites to try and stop the disaster. Frei-Se's Watrish population commanded by Rai work to stabilize the tides while Dera representing both The Symbols & PAC and Aeri representing Endsight are sent to the moon, both aiming to stop the moon, but Aeri aiming to stop it later. The two duel on the Moon, starting their rivalry. The moon is eventually stopped once Dera spreads Arete's val across the moon, allowing her to override the pull of The Whispers and return the moon to where it belongs, and the entire planet breathes a sigh of relief. 
## ⦿ Rai & Spara Marriage; Dei-Sor Royal Dynasty & Frei-Se Great War
Having been allies and symbols of their people, Rai and Spara marry, forming the Dei-Sor Royal Dynasty as Rai takes Spara's last name. They hold a marital ceremony that slightly unites their souls, and slightly merges their abilities as Rai's Water becomes colder as his rain turns into hail, and Spara gains the ability to cast heavy blizzards in her area. Recordings of the marriage cause outrage among humans as Elemental customs spook them. Immediately after this,  Nations in South Africa, Oceania, and South America launch a massive Aerial Invasion of Frei-Se with Jets flying over to bomb major cities and attempting to destroy the nation economically. Luckily for Frei-Se, The Symbols happened to be visiting their Queen, so the attack wasn't as bad as it could've been- but the damage wasn't negligible. Hundreds of jets were taken down, but multiple cities were flattened. This attack radicalized the formerly peaceful Frei-Se, but also further boosted human morale.
## ⦿ The Whispers Connect to The Spirit Realm
Watching as defeat grows closer, the Whispers get desperate. They begin to reach into the unknown, connecting through to the Spirit Realm and speaking with Emirla, who take this news to their god. While the human God sees an opportunity to grow his own power, the Valoran Gods see the Forbidden Users and opt to try and recruit them, preparing their Emirla to recruit their respective Element. The Great God War seems to slow slightly as the Spirit Realm and Mortal Realm begins to bridge. They also notice how Gaea has begun destabilizing the realms, and plan to prevent further destabilization by unleashing Apex Emirli onto the planet- and begin preparing for that, too.
## ⦿ HUMAN Units improve, reaching 50% win rate against mid-tier Elementals.
Extensive investments into the ADS military causes HUMAN units to be optimized for warfare against mid-tier Elementals, though high-tier Elementals continue to cause problems. Alongside this, Spear Cannons become far stronger and foot infantry also become more effective against Elementals. This event brings hope to humans who previously thought the war was hopeless, consequently raising the manpower and enlistment of the ADS. 
## ⦿ Death Pandemic; Death Disease spreads thanks to The Whispers.
The final move of The Whispers: Releasing the Death Forbidden into major cities across the globe. All around the world, including Frei-Se, citizens began to suffer from immense internal pain, gray skin/elements, and paralysis as the Death Disease purged through towns and massacred millions. This is the first time The Whispers made themselves noticed as they publicly claimed responsibility for this, once again uniting the world against them.
## ⦿ Val Suction is detected; ADS Begins Val Suction
The ADS, after noticing a distinct lack of Val Pollution, noticed inexplicable Val Suction- the same Val Suction that The Whispers found years ago. This discovery is major, because it proves that extracting val from a non-elemental source is possible. The ADS immediately begins trying Val Suction themselves, aiming to empower future weapons.
## ⦿ The Whispers are defeated.
The Whispers are defeated in Siberia by PAC, its leader being captured and put on public trial in Berlin. Humans and Elementals alike watched the trial, both unanimously despising The Whispers and the pain they caused. The leader and its members are executed, but small splinter factions of the Whispers escape and continue to cause havoc throughout minor cities in the world. 
## ⦿ Strange Portals Open
Across the planet, strange portals with energy strong enough to kill humans and low-grade elementals who step too close begin appearing in the skies and dark areas. Panic rises globally as Emirli- spiritual animals with divine val- begin to rush through, some terrorizing local populations. While many fear, the Forbidden Users feel an inexplicable calling into the realm…
