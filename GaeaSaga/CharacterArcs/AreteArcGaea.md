# Arete Ishel | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_The world is a better place when everyone works together!_** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Arete works in a family that owns many global charities, resulting in her being very charitable and selfless herself.

**Ending State**: With Bastion, she resides in the Amazon Rainforest with 3 others, her only goal being to keep them safe.

**Inciting Losses**: The Burning of Gaea making her fear losing Rai.

**Irreversable Actions**: Choosing to search for Bastion, knowing it'll cost her much of her own self-preservation.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being a major grounding point for Imbra and Adva.
## Summary 
A charitable Roccian Elemental, Arete has rarely put herself before others. She travelled around the world constantly with desires to make the world as good of a place as she could. Unfortunately for her, war broke out and tore it apart, causing her and others to flee the planet immediately to avoid the horrors. On Earth/Gaea, she lives with Rai in the Amazon Rainforest, off-the-grid from any Earthling/Gaean Governments. They both heavily distrust humanity, and live off the land with other, nearby elementals, though mostly remaining alone. When the Burning of Gaea begins, their fears peak- and they both seek Forbidden Elements, with Arete seeking Bastion. While Rai opts to try and intervene with the main figures of the Burning of Gaea, Arete opts to remain, failing to stop his ambitions. He eventually succeeds, bringing them both back, where Arete welcomes the two with open arms. She now lives with them and Rai in the rainforest, missing her family- but loving her new one.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Arete travels around Tulia frequently, participating in charity events. She invests a lot of time in these events, expecting little in return.
- At the time, she is in the Watrish city Til, helping distribute food to the less fortunate during some government hardship.
- Arete meets Rai | Their families were already connected, but Arete personally met them when she assisted them with food. The two became good friends almost immediately.
- When the Firen fall to Knightsdale, all of her charity is banished as the nation begins "Nationalization of Strength". 
- Towards the end of the year, she ends up in the Poisi city Texxa, which had just been hit by a major natural disaster.

### ⦿ **T-Year 901**
- Arete participates in 3 different charity events, all reacting to natural disasters as a result of Val Pollution.
- Arete and her family become very anti-Knightsdale, causing the Firen nation to ban them from visiting.
- Silently, her family begins to build funds for people fleeing the young Knightsdale.
- When the Poisi fall to Knightsdale too, they funnel more funds into the potential fleeing fund.

### ⦿ **T-Year 902**
- The Great Massacre in the Icik/Snai nations causes them to fall to Knightsdale as well.
- When the coup in the Snai nation happens, Arete attempts to send humanitarian aid to the citizens- which is blocked by Icik Soldiers.
- Her family halts all operations as Knightsdale movements grow around the globe.

### ⦿ **T-Year 905**
- Despite her family's wishes, Arete visits the Airren Nation, which is projected to fall next.
- When it does fall, she is arrested. Her family uses their political influence to get her released after 8 days behind bars.
- Arete returns when the Civil War breaks out in the nation. She helps feed hungry civilians, leaving food packages around demilitarized zones.
- A mass refugee crisis begins, but Arete and her family's funds helps to keep the world somewhat stable.
- Arete's family forces her to return home, fearing for her safety.
- She keeps an eye on the situation from a distance, desperate to return.

### ⦿ **T-Year 906**
- Arete begins working with other charities and governments to chart out plans for people to flee to Earth/Gaea.
- After leaving a meeting revolving around this, Arete survives an assassination attempt, but she is brutally injured. 

### ⦿ **T-Year 907**
- Arete's family fights to convince her to flee, not wanting her to die.
- Eventually, her parents decide for her, demanding she leave to Earth/Gaea with Rai. Arete very reluctantly agrees.

### ⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, sooner than the people running the fleeing party could have predicted.
- They manage to still flee the planet, Rai and Arete fleeing together.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- The refugees arrive on Earth/Gaea, where the Global Government slowly works to give them homes.
- Arete and Rai opt to leave before they can be documented, choosing to live in isolation rather than be subjects to another species.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2022**
- They eventually settle on the Amazon Rainforest, where they survive on the land together. They work with other nearby Elementals who had similar ideas to them, though the other Elementals migrate frequently.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2026**
- Rai and Arete begin to peek out into human society, visiting nearby cities. They admire humans and their culture, but still do not entirely trust them.
- After the short trip, they return to their camp. Arete declares that humans are "like elementals, but without the element" to Rai.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- The pair moves their camp after humans nearly discovered them.
- They toss around the idea of visiting Humans again.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- The Burning of Gaea convinces Arete that they should stay hidden for longer, ridding of any chance that the two go visit Humans again.
- Rai and Arete search for Deluge and Bastion respectively, just in case.
- Arete is shocked at how quickly Rai unlocks Deluge- she unlocks Bastion a while later.
- She begins to struggle to sleep deeply and frequently wakes up in the middle of the night from any small sound, prepared to defend herself and Rai.
- She tries to convince Rai not to go out and try to stop Imbra, but he does anyway. She worries deeply for him, but respects his free will.
- Rai brags about his successes, but his stories only entertain Arete- who still silently worries for him.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Arete meets Imbra | Imbra is silent and reserved, but Arete seems to love meeting her. She enjoys her flames, and tries desprately to get closer to her.
- Arete meets Adva | Adva seems contained and careful, yet Arete is the opposite- she shows lots of love immediately, confusing the Airren.
- Arete immediately loves the presence of Imbra and Adva, despite what they have done- confusing them both.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Arete tries to grow closer to the pair. She does so successfully, helping them feel loved and appreciated.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Arete has formed a new family with three people, and she loves them all dearly.
