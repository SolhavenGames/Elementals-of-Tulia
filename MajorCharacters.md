## ⦿ Imbra "Phoenix" Hayes (Female) - Firen - Forbidden Element User (Hellfire)  
A Firen born in New Iris into a wealthy popular family. Her father was a warrior, and her mother was a jeweler. Through her youth, she was the prized possession in her family, treated and spoiled as much as possible, while her parents taught her to be selfless and respectful. When the March on Tramalen happened, she was a student away from home, who quickly returned to be with her family in the time of uncertainty. The newly established Knightsdale in Tramalen took her family from her, drafting them into war. Her parents ordered her to flee out of the nation, and she eventually ended up inWrenao where she met her closest friend, Adva. Together, after narrowly surviving Wrenao, they fled to Earth with a new friend, Kael, who called himself Vael. There,she met Xor, who helped her, Adva, and Kael integrate into human culture. Unfortunately, humanity grew hostile. After multiple encounters with Human soldiers and Covenwarriors, she killed Xor for his ties to the Knightsdale movement, and Kael was ripped away, who she had grown to love. She declared a crusade against Humanity with her newly awakened Hellfire, assisted by her long-time friend, Adva.

## ⦿ Adva "Tempest" Anil (Female) - Airren - Forbidden Element User (Squall)

They survived together before finding Vael (Kael) and fleeing to Earth. There, conflicts caused her to silently decide thatthe universe was inherently violent, so she would be violent back. With Imbra by her side, she assists her in the crusade by seeking out Squall.

## ⦿ Rai Ronal (Male) - Watirsh - Forbidden Element User (Deluge)

He never got the chance to succeed, as Dal yon Tulia started. When his city was destroyed in a bombing, he decided to meet up with family friend Arete Ishel to flee the planet to Earth. ## ⦿ Samae Herba (Female) - Nile-Born - Forbidden Element User (Pure)
Raised in a high-war area, Samae is highly distrustworthy of those she’s not close to. Her parents were inventors and fighters, using their tech to augment themselves and their family to become great soldiers. Unfortunately for Samae, her family was lost in a tough battle for her then nation, the Cairo Defense Organization. Unwilling to declare loyalty to her new leaders, she fled, moving frequently. When Elementals were first arriving, she was the first to vocally speak out, gaining traction online from both sides (pro & anti elemental) due to her radical voice and intense outlook. When the Elemental Crusade began, she put herself on the frontlines, leading the anti-elemental Knights of Gaea mercenary group, who share similar, weaker augments to personally defend the planet from Elementals, free of authority.

## ⦿ Troy Talona (Male) - Human (Australia Resident) 
Major Antagonist - Troy was born in Australia just in time to witness the Terra Union. When it collapsed and the Great Terra Wars began 3 years later, his parents were not drafted due to his youth. That is not to say they were not affected by the war; Australia faced heavy fighting. Once it was secured by the Military Democracy of Oceania, Troy was 18, and began to pursue a career in politics based on reunification of humanity and promises of glory. His pledges won him some political power in Oceania, before he was offered a seat in the new, restored world government the “Association of Democratic States” as a mediator and diplomat. He was still in this position when the first Elementals arrived in 2020. He immediately declared them a threat, and lobbied for the Elemental Integration Initiative. Unfortunately for him, it was not implemented in the way he liked— it was friendly and welcoming. He spent the next 13 years attempting to control the agency. When he succeeded, he renamed it to the Elemental Research Initiative, turning it hostile.

While Tulia knew little about their Earthling counterparts, some dared to claim the title- Terkou being one of them. Uniquely, he was the only one to preach complete elemental supremacy to these creatures. He eventually went to Earth, before Irision had even risen, in attempts to study humanity further. His time there, hidden by the Europa Government as a Tulian Representative, further solidified his ideas. He eventually fled, taking refuge in Antarctica, where he continued to gather studies. When Dal yon Tulia broke out, bringing upon the first refugees, he was ecstatic. He secretly hoped for an Elemental conquest of the planet, colonizing it as a second Tulia. All of his ideas came to an absolute peak when Imbra declared her crusade. He began to support her and Adva, even giving Adva a weapon …

## ⦿ Endzone (Genderless) - Android - Major Character
Endzone, an experimental android built for war on Earth, is a lost, wandering android residing in the sahara desert. The only one of its kind, it was built for espionage, specifically assassinations, having abilities to twist, turn, and contort itself in ways that makes it elite for close-quarters hand-to-hand combat. It also has multiple weapons, including a dagger, sniper, and poisons, all of which are now broken and somewhat dysfunctional. Despite its purpose, Endzone is known to hardly be violent at all, resorting to it only in extreme cases. 

⦾ Birthday: Year 2001, Month January, Day 17
## ⦿ Wykoi Maita (Male) - Inca-Born - Major Character
Wykoi dedicated his life early on to the research of Elemental life and customs. He’s spent most of his life doing so, finding much pride and joy in his studies, being the loudest human to be for co-existence with Elementals seeking refuge, publicly debating those who disagree with him, including Samae. His knowledge on the species got him a job in the ADS’ Elemental Integration Initiative, where he’d assist in making spaces comfortable and fit for Elementals residing on the planet. Unfortunately, when Troy takes over and rebrands the EII to the Elemental Research Initiative, he loses his job, now fighting from the sidelines to keep peace between the species. His efforts were in vain, as the Elemental Crusade broke out, brutal and swift. Thanks to his connections, however, he is one of the few humans on the “Do Not Harm” list for Elemental Factions. 

⦾ Birthday: Year 207, Month December, Day 2
