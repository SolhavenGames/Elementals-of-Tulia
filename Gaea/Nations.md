# State of Japan (North Japan)
## General
Capital - Tokyo (Northern Half)

Continent - Asia 

Region - East Asia

Ideology - Presidential Technocrstic Democracy

# Politics
## Political Parties
[自由州] The Free State (FS) - Single Issue; Personal Freedoms | The FS party only votes to further personal freedoms, and always abstains on anything else. It's often the party most apolitical voters vote for (voting is mandatory), so it doesn't campaign often. It has minimal popularity in the state, making up small percentages of the vote and executive after each election cycle. 

[社会科学] The Social-Science Party (SSP) - Social Tehcnocrats, Mild Authoritarian, Anti-Capitalist, Pro Reunification, Pro War, Internationalst/Globalist | The SSP is the furthest left legal party that North Japan has. This leads to most left-wing voters further left than their Ideology voting for them, despite not having a home in the party. This leads to it often being the second most popular party in the country, which leads to many technocratic laws defining the state as a whole.
