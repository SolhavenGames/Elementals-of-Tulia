# Wykoi Maita | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Peace was an option. You just didn't want to choose it._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A very loud pro-co existance voice on Earth/Gaea fighting to prevent open conflict between the two species. 

**Ending State**: A human highly protected by Elementals, still fighting to end the Burning of Gaea with peace and co existance.

**Inciting Losses**: Losing his father, but having Elementals be the main ones there for him.

**Irreversable Actions**: Many believe he chooses Elementals over Humanity due to him living within their borders. 

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the human who's pro-elemental, showing that Elemental mercy exists, it's just very selective.
## Summary 
Wykoi was considered an "Elemental Specialist" due to his long term studies and ties with the species, even before the mass refugee crisis. He was a credible source for things like Elemental Anatomy, culture, language, and even behaviors. This pushed the ADS to choose him to be the leader of their Elemental Integration Initiate. When he refused, they instead offered him a high ranking role in productions of Elemental-Friendly spaces, which he happily accepted. There, he got to speak with many refugees, plan and plot many places for Elemental refuge on Gaea, and assist with integration and translation. However, when Troy Talona took over, he was fired for not aligning with Troy's views. Even still, he served as a voice for Elementals everywhere- until the Burning of Gaea began. His home city was attacked, and when an Elemental came face-to-face with him during it, he thought he would be killed. Instead, the Elemental seemed almost joyous to have met him- as if meeting a celebrity. The Elemental called over his friends and allies to meet Wykoi, all of who praised him as well. Confused, Wykoi played along, and the Elementals eventually offered him refuge and protection, which he felt obligated to accept. Now, he lives in an undisclosed location, still preaching for peace between the two species on Earth/Gaea.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **E-Year 2010**
- Wykoi has been studying Elementals for many years after growing fascinated with them, his single father funding his research.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2013**
- Wykoi meets Kterla | The two get along incredibly well at an award ceremony. They knew of each other, but had never met.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2015**
- Wykoi is considered one of the few Elemental Specialists on the planet, being employed part-time by the EII.
- With Kterla, Wykoi forms the Strategists of Peace- an organization dedicated to spreading peace and stability across the planet.
- Him and Kterla win 5 peace prizes, 2 of which going to the Strategists of Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- Wykoi becomes fluent in the Elemental language Firen, taught by Kterla.
- Wykoi has personally won 20 peace prizes, some of which he dedicates to his mother who passed during his birth.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2021**
- Wykoi's father is diagnosed with a heart disease, causing Wykoi to slow down to spend more time with his dad.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2027**
- When Wykoi's father dies of heart complications, Elementals are the first ones to comfort him. They shower him in gifts and attend his father's funeral, even holding an Elemental funeral for him.
- Wykoi becomes a very public co-existance speaker, citing his own experiences with the species as a reason they can be trusted.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Wykoi meets Samae | The two have a heated debate on Elemental Co-existance, which gets lots of viewership online.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2029**
- Wykoi commends Kterla for her work with the Strategists for Peace, and the two become public figures of Human-Elemental companionship.
- Wykoi becomes a full time employee at the EII, using the Strategists for Peace to assist in Elemental Integration.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- Wykoi fights to allow Elementals into human schools, though he fails.
- He hears of Troy Talona, and gets worried. Though he doesn't say anything, not wishing to spread any fear.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2032**
- Wykoi warns people in the EII that Troy will cause bad things, but he is ignored.
- He doesn't tell Kterla, fearing she may speak out publicly and spread public fear.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- The attack on Wuhan-Nanjing breaks his heart, and the Strategists for Peace are sent for humanitarian aid- though rejected, as Kterla is an Elemental.
- The Strategists for Peace lose membership during the Burning of Gaea as trust for Elementals sinks drastically.
- Kterla and Wykoi are given shelter and protection in an Elemental faction, though they do not agree with the factions war tendencies. 
### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Wykoi is on the ground during NYSC, assisting directly in evacutations of citizens with the Strategists for Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Wykoi critizices Troy for radicalizing Elementals to attack Humanity. Many being to call Wykoi a traitor to Humanity, as Troy holds the trust of the public.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Wykoi continues to fight for peace and coexistance, despite the dire situation.
