# Is’ale Minsi | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Freedom for my people, at any cost._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A staunch anti-Knightsdale protester in Rutt (Naturine Nation). 

**Ending State**: Joining Terkou to make Earth/Gaea the perfect Elemental Pluralist Democracy.

**Inciting Losses**: Taking a major injury to his abdomen to save many of his fellow fighters. This causes him to be honorably discharged, but also increases his val bank substantially.

**Irreversable Actions**: Becoming as outspoken as he was caused him to be a target for Knightsdales later.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Giving insight to fighters in Dal yon Tulia.
## Summary 
Is'ale is a strong believer in the democratic processes of Tulia. When the Knightsdale movements first began, he quit his job and began a campaign in his nation to subdue any such movement before it could even begin. This worked beautifully, causing little to no Knightsdale action in Rutt, the nation of the Naturine. Unfortunately for him, neighboring nations like New Iris and Wor, changing their names to Irision and Wrenao respectively, now seeing him as a major target for removing a potential ally. His pleas to the Rutt Government to intervene in International Knightsdale Movements were quickly ignored, though videos of them grew rapidly popular around the free world. When Dal yon Tulia broke out, he immediately enrolled in the military, fighting on the frontlines as a brave soldier to hold back the evils of Knightsdale from taking his home. Being so popular and on the front lines was a downside, though, as many of his enemies would aim for him. This worked one day, brutally wounding him and causing him to be honorably discharged by Rutt. The Knightsdales were not done with him, though. They began sending assassins after him, seeing his mere existance as an insult. Due to this, Rutt began plotting to send him to Earth/Gaea for safety. This plan eventually succeeded, where Is'ale initially cooperated with the authority. When Earth/Gaea grew hostile, he went his own ways- and eventually ran into Terkou, who began preaching his ideology to him, wrapping him slowly into his ideology.
