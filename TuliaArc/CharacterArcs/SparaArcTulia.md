# Spara Dei-Sor | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_We were betrayed by our own people. They want blood, not power._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A member of the Icilen (Icik Nation) military as a mid-ranking soldier known for her bravery and dedication.

**Ending State**: An exile, hiding from extremism and connection. 

**Inciting Losses**: After being ordered to slaughter a city of civilians, she refuses and defects from the army.

**Irreversable Actions**: Carrying out Knightsdale orders for multiple Tulia years.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Showing that participation in Knightsdale isn't always radicalism, it can be fear.
## Summary 
Spara Dei-Sor was a powerful and trusted soldier in the Icilen military. Once they fell to Knightsdale, she was forced to play along- following their orders of arresting dissenters and resistance groups as the government continued to threaten her life. When Dal yon Tulia broke out, she was sent to the frontlines multiple times, mainly in Bunnis, where she was praised for her decisive victories. However, when she was ordered to clear out a village of all civilians to steal and plunder their food and resources, she not only openly refused, but she killed the messenger and fled back to The Far South to hide in its snowy mountains. There, she reflected, building many regrets. She partially couldn't forgive herself for what she had done, and decided that she was no longer worthy of anyone's affection. She is later found by Snai soldiers who were sent looking for her, who she is forced to evade as she flees back to Bunnis, though she is a wanted criminal there. To defend herself, she seeks out Subzero, and is forced to use it when Phlanax soldiers attempt to arrest her. She finally flees even further, eventually joining Zezri's Underground Society. She stays isolated from others, keeping her distance emotionally. She does join them when they assault Tixolam's labs, and when they flee to Gaea, but she does not cooperate with Troy Talona. Instead, she leaves them, too, going to live in an icy island known as Greenland.
## Timeline
⦿ **T-Year 900**
- A mid ranking Icik soldier, passionate about defending her country, family, and people from any possible threat. Though, Tulia is in an extreme time of peace, so she and her peers mostly serve as symbols.
- The military is thrown into a minor alert when the Firen falls to Knightsdale, as the nation was a major ally to the nation.

⦿ **T-Year 901**
- The Poisi fall to Knightsdale, causing the nation to lose another ally.
- Spara and her division are called to the capital under fears of the Knightsdale ideology taking over their nations.

⦿ **T-Year 902**
- The Great Massacre causes her nation to fall to Knightsdale as well, as the elected officials are slaughtered by the army. Being such an important asset, the new government immediately begins to try and get Spara on their side.
- Spara resists secretly, but pretends to be on their side.
- She is sent to subdue the counter-coup in the neighboring nation, Swailen, a Knightsdale ally. She is forced to subdue protesters by force and fight her way into the capital through soldiers.
- Even after the coup is over, she remains to help keep the nation stable. She witnesses horrors committed upon Snai protesters that she could never forget.

⦿ **T-Year 905**
- Spara is finally called back from Swailen, but her spirit is partially broken. She feels bad for the people of Swailen, and commonly has sleepless nights over it.

⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia begins, and Spara is deployed to invade a nation north of hers across a sea named Bunnis.
- Once the invasion begins, she is commanded to lead a small group through the nation's countryside.

⦿ **T-Year 909**
- A messenger brings her a command which tells her to slaughter civilians. She immediately refuses, killing the messenger and defecting from the Icik Army.
- She hides out in The Far South, attempting to be off the grid and hidden from the government.
  
⦿ **T-Year 910**
- Spara learns to survive, living off the frozen land.
- She tries to repent to her wrongdoings by growing closer to her Element, Ice. Though, Ice mostly ignores her, as it's caught up in the Great God War.

⦿ **T-Year 912**
- Spara is found by Snai soldiers, and she's forced to fight her way out to flee to Bunnis, the Sakai nation.
- She hides out in the deserts of Bunnis, trying not to be noticed, as she is a wanted criminal there.

⦿ **T-Year 913**
- Fearing for her life, Spara unlocks Subzero, after pleading with Ice to give it to her. Ice does, more out of pity and hatred towards the other gods. It doesn't monitor her further.
- She builds a new shelter in the Bunnis deserts, but she stays on the move.

⦿ **T-Year 914**
- Spara has a close call with Delekt Soldiers searching for her.

⦿ **T-Year 916**
- Spara reaches Subzero Gate II after epxerimenting with her power and causing blizzards in the desert.
- Her weather affects are publicly declared as "val pollution" by scientists, an already well-known issue that causes other types of odd weather like blood rains, fire tornados, and incredibly fast winds.

⦿ **T-Year 918**
- Spara is finally caught, and she's forced to fight Phlanax soldiers to escape.
- She stumbles across Zezri's underground society, joining it as a Forbidden user. Her element is completely unique, and nobody as seen it before.

⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Despite her popularity within the society, she tries to keep away from others.
- Spara meets Zezri | Zezri was excited to meet Spara after hearing of her unique element, but Spara was very awkward and quiet. Zezri left her alone after a short talk.

⦿ **T-Year 921**
- Zezri leads an attack on Tixolam labs, freeing lab rats. This is the first time Spara fights for something she actually believes in.
- She personally frees a few victims of Tixolam's labs, their praise and thanks making her feel worth people's time for once.
- The feeling is short-lived, as she begins to remember her past actions again.
- Zezri, after having built a plan to flee, leads her underground society to Earth/Gaea.

⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- When the group arrives on Earth/Gaea, Spara splits after Zezri opts to cooperate with Troy.
- She finds her way to Greenland, where she lives today, trying to forget her past and live a peaceful, quiet life.
