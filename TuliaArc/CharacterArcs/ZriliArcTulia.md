# Zrili Qonn | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_This isn't the liberation I wanted._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Zrilli was more of a statistic; just another employee in the Stalto power generators. Only their family knew their name. 

**Ending State**: In captivity with Troy Talona, preparing to escape and re-unite with Zezri.

**Inciting Losses**: Awakening their val causing their life to fall apart as Din slowly replaced their element over 6 Tulia days. 

**Irreversable Actions**: They took their first life in the battles against Tixolam, causing them to view others slightly differently from this point on.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Showing how it is to be a follower of Zezri.
## Summary 
Zrili was a nobody for their entire early life, choosing to make very few friends and rarely existing outside of their inner circle. Due to their larger val bank, their country, Stalto, had been preparing them to join the work force for its economy's main drivers: its massive power banks. Even during Dal yon Tulia, Stalto kept supplying power to both sides. Zrili was there, working early in the mornings and late at nights. One day, they were offered a promotion: a lot more pay, a lot less work. But it required them to awaken their val. Their family encouraged them to take it, but Zrili cared little. They wanted a peaceful, quiet life for once. Once Zrili's val was awakened, though, their element dissapeared. Confused, dazed, and upset, their life began to fall apart as their workplace dropped them, their family ridiculed them, and they ran from their home to the neighboring mountains of Delekt. There, they finally got their peace... for about two days, then Din arrived. Every sound tormented them. They eventually accepted that peace was now long gone, and found Zezri's underground society, quickly joining and becoming close friends with Zezri herself. Together, they'd lead an assault on Tixolam, liberating many more forbidden users/victims, and then fleeing the planet to Gaea, where they cooperated with Troy Talona, a major mistake that led to Zrili's capture. Zrili would not surrender- and their escape plans were already being drawn.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Zrili has been being trained to work in the Stalto power banks for multiple years of their life, despite never truly wanting to work in them.
- Their family ignores their cries, continuing to force them to train for it.
### ⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia hardly affects them. Their nation does not fight in the conflict, and they don't consider themselves politically active.
- Eventually, Zrili is finally hired to one, but in a minor role. It doesn't require them to awaken their val.
- Just as they suspected, they truly hate the job. It's loud, tedious, and fast, opposite to the quiet and slow life they wanted.
### ⦿ **T-Year 909**
- Zrili's power and excess val makes them very useful to Stalto. They rise through low ranking positions quickly, but still are not required to unlock their val.
### ⦿ **T-Year 911**
- Their time comes: A promotion that requires them to unlock their val.
- Zrili's family is happy- but they are not. They still hate their job, but take the promotion for their family.
- Zrili finally unlocks their val, but their element disappears.
- Their family ridiculed them when their job dropped them, and they decided to use this time to flee to Delekt and finally live a peaceful, quiet life.
- Without knowing it, Zrili enters Din Gate I.
- Every small noise irritates them as their ears seem to pick up every little sound.
### ⦿ **T-Year 912**
- Zrili suffers with their newfound hearing abilities, their quiet life being destroyed.
- They eventually are found by Delekt soldiers, who give them food and agree to keep her existance a secret. 
### ⦿ **T-Year 914**
- Zrili finally decides to leave after her trust with the soldiers slowly waivers.
- They find their way to Tixolam, weaving through the rural part of the country to reach the jungle.
- They live off the land, slowly losing their mind as the sounds of animals ring through their head constantly.
### ⦿ **T-Year 915**
- Zrili gets their first full nights rest since they've wielded Din.
### ⦿ **T-Year 916**
- Zrili hears whispers of nearby underground societies for "people like them". They begin searching for it.
### ⦿ **T-Year 918**
- Zrili finally finds the underground society. They do not join directly, but sthey remain adjacent to where they camp.
- Residents of the society notice them, frequently bringing them supplies to survive.
- Zrili wants to join, but is unsure. The residents bringing them food encourage them to weigh their options, but reminds them that they are always welcomed.
### ⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Zrili finally decides to join.
- Zrili meets Zezri | They caught Zezri off guard, but the two click quickly. Zezri is the first person in years that Zrili considers a friend.
### ⦿ **T-Year 920**
- Zrili assists in the capture and trapping of Zero.
### ⦿ **T-Year 921**
- Zrili participates in the attack on Tixolam, using their new powers aggressively for the first time- but mainly using them to distract and disorient guards. This battle helps them reach Din Gate II.
- After this battle, Zezri asks them for help to draft a plan to flee the planet. Zrili does- but under time crunch, it ends up being slightly rushed.
- The rushed plan cause many to die in transit.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- The society arrives on Earth/Gaea.
- Despite Zrili's distrust and warnings, Zezri still cooperates with the Gaean Institutions.
- This causes many to be killed and imprisoned, with Zezri themselves getting imprisoned. They now prepare a plan to escape, freeing who remains alive with them.
