# Zrili Qonn | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_This isn't the liberation I wanted._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Zrilli was more of a statistic; just another employee in the Stalto power generators. Only their family knew their name. 

**Ending State**: In captivity with Troy Talona, preparing to escape and re-unite with Zezri.

**Inciting Losses**: Awakening their val causing their life to fall apart as Din slowly replaced their element over 6 Tulia days. 

**Irreversable Actions**: They took their first life in the battles against Tixolam, causing them to view others slightly differently from this point on.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Showing how it is to be a follower of Zezri.
## Summary 
Zrili was a nobody for their entire early life, choosing to make very few friends and rarely existing outside of their inner circle. Due to their larger val bank, their country, Stalto, had been preparing them to join the work force for its economy's main drivers: its massive power banks. Even during Dal yon Tulia, Stalto kept supplying power to both sides. Zrili was there, working early in the mornings and late at nights. One day, they were offered a promotion: a lot more pay, a lot less work. But it required them to awaken their val. Their family encouraged them to take it, but Zrili cared little. They wanted a peaceful, quiet life for once. Once Zrili's val was awakened, though, their element dissapeared. Confused, dazed, and upset, their life began to fall apart as their workplace dropped them, their family ridiculed them, and they ran from their home to the neighboring mountains of Delekt. There, they finally got their peace... for about two days, then Din arrived. Every sound tormented them. They eventually accepted that peace was now long gone, and found Zezri's underground society, quickly joining and becoming close friends with Zezri herself. Together, they'd lead an assault on Tixolam, liberating many more forbidden users/victims, and then fleeing the planet to Gaea, where they cooperated with Troy Talona, a major mistake that led to Zrili's capture. Zrili would not surrender- and their escape plans were already being drawn.
