# Kael Voris | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_"Only Death can liberate me from this cruel existence."_** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Kael and his family are heavily involved in politics online, while he is in school aiming to become the leader of Calen.

**Ending State**: Kael is deep into the gates of Death and in captivity of the ERI and Troy Talona. 

**Inciting Losses**: He begins to forget his favorite memories with his family and Imbra, causing him to slowly see his life as pointless.

**Irreversable Actions**: His attempt to defend Imbra from Asli during their battle directly leads to his capture. 

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the final straw for Imbra to begin her crusade.
## Summary 
Kael Voris witnessed his entire family and friends slaughtered by his state (once it fell to Knightsdale) for their continuing beliefs in the Tulian Democratic Processes. Due to this, Kael flees, jumping from nation to nation, surviving on scraps and charity. He's eventually recruited by Adva and Imbra, who bring him to Earth/Gaea with them, where they are mistreated, watched, and attacked. He falls in love with Imbra, whom he nearly gives his life for. Instead of killing him, the Knights sent to capture Imbra take him instead after declaring Imbra too volatile to transport from Gaea to Tulia, where he's used as a test subject in Tixolam for the Forbidden Element Death. He rapidly flies through the early gates, and eventually is freed by Zezri, who fails to free him but vows to keep him alive. With her, he returns to Earth, and is captured by Troy Talona and the Elemental Research Initiative once Zezri makes the blunder of cooperating with them.
## Timeline
⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Kael is enrolled in school, studying political sciences, law, and economics, with aspirations to become the leader of his nation.
- When the Firen nation falls to Knightsdales, his classes teach it as if it's an old historical event, slightly desynsitizing the students.

⦿ **T-Year 902**
- The Great Massacre leads to his nation being taken over by Knightsdales, and higher education- especially political students- are targetted.
- Kael is forced to return home, and is silently declared a person of interest by the government.
- A coup in his nation succeeds, but it's quickly put down by the neighboring nation, Fraklen.
- The coup does leave windows for political unrest and dissent as law enforcement agencies temporarily slip away from government control. 

⦿ **T-Year 903**
- Kael's family gets incredibly politically active, beginning to push back against the Knightsdale. Kael is encouraged to stay home, as his family fears for him due to his political past.

⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, and some of Kael's family and friends are drafted.
  
⦿ **T-Year 909**
- Icik soldiers begin to enter Kael's nation to restore order and power to the government.
- Kael's city becomes a direct hold out against Knightsdale in the Snai nation.

⦿ **T-Year 910**
- Icik soldiers invade Kael's house and slaughter his family. They leave him alive due to him not being present at protests & his still underlying POI status. They leave the bodies there.
- Kael, having nowhere else to go, is forced to stay in the house.
- Him and his former classmates form a connected circle together, aiming to keep each other alive through obedience to the state, while not supporting the ideology.

⦿ **T-Year 911**
- Some of Kael's friends are killed when they try to resist being drafted.
- An Icik soldier kills a child right in front of its mother while Kael watches from a distance.

⦿ **T-Year 912**
- A coastal invasion by Watrish and Naturine soldiers into Kael's leads to him and his group members being captured, as they are mistaken for Knightsdale Youth.
- They are taken to Rutt, and held as prisoners of war.

⦿ **T-Year 915**
- Most of his friends end up dying from the poor conditions and failed escape attempts.
- Right after this, laws are passed to force better conditions in the detention centers, as their deaths sparked local outrage.

⦿ **T-Year 916**
- Kael escapes the prison, roaming around with only one remaining friend, hoping to survive.

⦿ **T-Year 917**
- Kael's last remaining friend is killed by a Peacekeeper when they believe he was attempting to sneak attack them.
- Kael wanders alone, mainly sticking with larger groups to not feel suspicious. Though, he rarely speaks, not willing to reach out to others.

⦿ **T-Year 918 (PT 1)**
- Kael ends up in a large group for refugees planning to flee the planet.
- Kael meets Imbra | Imbra attempts to comfort him after seeing him distressed, though it doesn't work too well.
- Kael meets Adva | Adva is skeptical of him, yet she tolerates him because Imbra does.
- He is recruited by the two after they narrowly survive an attack on the camp.
- They eventually flee the planet, beginning a 90 day journey to Earth/Gaea.

⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- The trio arrive to Earth/Gaea. Refugees are treated poorly, but they endure.
- They are relocated to a better living space with an assigned human who treats them with respect.
- Kael and Imbra kiss, growing romantically close. Though Imbra flees due to fearing such an intimate relationship- but Kael doesn't know the reason.
- Kael's val is awakened, and he passes out due to his high val bank.
- Kael is injured by human soldiers when they are attacked by Humans, who also happen to kill their assigned human.
- Knights sent to take Imbra back to Tulia end up taking Kael instead, bringing him back to the planet.

⦿ **T-Year 918 (PT 2)**
- Kael is presented to the leader of Irision, Knison Iris. She eventually decides to use him for barganing.
- Kael is sent to Tixolam, placed in a secret underground detention center used for lab rats. Though, he has no idea where he is- guards are made up of all kinds of Coven nation elements.
- The first few times he's experimented on, nothing seems to happen. They don't properly heal his wounds, leading to small holes and cuts on his skin.

⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Kael is finally infected with Death, pushing him into Death Gate I. He is the first successful case, and many scientists avoid him due to this, leaing to him being nearly starved- down to one meal a day.
- The illness from Death Gate I begins to ruin Kael's mood- worse than it already was being a prisoner, of course.
- Kael is relocated to a more isolated sector of the holding cells, where only other Death victims reside. There is currently only him and 3 others, though they are kept away from each other. Kael still speaks to one through the wall.

⦿ **T-Year 920**
- Kael is chosen as the main test subject for Death, which starts his rapid expansion through the Gates.
- He reaches Gate II when Tixolam Scientists begin to pump his body with unstable (NOT Forbidden, just unstable) val.
- This gate worsens his sickness. It's only here he realizes this isn't a normal illness, and the scientists have done something to him.
- He reaches Gate III when Tixolam Scientists begin torturing and mistreating him. The scientists pinpoint that what progresses gates is the user's will to live, so they continue this treatment.
- Kael feels terrible due to the affects of Gate III, but Tixolam Scientists still force him to walk and follow orders. If he doesn't, they will brutally beat him.
- He reaches Gate IV when Tixolam Scientists force him into hard labor with few breaks and little food, alongside more torture, berating, and unstable val.
- Memories begin to slip, and he begins slowly forgetting small memories with Elementals like his friends, family, and Imbra & Adva.

⦿ **T-Year 921**
- Kael and the other prisoners are freed by Zezri's underground society when they launch an assault on Tixolam's labs to free the remaining subjects.
- Kael meets Zezri | Zezri approaches Kael, promising to free him from the plight as she refuses to let him die.
- Unfortunately, Zezri's initial attempts fail, and Kael reaches Death Gate V.
- This allows him to spread a weaker form of the Death Forbidden Element, but Zezri's society is mostly immune due to having their own. However, citizens are not, causing a minor pandemic in the area.
- Zezri hides Kael away, but is sure to feed, visit, and keep him company so he doesn't go crazy or die.
- Kael loses more memories, remembering that he kissed Imbra, but not remembering that it was her. He also forgets most of his friends and family, and even forgets Zezri for a moment. 
- Zezri's society begins plots to flee the planet.
- They eventually leave towards the end of the year. Zezri keeps Kael close.

⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Zezri and her society arrive on Earth/Gaea. She encourages her society to cooperate with the local authority.
- Unfortunately, this leads to most of them being imprisoned and killed. Kael is imprisoned, but his element spreads to hundreds of humans, causing a small pandemic.
- Kael is isolated from everyone else, before Troy learns of Kael's importance.
- Kael meets Troy | Kael was still in the systems of Earth/Gaea, and Troy realizes that he has connections to Phoenix (AKA Imbra). He decides to use him for leverage.

⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Kael is still locked away, but Troy's plan for him is being drawn out. Kael either does not care, or lacks the capacity to care. He never smiles, never laughs, and hardly talks.
