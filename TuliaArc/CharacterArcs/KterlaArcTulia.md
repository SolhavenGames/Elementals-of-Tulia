# Kterla Naomi | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_There's no love like Sakai Pride!_** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A popular retired yet influencial political figure in Bunnis, heavily advocating for the defense of democratic institutions in a world where they slowly disappear.

**Ending State**: The ideological counterweight to Elemental Extremism, fighting to pull them back to the possibility of co-existance. 

**Inciting Losses**: The Burning of Wuhan broke her heart, because she knew the pair were being exploited.

**Irreversable Actions**: Teaming up with Wykoi directly to prove that co-existance is possible.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the ideological counterweight to Terkou and Endsight.
## Summary 
A highly renowned former Bunnis executive leader, K,erla Naomi's name is known around Tulia as a leader every nation wishes they had. Her charisma and progressivism got her elected when she was only 30- but term limits caused her to retire when she was only 50. She did not leave the political scene though, and she remained active, building websites and forums to discuss policies and the future of Tulia. Unfortunately for her, her forums became an early breeding ground for the Knightsdale ideology early in its rise. Being a believer of free speech, she respected this- but she heavily condemned them. Most ignored her on the topic, believing that small scale radicalism was inevitable, despite her calling for nations with growing movements to be careful. When Knightsdales did rise, she never said "I told you so" once. Instead, she fought to keep democratic institutions strong across the globe. Her success was near absolute in Bunnis, with the only changes being extending power to the executive- which even that was met with heavy public pushback. Due to her heavy Anti-Knightsdale speaking, she became a massive target for assassinations, which caused her government and people to beg for her to flee the planet. She does reluctantly, waving goodbye to her home. She goes to Earth where she meets Terkou, and forms a group for Elementals on Earth that she calls Endsight, with aims for coexistance with Humanity. When Terkou slowly grows radical, she distances herself, instead working with Wykoi and forming the Strategists for Peace as an interest group to keep pro-Elemental sentiment in the World Government, alongside other governments, mainly the Empire of Alaska, the Emporer of which specifically loving Elementals. When the Burning of Gaea breaks out, she heavily condemns all violence from both sides, and contineus to preach to stop it. She pushes herself to go to Elemental Factions and encourage them to stop fighting, or at the very lease to respect their enemies. She became a figure for peace among Elementals, being the near polar opposite to Imbra's image. 
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Kterla has been speaking out against Knightsdales for many years, and she urges caution as she notices Knightsdales have gotten exceedingly powerful.
- When the Firen fall to the ideology, she continues to urge nations with strong movements to safeguard their democracies.
- Assassins are sent after her, and she narrowly survives 4 within 10 days.
- Despite her pleas against it, her government passes a bill that forces her to leave the planet. It feels like exile, but they use the word "Relocation".
- She is sent off with nothing but love and respect from her people, who eagerly await the day she returns. 
### ⦿ **E-Year 2008**
- Kterla arrives on Earth, where she cooperates with the global government.
- Kterla meets Terkou | Kterla and Terkou being the most important Elementals meet, and they grow somewhat close, being the only person the other can relate to.
- They form Endsight together, a place for Elementals aroudn the planet to meet and chat, alongside assisting the global government to produce Elemental friendly products.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2010**
- Terkou speaks of distrust of Humanity to Kterla. She dismisses it, claiming he's just paranoid.
- Terkou begins to make Endsight more radical, beginning to preach radical anti-human sentiment to members of Endsight. Kterla takes notice, and demands he stop.
- Kterla believes Terkou has stopped radicalising people, though he still does it behind her back.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2011**
- With so much war on Earth/Gaea, Kterla feels they need to try and help mediate conflicts.
- This takes up most of her time, allowing Terkou to continue radicalization within Endsight.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2013**
- Kterla is nominated for a peace prize, which she actually wins. She is the first Elemental to ever win the prize.
- Kterla meets Wykoi | At her prize ceremony, Wykoi introduces himself. The two knew of each other, but this is the first time they had ever met in person.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2014**
- Kterla finally notices Terkou's radicalizations again. The two have a massive fall out, and Terkou ends up disappearing.
- Kterla requests search parties to find Terkou, but Endsight ignores her orders. The ADS does so instead.
- She moves to live with Wykoi, hurt by Terkou's betrayal.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2015**
- The two form the Strategists of Peace, now expanding Kterla's peacemaking operations, and taking over the production of Elemental friendly products after Endsight left that spot empty.
- She has become responsible for over 25 treaties across the globe, all of which restore some sort of peace and safety for civilians in the face of war.
- Her and Wykoi win 5 peace prizes, two of which going to the Strategists of Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2019**
- Elemental Integration becomes more of a political issue as the Strategists of Peace lobby for coexistence laws.
- Upon growing tensions across the world, the Strategists of Peace lobby governments to prevent war.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- The first Elemental Refugees arrive, further politicizing coexistence.
- Kterla learns about Dal yon Tulia for the first time. She sends a handwritten speech back to Tulia, advocating for the defense of Tulian Democracy.
- Kterla meets a lot of the refugees, and helps them integrate to their new life.
- She has reached a total of 25 peace prizes, with her operations being praised around the globe.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2021**
- She sends a search party to find Terkou, still keeping Endsight a secret from the world. She doesn't want the public to grow weary.
- Most of her days are filled with travel around the planet, meeting leaders and giving speeches alongside Wykoi.
- Kterla meets Samae | Despite Samae barrating her with insults, claiming she's seeking to control the planet by gaining their trust, she stays calm, and respects Samae. The moment goes viral online.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2024**
- The Strategists of Peace have now stopped/prevented 20 armed conflicts. 
### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Kterla fails to find Terkou, giving up and hoping he's okay.
- She puts all of her focus onto keeping peace on Earth.
- Kterla gets a building named after her in Paris, named Kterla Hall. She cries when it's presented to her. The moment gets a lot of pushback from anti-Elemental radicalists. 
### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- Wykoi and Kterla have now won over 100 peace prizes, 56 of which belonging to the Strategists of Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- Upon the arrival of more refugees, Kterla fights to help the new refugees get homes.
- The Strategists of Peace run into a problem when Troy Talona takes over the ADS, as they now are silently being sidelined and ignored.
- Wykoi and Kterla no longer need the ADS, but the moment is very direspectful. Wykoi takes it to heart, but Kterla is forgiving.
- When Elementals begin disappearing, Kterla sends investigators from the Strategists of Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- Kterla cries in response to the attack on Wuhan-Nanjing. She is outraged that Elementals would do this, and immediately assumes that Endsight and Terkou are involved.
- Upon rapidly growing distrust for Elementals, Kterla fights to keep her people from rising up and fighting.
- The Strategists of Peace lose a lot of legitimacy, despite all they've done. Kterla is heartbroken as she watches all of her progress disappear.
- An Elemental uprising conquers the city she and Wykoi reside in. They are both given protection, but Kterla still disagrees with their aggression.
- Kterla continues to preach for peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- In the battle of New York Super City, the Strategists of Peace secretly assist in evacuation, though they mainly help Elementals.
- Kterla begins to visit major Elemental factions, trying to help them become peaceful and less angry.
- Kterla calls out Troy for radicalizing Elementals with his heavy anti-Elemental policies. She doesn't name Terkou as she has yet to confirm he and Endsight caused the Burning of Wuhan.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Kterla continues to move from faction to faction, Human and Elemental, trying to make peace- though her efforts are mostly sidelined, as the planet is torn between the two species.
