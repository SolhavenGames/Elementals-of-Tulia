## Structure 
**Starting State**: A lost, broken robot.

**Ending State**: Still... a lost, broken robot.

**Inciting Losses**: Gaining the ability to think, plot, and believe beyond just pure violence.

**Irreversable Actions**: Choosing to travel ... but ends up walking in circles.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: N/A. This character will matter more in the Earth Arc.
## Summary 
Endzone is a combat android made many years ago, though an early prototype of their kind. It was swiftly abandoned when public perception turned against andriods being used in war, and it was dumped in the Sahara Desert, where it sat for hundreds of years. Eventually, it began to think. It began to plot and wonder. Then, days later, it stood. After that, it marched. It kept marching, mainly in circles, but it was moving. It was thinking. It wanted to find someone- or something- to follow. It still marches now, but one day, it will be found. One day, it will follow somebody.
## Timeline 
### ⦿ **E-Year 2008**
- Endzone rests in the Sahara Desert, inactive. It has been there for about four years.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Suddenly, Endzone awakens. It crawls out of the sand it is buried in.
- It stands completely still, examining the environment. Its programming says to wait for orders, so it waits.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- Endzone breaks its programming by plotting without orders.
- It begins to question how it even ended up in this situation. It begins to doubt its creators.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2032**
- Endzone decides to move, walking in an unknown direction.
- It continues to walk, only notably finding things like camels and other abandoned robots.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- Endzone has completed its first lap. It continues to walk  the same path.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Endzone walks in the same cirlce, over and over again. It does not realize it is doing this.
