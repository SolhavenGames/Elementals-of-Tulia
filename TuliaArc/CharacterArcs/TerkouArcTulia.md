# Terkou "The One" Lynqo | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_I'm an architect in the shadows. History won't remember me, it'll remember my actions._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A "Human Specialist" living on one of the 3 moons of Tulia.

**Ending State**: An ideological mastermind puppeteering powerful and emotionally vulnerable Forbidden Element Users.

**Inciting Losses**: Honestly? He's always been like this. He had to lose nothing to declare Earth/Gaea rightful Elemental land. 

**Irreversable Actions**: Pointing Imbra and Adva to massive cities, indirectly causing major damage.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the architect of Elemental Evils. 
## Summary 
Terkou Lynqo has always been called a Human Specialist by his Elemental counterparts, despite knowing little about the species. When he eventually left Tulia's moon to Earth/Gaea, he originally cooperated with the government of the planet, meeting with an Elemental named Kterla to become a leader figure among Elementals on Earth/Gaea, forming Endsight. The two became a major source for humanity to understand the species- until he and Kterla began disagreeing on the future of Endsight. This caused him to disappear without a trace, retreating to live in Antarctica. There, he began his shadow operations, teaming up with other Elementals who aimed to conquer Earth/Gaea from the inside (Namely: Xor.) Their movements were slow and calculated, working alongside Human institutions to get to other elementals on the planet. While this radicalism did not work most of the time, few nodded along. It was when Xor was killed by Adva and Imbra's Hellfire showed that Terkou saw true potential. He began to manipulate the two, leading to the crusade that began the Burning of Gaea. When Adva and Imbra abandoned him after discovering that he was using them, he silently began to command and plot for other Elemental factions- Until he ran into a Teira. A lost soul, emotional and broken just like Imbra and Adva were. Then came I'sale, then Aeri, and finally, Zero- his perfect team. Four Forbidden Users, working under him loyally, following his cause. Under him, Endsight became more powerful than it ever had been. 
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Terkou lives on Kinnsi, studying Earth/Gaea from a distance.
- He eventually builds the funds to move to the planet, saying goodbye to his home to further understand the species that resides on Earth/Gaea.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2008**
- Terkou arrives on the planet, cooperating with the governments.
- He slowly grows to resent them, the institutionalism being far different to the laid back Moon Government he's grown used to.
- Terkou pretends that everything is fine, becoming a figure of Elementals among human governments.
- Terkou meets Kterla | Terkou, after being on the planet alone for so long, is estatic to meet another Elemental. They get along well, quickly growing close.
- They form Endsight together, a place for Elementals aroudn the planet to meet and chat, alongside assisting the global government to produce Elemental friendly products.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2009**
- Terkou and Kterla grow incredibly close, almost romantic- but both are always incredibly busy, making a romantic commitment difficult. They do spend their freetime together, and their mutual friends commonly poke fun at the idea.
- Terkou begins to think that Humanity is inferior, and imagines a future where Earth/Gaea is united under Elemental Pluralism.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2010**
- Terkou admits his distrusts of Humanity to Kterla. She seems worried, but dismisses his words as paranoia.
- When he begins to radicalize Endsight members, she demands he stop. He pretends he does, but keeps doing it behind her back.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2011**
- Terkou ramps up his radicalizations when Kterla goes off to assist in geopolitics on Earth/Gaea.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2013**
- Radical Endsight has multiple deep online forums hidden on hard-to-find networks.
- Terkou never shows his face, using his name as an alias online. Among Radical Endsight, be comes a leader figure, though he rarely interacts with the members.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2014**
- Kterla discovers Terkou's actions. Instead of taking responsibility, he simply flees to antarctica, leaving no trace.
- He quickly adapts to his new life, building small structures on the continent to create his own small colony.
- Due to being deep in the icy wasteland, he loses access to his online forums. However, he continues to connect to the world using higher ranking members of Endsight.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2016**
- Terkou learns of Kterla's new friendship with Wykoi. He feels betrayed that she'd work with humans over him.
- Endsight begins carrying out minor terrorist attacks, leaving little proof of Elemental involvement.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2018**
- Terkou and Endsight have a lot more influence than people realize, influencing governments to have pro-Elemental sentiment.
- He begins to think this is too slow, and plots out for quicker conquest of the planet. He begins to study Forbidden Elements.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- When the first wave of Elemental refugees arrive, Endsight sees a small spike in membership.
- He is unaware of this, but his name has become adjacent to godhood among low-ranking Endsight members.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2025**
- Endsight gets its first mention in years among the ERI by Troy Talona, who accuses the old institution of being a terrorist organization, alongside the Strategists for Peace.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2027**
- Terkou has fully lost track of low-ranking Endsight members, and now only has track of ~20-40 high ranking members.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Terkou trusts Endsight is being handled well, so doesn't work too hard to regain total control.
- Endsight, without his guidance, acts more as smaller splinter factions with the same goal.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- The second major wave of refugees has a lot more trauma striken Elementals, allowing Endsight to pull more into its wing.
- Terkou meets Imbra | He sees potential in her flames- and is exactly what he needs for his new idea. Burning the planet, causing total war, and conquering slowly.
- Terkou meets Adva | Adva's passion, pride, and confidence is perfect for him to manipulate. With Imbra, he sees an unstoppable duo.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- He helps Adva unlock Squall, and gives her a weapon- a metal staff that conducts her element too well.
- He trains the two in hand-to-hand combat after helping them further understand their Forbidden Element.
- Finally, he aims them to Wuhan-Nanjing, beginning the Burning of Gaea when they brutally burn and wreck the city. He chooses Wuhan-Nanjing due to its major influence on the global economy.
- When they return, he applauds and congratulates them, rewarding them with rest.
- Afterwards, he sends them out on solo missions to attack other cities, aiming Adva to coastal and trade cities, and Imbra to institutional and corporate cities.
- When they return from those missions, he notices their wavering belief. He does little about it, believing they will remain within Endsight.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- He aims the pair to New York Super City, choosing it due to its high Elemental population.
- When they return, they confront him. He is unapologetic.
- Adva attacks him, wounding him. They leave without killing him, attempting to prove they are not the monsters he tried to make them.
- Terkou meets Is'ale | Noticing how helpless Is'ale seemed, he decided to try and manipulate him by offering food and shelter.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Is'ale has fallen into Terkou's radicalism.
- Terkou learned his lesson- mold his soldiers before he sends them out immediately.
- Terkou meets Teira | Teira immediately falls into Terkou's speeches, clearly requiring some anchor.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Terkou meets Aeri | Terkou realizes that Aeri is connected to Teira, so he exploits that weakness to draw them in.
- Teira and Aeri are nearly entirely drawn into his words.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Terkou meets Zero | The lost soldier easily falls under Terkou's command, hardly resisting. Terkou immediately likes Zero.
- Building and training a new, strong team, Terkou is ready to unleash absolute hell onto the planet.
