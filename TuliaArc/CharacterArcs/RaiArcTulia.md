# Rai "Poseidon" Ronal | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_The beauty of the ocean is forever unmatched._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: An impoverished Watrish Elemental in a corrupt nation, his family barely making ends meet.

**Ending State**: Happy with himself, having helped two people pull away from evil.

**Inciting Losses**: His family giving up their money to help get him off the planet.

**Irreversable Actions**: Seeking Deluge behind Arete's back- despite her only wanting to help and protect him.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Helps to pull Imbra and Adva out of Terkou's grasp.
## Summary 
A young and impoverished Elemental in the corrupt Watrish nation, Rai found himself working odd jobs and gigs to help his family make even a little bit of money. His family often found themselves getting food from global charities and government assistance, and this caused him to have a very busy life. He attempted to get higher education, but his family quickly called him back when they struggled without his assistance. He met Arete when she visited to help families get a food surplus, and quickly became friends with her. When she left, he tried to pursue higher education from his home, stressing himself out. Dal yon Tulia complicated things, as people within higher education were prone to drafts. His family gives up their money to get him off the planet, and he travels with Arete to Earth/Gaea, where he lives in isolation in the Amazon Rainforest. When the Burning of Gaea begins, he feels obligated to meet Imbra and Adva to stop them from terrorizing the planet, even though he cares little for humanity. He mostly does it to prove that he could- and he does. Thanks to him, Imbra and Adva are pulled away from Terkou's grasp, and they now reside with him and Arete in the Amazon Rainforest.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Rai lives on the outskirts of Til with his family. He's working at a plant that mass-produces small nicnacs for children that uses legal loopholes and political connection to pay him an illegaly low wage.
- Being treated poorly by his managers, Rai opts to quit the job instead of dealing with the disrespect. He struggles to find jobs elsewhere.
- He decides to try and get higher education for a shot at better jobs. Unfortunately, his family calls him back only a month after he leaves to a university in the city, still needing his help.
- Rai meets Arete | Their two families had already known each other well, but when Rai and Arete meet, they immediately become great friends. They stay in contact, even when Arete leaves.

### ⦿ **T-Year 902**
- Rai finds a new job that was opened up at a media company. He now works to filter through data from Knightsdales, pinpointing what's real and what's fake.

### ⦿ **T-Year 904**
- Rai begins trying to juggle home-based higher education and his new job, which he has grown to love.
- His family begins participating in anti-Knightsdale protests.

### ⦿ **T-Year 905**
- The media company Rai works at predicts a major global war will start within the next 5 years.
- The media company is silently shut down by the state for spreading false news and fearmongering.
- Rai is put on unemployment by the government, as they were the cause of his job vanishing.

### ⦿ **T-Year 906**
- Rai gives all of his money to his family, focusing on his education.

### ⦿ **T-Year 907**
- His family finally tells him of their plan to get him to safety. He doesn't agree with it, but they insist. 

### ⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia begins, and Rai is immediately sent away with Arete. He is still unhappy with leaving his family, but Arete helps him feel better.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- The refugees arrive on Earth/Gaea.
- The Global Government beings working to give them all homes.
- Rai and Arete decide that they don't want to be marked by humanity, so they flee and wander around.
- Rai secretly believes Arete is too controlling and too worried about his safety.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2021**
- Rai begins to learn about Forbidden Elements using books of myths sold by other Elementals on the planet. Deluge intrigues him the most, and it becomes a fixation.
- He begins to collect books about Deluge and store them in his bags, hiding them from Arete.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2022**
- Rai and Arete eventually settle in the Amazon Rainforest, where they survive on the land together. They work with other nearby Elementals who had similar ideas to them, though the other Elementals migrate frequently.
- Rai hides his books in his private space, as Arete respects his secrecy.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2026**
- Rai and Arete begin to peek out into human society, visiting nearby cities. They admire humans and their culture, but still do not entirely trust them.
- After the short trip, they return to their camp. Arete declares that humans are "like elementals, but without the element" to Rai. He nods along, but has no idea what she means.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2029**
- Rai's fascination with Forbidden Elements seem to peak as he begins to branch out and study them all.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- The pair moves their camp after humans nearly discovered them.
- They toss around the idea of visiting Humans again.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- Rai begins trying to unlock Deluge, without telling Arete.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- The burning of Gaea encourages Arete to seek Forbidden Elements after Rai suggested them. She seeks Bastion, and Rai seeks Deluge- though he doesn't tell her that he's already been working towards it.
- Rai grows fascinated with Imbra and Adva as he recognizes the patterns of their elements to Forbidden Elements, but he fears what they could do. He decides to try and stop them, despite Arete begging him not to.
- Rai meets Imbra | Rai encourages Imbra to halt her attacks upon their first meeting. When he's ignored, he manages to subdue her over the ocean, causing her to retreat. He silently studies her Hellfire.
- He brags about this victory to Arete.
- Rai and Imbra battle again, though Rai seems to treat it more like a dance. Imbra doesn't seem to like this.
- Rai begins to grow feelings for Imbra.
- The two meet again, this time not fighting at all- just talking. Rai presents multiple questions to her, and they seem to work, but he is unsure.
- Rai meets Adva | Just like he does for Imbra, he encourages her to stop her attacks. Adva seems to listen, but her staunch stone-face makes it hard to tell.
- Rai decides to return to the Amazon, to stop stressing Arete out. He gives the pair directions to his camp before he leaves.
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- The Burning of New York Super City makes Rai think he failed.
- When Imbra and Adva arrive, upset and sad, he realizes he didn't- they were just stuck.
- Him and Imbra nearly kiss- but she pulls away at the last moment. He doesn't know why.
- Silently, Rai declares his own personal war against whoever turned Imbra and Adva into monsters. 
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- The Amazon Camp has become a small family home, the squad living together peacefully and silently. Adva took a moment longer to warm up, but once she did, the entire mood flipped.
- Rai is kept up at night by the moment with Imbra. Why did she pull away?
  
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Rai now has a new family- no longer worried about money, he feels happier, and he realizes his purpose.
