# Aeri Glem | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Perfection isn't easy... but I love challenges, anyway._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A heavily indoctrinated young soldier dedicated to dying for the Litti cause.

**Ending State**: Tied to a friend, Teira Kni-Soa, and her ideology, though somewhat aligning with it themselves.

**Inciting Losses**: Being abandoned inexplicably twice; once by their God (Light), and the other by Teira.

**Irreversable Actions**: Joining and listening to the preachings of Terkou, simply because Teira did first.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the final straw starting the Great God War.
## Summary 
Aeri Glem was a soldier before it was even legal to be one. Their entire life, they believed everything about the Litti Ideology: Superiority to all, equality to none. There was no purpose or question in their mind that anyone could live up to the perfection or beauty of the Light Element- until they met Teira. Teira Kni-Soa grew close to Aeri in a Poisi Refugee Camp, which caused them to get discharged and sentenced to "Godly Intervention", and they were banished to the conutryside. Here, their God gave them Warshine, and began to train them on a small, mountainous island. This intense training allows them to quickly fly through the gates, reaching Gate III, when they bump into Teira again, who has unlocked Dimension Gate I. Together, they reside, bonding over their forbidden powers. But one day, Light abandons them without a trace. No words, no notes, nothing. So together, they wander, searching for the god- until Teira abandons Aeri, too, leaving them confused and sad. They wander, being forced to dodge Coven and Phlanax law enforcement alike, before finally deciding to flee to Gaea, where they find Teira again. Teira excitedly introduces them to Terkou, who begins to indoctrinate the Warshine user, preparing them for a greater crusade than Imbra's.
## Timeline
⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Aeri serves as a soldier in the Sharlin military, though not fighting in any battles, serving more for state control, law enforcement, and stability.

⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, causing thousands from the neighboring nation of Tixolam to flee to Sharlin.
- Aeri is redeployed at a Poisi refugee camp. 

⦿ **T-Year 909**
- Aeri meets Teira | The pair immediately grows close, interested by each other. They tell stories and bond over trivial interests. This changes Aeri's interest on the Poisi people.

⦿ **T-Year 910**
- Aeri is discharged from the Litti Military after their friendly treatment of Teira made the state question their loyalty. Aeri is sent to the countryside, sentenced to "Divine Intervention".
- Aeri wanders, surviving off of the land. They eventually make it to a small, hilly and forested island, inhabited by nobody.

⦿ **T-Year 911**
- Found by their god, Light, the divine entity is intrigued by Aeri's will.
- Aeri is gifted Warshine by Light, who begins to speak to them, keeping them company.

⦿ **T-Year 914**
- Aeri reaches Warshine Gate II, with Light training them with the new power.
- Warshine begins to warp Aeri's memories, making them feel far more prideful. It also gives them gold streaks in their hair.

⦿ **T-Year 915**
- Aeri reaches Warshine Gate III, Light's training pushing them through the Forbidden Element quickly.

⦿ **T-Year 917**
- Aeri and Teira find each other again when Teira stumbles onto their island. Light goes quieter when Teira arrives.
- The two bond on the island, both being the only real interaction they've had in a long time.
  
⦿ **T-Year 918**
- Light completely abandons Aeri, with them having no clue as to why.
  
⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Teira abandons Aeri, too. They are now entirely alone.
- Aeri chooses to stay on the island, waiting for Light to return.
    
⦿ **T-Year 921**
- After being attacked by Coven soldiers, Aeri begins drafting plans to flee from Tulia to Earth/Gaea. 
  
⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Aeri arrives on the planet, wandering aimlesssly trying to remain off-the-grid.
- Teria finds Aeri again, who introduces them to Terkou.
- Aeri meets Terkou | Their first encounter, Aeri is slightly suspicious, but Teira lightens the mood. Aeri ends up listening to Terkou for hours.

⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Aeri begins listening to Terkou, mainly through Teira. They don't entirely agree with his outlook, but stay for Teira...
