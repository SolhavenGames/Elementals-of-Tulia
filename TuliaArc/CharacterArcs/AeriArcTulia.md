# Aeri Glem | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Perfection isn't easy... but I love challenges, anyway._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A heavily indoctrinated young soldier dedicated to dying for the Litti cause.

**Ending State**: Tied to a friend, Teira Kni-Soa, and her ideology, though somewhat aligning with it themselves.

**Inciting Losses**: Being abandoned inexplicably twice; once by their God (Light), and the other by Teira.

**Irreversable Actions**: Joining and listening to the preachings of Terkou, simply because Teira did first.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the final straw starting the Great God War.
## Summary 
Aeri Glem was a soldier before it was even legal to be one. Their entire life, they believed everything about the Litti Ideology: Superiority to all, equality to none. There was no purpose or question in their mind that anyone could live up to the perfection or beauty of the Light Element- until they met Teira. Teira Kni-Soa grew close to Aeri in a Poisi Refugee Camp, which caused them to get discharged and sentenced to "Godly Intervention", and they were banished to the conutryside. Here, their God gave them Warshine, and began to train them on a small, mountainous island. This intense training allows them to quickly fly through the gates, reaching Gate III, when they bump into Teira again, who has unlocked Dimension Gate I. Together, they reside, bonding over their forbidden powers. But one day, Light abandons them without a trace. No words, no notes, nothing. So together, they wander, searching for the god- until Teira abandons Aeri, too, leaving them confused and sad. They wander, being forced to dodge Coven and Phlanax law enforcement alike, before finally deciding to flee to Gaea, where they find Teira again. Teira excitedly introduces them to Terkou, who begins to indoctrinate the Warshine user, preparing them for a greater crusade than Imbra's.
