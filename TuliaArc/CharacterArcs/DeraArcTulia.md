# Dera Kni-Soa | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Time will subceed all._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: Tied heavily to his family's lineage, failing to meet many expectations as he pushed for his own independent career as an engineer.

**Ending State**: Isolated (with K'zorak) and afraid with an unfamiliar element on an unfamiliar planet.

**Inciting Losses**: Getting drafted causing him to lose his family.

**Irreversable Actions**: Faking his own death to escape the Tixolam draft.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Much smaller influence overall; mainly showing that Knightsdales can be escaped (even when they think they've gotten you).
## Summary 
Dera Kni-Soa, a member of the newest generation of the long Kni-Soa Poisi bloodline, always wanted to live a more silent life as an engineer, a rare profession for Poisi Elementals. Unfortunately for him, this fascination backfied when Tixolam came to power, as they wished to use his specializations for their agenda of world conquest. Refusing to fight alongside this, he faked his death and fled to a different continent, hiding out in the rainforest alone. But Dera is paranoid, very much so. To him, everyone was after him. He had illegaly entered a country who considered him hostile, and illegaly fled one who considered him an asset. This pushed him to awaken his val- where he entered Era Gate I. If it weren't for K'zorak finding him soon after, he likely would've gone insane. Together, the two survived for many years, until Ralia soldiers did indeed come. Not for Dera, but for K'zorak. He defended K'zorak, getting wounded himself in the process, but this helped him become familair with the power he feared. K'zorak, who began seeking Omni for better protection, reached Gate II in it when Dera reached Gate II Time, and together they fled to Earth where they reside on a small, uninhabited island.
## Timeline 
⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Dera is commonly overlooked in his family due to his own dreams not aligning with his family's wishes. His sister Teira gets most of the attention, though this does not seem to harm the relationship between them.

⦿ **T-Year 901**
- Being residents of Set, Dera and his family are directly affected by the Seige of Set, the 8-day seige of the city by Knightsdale soldiers.
- Set falls, leading to the establishment of Tixolam, a Poisi Knightsdale nation.
- The Kni-Soa family loses a heavy amount of political influence due to the change in ideology, and fear becoming targets.

⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, with Tixolam immediately passing draft legislation, which will hit Dera in a year.

⦿ **T-Year 909**
- Dera is drafted into the military, immediately facing mistreatment and mass propaganda.
- Outraged and hurt by what his nation has become, Dera fakes his death by Element Suffocation (usually leaves a space of the squished Element and is a common form of suicide) 3 days after being forced into the military, and flees the nation.
- Dera arrives in Ralia, hiding out in the Kial Rainforest.
  
⦿ **T-Year 911**
- Dera grows incredibly anxious, due to being hostile to both sides. He beguns looking over his shoulder everyday, every noise in the Rainforest causing him to overthink.

⦿ **T-Year 914**
- Dera awakens his Val, entering Era Gate I.
- He begins losing his mind as he feels as if he is reliving all of his life due to Era's affect on the Elemental mind.
- Dera meets K'zorak | K'zorak finds Dera and decides to help him stabilize. Dera is dazed, unable to form many coherent thoughts.
  
⦿ **T-Year 916**
- Soldiers of Ralia come to assassinate K'zorak, who only survives because Dera protects them.
- Dera suggests K'zorak seek a Forbidden Element in order to protect himself better.

⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Dera reaches Era Gate II after working to understand his new power.
- With Ralia soldiers chasing them, Dera and K'zorak flee the planet, aiming for Earth/Gaea.

⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- The pair arrives on Earth/Gaea, being rejected and mistreated everywhere they go.
- Through societal hints, they learn of the ongoing conflict between Humans and Elementals.
- Due to this, the two decide to settle on a small distant island.

⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Dera's anxiety has been growing again. The fear of the unfamiliar and hostile planet makes them shiver, but K'zorak keeps them sane and stable.
