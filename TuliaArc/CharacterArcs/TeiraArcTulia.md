# Teira Kni-Soa | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Nowhere is home anymore. I'm lost in my own skin._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: The gem of her family's newest generation: beautiful, smart, and charismatic.

**Ending State**: Isolated by what she believes is her own choice (Dimension influences it), but beginning to listen to Terkou's preachings. 

**Inciting Losses**: Her family encouraging her to flee so she's not drafted or used by the state.

**Irreversable Actions**: Abandoning Aeri. This hurts them, but Teira doesn't see anything wrong with it.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Showing the more subtle side to Forbidden Element horrors. 
## Summary 
The star of the youngest Kni-Soa generation, this young lady was well known in multiple communities. She was incredibly outspoken, popular, and independent. This did not work well for her when Dal yon Tulia broke out- despite her family begging her to be silent, she still spoke out against the state. This later pushed her family to send her away to a refugee camp in the neighboring Litti nation, where she first meets Aeri. There they grow close, sharing stories about their life, before Aeri is sent away due to their wavering beliefs in the Litti propaganda. Partially influenced by this, Teira leaves the camp, searching for her family. It's then she awakens her val, with her original element being replaced by Dimension as she enters Gate I. This (unknowingly to her) influences her into an isolation period of 5 Tulia years, until she's finally adopted by a nice rural Litti-Poisi family. Though, she quickly leaves them to, struggling to form attachments. Then she finds Aeri again, and she remains with them for a while- before abandoning them, too, and fleeing to Earth, where they are still alone, but not afraid. After a while, they meet Terkou, who grounds them and gives them stability.
## Timeline
⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Teira is the gem of her family, and heavily favored over her brother. Though this does not seem to harm their relationship.

⦿ **T-Year 901**
- Being residents of Set, Teira and her family are heavily affected by the Seige of Set, the 8-day seige of the city by Knightsdale soldiers.
- Set falls, leading to the establishment of Tixolam, a Poisi Knightsdale nation.
- The Kni-Soa family loses a heavy amount of political influence due to the change in ideology, and fear becoming targets.

⦿ **T-Year 902**
- Due to their fears, the Kni-Soa family keeps Teira home, isolating her from the outside world. She still gets plenty of attention from her family.

⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, and her nation passes a law that will enlist her brother in about a year. She is silently devastated by this, but her brother tells her it's okay.

⦿ **T-Year 909**
- After Dera is drafted into the military, Teira is sent away to a refugee camp in Sharlin, the neighboring Litti nation, by her family. They beleive she will be safer there, away from the horrors of Knightsdale.
- Teira meets Aeri | Aeri, working as a guard in the camp, speaks to Teira. They grow close, sharing stories from their own nation.

⦿ **T-Year 910**
- Aeri disappears without explanation. Teira asks about them, and the other guards simply brush it off as "relocation" or "redeployment". Teira suspects something bad happened.
- Feeling lonely at the camp, she begins making plans to leave.

⦿ **T-Year 912**
- Teira leaves the camp, aiming to find her family, mainly her brother.
- She awakens her val, believing it'll help her on her mission. However, her element disappears, and she enters Dimension Gate I as the element has chosen her.
- Immediately, she goes through massive changes. She no longer wishes to find her family, instead beginning to wander aimlessly in the Litti countryside.

⦿ **T-Year 914**
- Teira survives off of the land, eating berries and animals whilst commonly losing consciousness. She hardly knows where she is.

⦿ **T-Year 917**
- A rural Litti family finds her in their farm. They decide to adopt her, feeding and clothing her while giving her shelter.
- Teira eventually leaves, but she doesn't even remember leaving. She continues to wander, hoping she didn't hurt the family's feelings.
- Teira re-connects with Aeri after stumbling onto their island. They connect again over their new Forbidden Elements.
- Aeri brings Teira to their shelter, and the two survive together.

⦿ **T-Year 919**
- Teira suddenly leaves Aeri, and she doesn't even remember doing so. She feels bad for them, and considers going to search for them again- yet some underlying force makes that task feel impossible.
- She hears of many fleeing to Earth/Gaea, and she decides to do so herself. She can't conclude why she decides this.
- Only a few days later, she takes off into space.

⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- Teira arrives on Earth/Gaea. During her travels, she unlocked Dimension Gate II due to her use of it- and its silent use of itself. She doesn't even realize she unlocked this Gate. 
- She originally wanders aimlessly through the lands of Africa, her new-found balance issues from Gate II Dimension causing minor injuries.
- She's briefly noticed by the local government, who reports her to the ADS.

⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Teira leaves just in time to dodge the Global Government. She arrives in Madagascar, where she commonly sleeps on the beaches.
- She then moves multiple times, before eventually arriving in Antarctica.

⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Teira meets Terkou | After finding solace in the cold of the continent, she seeks to move further inland, where Terkou finds her, offering her company, shelter, and food. He then speaks to her, slowly indoctrinating her into the ideology.
- Teira meets Is'ale | Also residing with Terkou, he assists in indoctrinating her, mostly by accident. He seems to care more for her mental health, as she's clearly unstable.
- Teira adapts to her new society, her urge to migrate weakening.

⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Aeri arrives, meeting Teira again in Antarctica. She happily introduces them to Terkou and Is'ale.

⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Fully indoctrinated by Terkou, she believes in his ideology completely, ready to fight for Endsight and bring Valoran Democracy to Earth/Gaea.
