# K’zorak Shima | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_My fight for freedom shall never end._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A popular independent journalist known for exposing and de-platforming immoral people.

**Ending State**: A hidden elemental, with only one other as company, hiding from the evils of Earth/Gaea.

**Inciting Losses**: After surviving assassination attempts from his own nation, he believes there is no "Good Side" on Tulia. 

**Irreversable Actions**: Seeking out Omni, knowing he will lose his original element.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Proving that Phlanax nations are not perfect.
## Summary 
K'zorak was a journalist living in Ralia, sparing no news from coverage during Dal yon Tulia. All brutal attacks, all massacres, all government secrets were exposed by him and spread to everyone he could reach. Unfortunately for him, Ralia and her allies began to believe that his actions were destabilizing populations due to fear and radicalism. When asked to stop, K'zorak refused. Then, only a few days later, he found himself being escorted by soldiers to the Rainforest of Kial, a large, dense rainforest deep within Ralia. There, he adapted, surviving off the land, learning to hunt, and thriving in his surroundings. Eventually, a young, dazed Dera at Era Gate I ran into him. He took this young Dera under his wing, still caring deeply for victims of Knightsdale Nations. Together, they survived for a while. Until assassins, believed to be have sent by Ralia, tried to kill K'zorak. They failed, thanks to Dera defending him. This provoked him into searching for his own forbidden, where he found Omni. Together, him and Dera reach the second gate of their respective Forbiddens, and they begin plotting to flee to Earth/Gaea. Their plan is rushed when Phlanax soldiers chase them off the planet, forcing them to reside on a small uninhabited island on Gaea when everywhere else refuses them refuge. 
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- K'zorak is a very loud independent journalist funded by government free speech laws which requires them to pay journalists like himself.
- When the Firen fall to Knightsdale, he is sure to cover it as much as he can. He even visits the nation despite the wishes of literally everyone, heavily endangering himself to expose the low quality of life in the nation.
- Ralia begins to turn against journalists covering Knightsdales, and begins to cut them out of their law.

### ⦿ **T-Year 903**
- K'zorak joins a small media conglomerate made of independent journalists who opt to continue covering Knightsdales, even without government funding.

### ⦿ **T-Year 905**
- K'zorak nearly dies when he goes to cover the civil war in the Airren nation. This coverage gets heavy media attention.
- He also writes an article on the conflict.
- His nation sues him for the article on the Airren Civil War, claiming K'zorak is using fear, which destabilizes the nation, to expand his audience base. His defense claims that even if this was the case, his actions are protected under free speech.
- He gets the support of his media congolmerate, who run a deficit to fund his defense.
- The legal battle ends up getting lots of media attention, people interview K'zorak who speaks very negatively of the government and begins to harm their public image, claiming they aim to take away free speech.

### ⦿ **T-Year 907**
- Support for K'zorak plateaus as the state launches its own propaganda campaign against him, claiming he's a fearmongerer who must be silenced.
- The legal battle becomes a fight for free speech instead of the right to cover sensitive war news.
  
### ⦿ **T-Year 908**
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out, causing more media outlets to back K'zorak, giving a much needed boost to fund his legal battle.
- Media outlets all around Ralia end up building a counter-suit against Ralia, claiming they infringe on free speech with the K'zorak lawsuit.
- The Grand Court combines all suits into one- Ralia v Media.

### ⦿ **T-Year 910**
- After a long fought legal battle, The Court rules in favor of Ralia, but not entirely. The court says the state may restrict speech that spreads unprovoked or unrealistic fears in peace time, but can suppress all media that does so during war time. Since they fight in Dal yon Tulia, this means they can censor K'zorak and other outlets.
- K'zorak does not respect the ruling, and continues to cover major war news.
- Due to this, he is arrested and banished to the Kial Rainforest. It's made illegal to contact him.
- He still publishes news articles from his exile on dark webs, making him a wanted criminal. Ralia chooses not to prosecute him publicly, instead silently declaring him a national security threat.

### ⦿ **T-Year 911**
- K'zorak stabilizes, building a forever shelter with steady food and water supply.
- Some of his old media friends and family break the law to visit him.

### ⦿ **T-Year 914**
- K'zorak meets Dera | He aims to help Dera stabilize, but he also seeks permanent company.
- K'zorak welcomes Dera to stay with him permanently, even despite his odd and unusual element.

### ⦿ **T-Year 916**
- Assassins sent by Ralia come to kill K'zorak, who only survives thanks to Dera defending him.
- While healing him, Dera recommends he seek out a Forbidden Element, so he begins searching for one.
- One day, while experimenting, he unlocks Omni mostly by accident. He prepares to sacrifice his element, feeling betrayed by his people.

### ⦿ **T-Year 919**
- K'zorak reaches Omni Gate II, and begins making plans to flee the planet with Dera.
- They flee the planet, being chased off by Phlanax bounty hunters.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- The pair arrives on Earth/Gaea. They are rejected and mistreated everywhere they go.
- They also learn of the ongoing conflict between Humans and Elementals.
- Due to this, they decide to reside on a small island, away from society.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- K'zorak, using his experience from the Kial Rainforest, easily sets up survival on the island for the two.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- K'zorak now hides from the conflict on Earth/Gaea, but he uses his journalism skills to stay aware of what's happening. He doesn't inform Dera, as he knows that he is prone to anxiety.
