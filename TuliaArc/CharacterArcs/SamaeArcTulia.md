# Samae Herba | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_My home will not be taken._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A radical anti-elemental human fighting tooth and nail to get others to agree with her.

**Ending State**: The leader of the Anti-Elemental Terrorist Organization known as the "Knights of Gaea", going so far as to attack humans who disagree.

**Inciting Losses**: Losing in a hand-to-hand no element battle with Adva makes her feel weak and embarassed.

**Irreversable Actions**: Authorizing the Knights of Gaea to attack humans who are pro or indifferent to elementals.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the head of the Knights of Gaea.
## Summary 
A young woman who lost her home to war, she grew up only knowing conflict. She was only 13 when she got her first technological augments, and 14 when she participated in her first battle, and it happened to be a devestating one that cost her her family and nation. With nowhere to go, she began moving around, preaching incredibly hateful sentiment to corpratist societies like the one that took her home. When the first Elementals arrived, she began to speak out against them, too, and the Global Government (Association of Democratic States) for accepting them so easily. She became one of the main figures for this stance, and eventually founded the Knights of Gaea to push this sentiment in the shadows. They kidnapped, killed, and tourmented Elementals all across the planet, who were mostly ignored when they complained about these issues. On Troy's takeover of the Elemental Division of the ADS, she became a lot more risky with her moves, seeing some of his views align with hers. Troy even began funding her operations, until she openly began attacking those who disagreed with anti-elemental sentiment. Now, the Knights of Gaea are their own faction fighting in the Burning of Gaea, aiming to be the final defenders of humanity from the evils of Elementals.
