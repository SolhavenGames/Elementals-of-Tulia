# Samae Herba | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_My home will not be taken._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A radical anti-elemental human fighting tooth and nail to get others to agree with her.

**Ending State**: The leader of the Anti-Elemental Terrorist Organization known as the "Knights of Gaea", going so far as to attack humans who disagree.

**Inciting Losses**: Losing in a hand-to-hand no element battle with Adva makes her feel weak and embarassed.

**Irreversable Actions**: Authorizing the Knights of Gaea to attack humans who are pro or indifferent to elementals.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the head of the Knights of Gaea.
## Summary 
A young woman who lost her home to war, she grew up only knowing conflict. She was only 13 when she got her first technological augments, and 14 when she participated in her first battle, and it happened to be a devestating one that cost her her family and nation. With nowhere to go, she began moving around, preaching incredibly hateful sentiment to corpratist societies like the one that took her home. When the first Elementals arrived, she began to speak out against them, too, and the Global Government (Association of Democratic States) for accepting them so easily. She became one of the main figures for this stance, and eventually founded the Knights of Gaea to push this sentiment in the shadows. They kidnapped, killed, and tourmented Elementals all across the planet, who were mostly ignored when they complained about these issues. On Troy's takeover of the Elemental Division of the ADS, she became a lot more risky with her moves, seeing some of his views align with hers. Troy even began funding her operations, until she openly began attacking those who disagreed with anti-elemental sentiment. Now, the Knights of Gaea are their own faction fighting in the Burning of Gaea, aiming to be the final defenders of humanity from the evils of Elementals.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **E-Year 2010**
- Samae is born in Cairo to a very radical and militant family fighting for a country of mercenaries.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2016**
- Her upbringing is violent, her family constantly fighting and moving. Even as a young child, she's forced to adapt to a dangerous society.
- Her nation falls to a corpratist society, and her family refuses to kneel, so they join resistance groups.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2020**
- The first Elementals arrive, and despite only being 10 years old, Samae already doesn't like them, silently believing they are here to take the planet away.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2023**
- Her family begins to give her technological augments stolen from their corporate conquerers, allowing Samae to become stronger and smarter.
- She begins to train, as she has been excited for years to join her family in battle.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2024**
- She participates in her first battle, where her entire family dies, along with the last of her nation's aspirations. They are now forever conquered by the corprate nation.
- She flees to avoid being captured, and begins to wander around the planet, preaching hateful rhetoric about corpratist societies and Elementals. Though, Elementals are still few and far between, making them more of a political issue.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2027**
- At only 17 years old, she forms the Knights of Gaea, an organization designed to instill fear into Elementals on the planet.
- They immediately carry out terrorist attacks against the Elemental populations on Earth/Gaea.
- Silently, they are declared a criminal organization by the Global Government, but they struggle to pinpoint or find them.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Samae's Knights of Gaea becomes the largest and most well-funded underground organization on the planet in under a year after it was founded.
- Samae remains a public anti-Elemental figure who constantly preaches that the species doesn't belong on the planet.
- Samae meets Wykoi | The two have a public heated debate about Elementals on Earth/Gaea, which gets massive viewership across the planet.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- The Knights of Gaea begins to infiltrate entire governments, building anti-Elemental laws and funneling money into the organization.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- The second wave of Elemental Refugees arrive, causing Samae to become even more outspoken.
- The Knights of Gaea are publicly addressed and confirmed for the first time, previously only being a consipiracy theory.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- In reaction to the attack on Wuhan-Nanjing, Samae and the Knights of Gaea deploy themselves to cities without approval from the nation to defend civilians. This gets them upgraded from criminal to terrorist organization.
- The Knights of Gaea fights all around the globe, being the strongest resistance against Elementals. Troy Talona funds them, despite their status.
- Samae meets Troy | Troy publicly calls her a wonderful and strong woman, endorsing her actions. This causes the Knights of Gaea to gain a lot of members in a short amount of time.
- The Knights of Gaea begins to attack humans who aren't anti-Elemental, causing Troy to pull back funding and support.
- Samae meets Adva | Despite having anti-val technology built into her augments, Samae is defeated by Adva in hand-to-hand combat, even while having back up from other members of the Knights of Gaea.
- Humiliated by her defeat, she begins to seek out Pure after hearing rumors of it from some of her wiser members. 

### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Samae does not participate in the Battle of New York Super City, as she is busy self-experimenting.
- She does still command her underlings to defend the city, though.
- Samae uses val from dead Elementals to pump into her own body. This makes her deathly ill, but she narrowly survives.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Samae suddenly unlocks Pure one day, after hundreds of experiments with val. This turns some of her organization against her, causing a small civil war within the Knights of Gaea.
- She eventually resecures power, though she lost a few thousand members. The ones that stayed were swayed when she convinced them that she merely uses their power against them.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Using further experimentation, Samae reaches Pure Gate II. She feels the strength difference, but she grows to hate the feeling of val coursing through her veins.
- Pushed by the Element, she decides she's not good enough, and pushes the Knights of Gaea and herself harder to defend the planet from Elementals.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Back on the frontlines, but slightly upset with herself. She believes she can do more, and she's already begun searching for Pure Gate III.
- The Knights of Gaea remains the strongest force against Elementals.
