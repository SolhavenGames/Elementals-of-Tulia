# Before The Hunt …

The room was too well lit. Candles rest in holsters decorated on the stone wall, with a chandelier decoration holding even more dangling from the ceiling. Flames flickered and danced upon the wicks of each individual candle.

Banners drape down the walls, flourishing the symbol of the Empire of Irision— A raging flame atop an opened eye, always watching. The banners themselves are decorated with the Irision orange and gold staples, a shameless flaunting of wealth. 

In the center of the room rests a woman they call Knison Iris, a name synonymous with the entirety of Irision and its actions. Some hate her name, others bow to it. But all unanimously fear it, to some extent.

Iris rests in the room, tapping her fingers patiently on the table resting before her. Her flames dance slowly, becoming almost infatuating. Two knights stand behind her, decorated in gold-plated armor, their bodies stiff as they hold their weapons. 

In walks a great warrior, her chin held high. Grand Knight Asli makes her entrance, though Iris’ expression does not change from her piercing stare.

“Twenty two seconds.” Iris begins. Her voice is almost too soft for who she is. “That’s how late you were, Grand Knight.”

Asli takes her helmet off, placing it down on the table before she takes a seat. The metal clinks and scratches against the stone. “Apologies, my flame.” She says, kneeling her head slightly. “I—”

“This is the fourth time.” Iris interrupts. She places her feet upon the table, leaning back in her chair. She doesn’t even look in Asli’s direction. “Don’t give me a reason to revoke your armor.”

There is a short pause, in which only the burning of flames can be heard. “I will do better.” Asli’s head is still knelt slightly forward in Iris’ direction.

Iris nods. “I know you will.” 

Another pause falls upon the room as Iris’ gaze finally falls upon Asli. She scans her up and down, taking note of every detail. Every twitch of her muscles. Every flicker of her flame.

“You may speak.” Iris says, turning her head away and waving her hand in the air. 

Asli picks up her head. “I’ve heard whispers of a plot that could turn the tide of the war.” She leans forward, pulling herself back slightly after catching odd glares from the knights behind Iris. “I want to be involved any way I can.”

Iris pauses, then stares at Asli, causing Asli’s heart to race. This time, Iris isn’t staring at her, or through her. She’s staring directly into her— rating her soul. Scaling her power. Searching for her resolve.

Finally, she lifts her hand, the closest knight handing her a tablet. She slides it across the table to Asli. “Imbra Hayes.” The tablet displays an image of Imbra, though slightly outdated. “Semi-famous daughter of Axor Hayes. Have you heard of her?”

“I have.” Asli nods. 

Iris pulls the tablet back to her side of the table. “Poisi scientists have told me that there’s a chance she wields the mythical Hellfire.” Flames seem to flicker differently at the mere mention of the element. She hands the tablet back to the knight. “But she fled the planet about 12 days ago, fleeing to Gaea.”

Asli sits almost entirely still, only her chest rising as she breathes. “Hellfire? I—”

“You must leave soon.” Iris interrupts. “Take two of your knights and bring Imbra back. Alive.” 

Asli’s eyes flicker around the room. Her hand twitches as she stands. “Yes, Knison.”

# After The Hunt …
<sub>~ 90-100 Tulia Days after “Before The Hunt”<sub> 

The doors swung open, two knights holding them. In walked Grand Knight Asli, holding a chained Snai Elemental by the collar as if it were an animal. Its face had been covered and mouth kept shut by a clamp. The chains have become frostbitten after remaining tied to it for so long.

Iris sat on the other side of the room, her chin resting upon two balled fists. As her gaze falls upon the prisoner, her eyes grow narrow. Her flames seem to jerk in odd, angular directions as she examines the visible parts of its body. 

She turns her head to Asli. “Where’s Imbra, Grand Knight?” She asks, calm and composed. 

“Miss Hayes is still on Gaea.” Asli’s voice shakes slightly. “She—”

“What was your mission?” Iris interrupts. She inspects her fingers, front and back, as she awaits Asli’s answer.

“Retrieve Imbra Hayes alive.” Asli says, almost robotic. “From Gaea to Tulia.”

Iris nods. “Has Miss Hayes become a Snai Elemental?”

“No, my Knison.”

“Then…” Iris stands, slowly stepping closer to the two. “Why is there one in my court?”

Asli kneels her head, swallowing silently. “Hellfire was too volatile to transport, my flame.” Each tremble in her voice grows more noticeable through the echoes in the room. “It would’ve been impossible to transport by force.”

Iris continues her journey closer, her feet clicking against the ground. Surrounding flames only seem to flicker towards her, as if trying to join her. She stands just in front of Asli, close enough for their fire to intermingle. 

Her gaze falls upon a wound on the neck. A discoloured spot that Asli’s skin hasn’t grown back over correctly. Damage that calls to her very soul. 

“What happened there?” Iris asks, hovering her finger above the wound.

“I was stabbed with a Hellfire Weapon.” Asli doesn’t raise her head, avoiding eye contact. “A dart of sorts. It hasn’t healed properly since.” 

Iris presses her finger into it, causing Asli to wince. She holds her finger there, examining the texture. Quantifying the temperature. Then, she thinks, staring dead towards Asli’s shoulder. The silence is only interrupted by the breathing of those who reside in the room. Then, she finally releases a small “Hm.” 

Iris steps back, leaning onto the table and crossing her arms. “Reveal the Snai.” 

Asli nods. “As you wish.” She rips the hood off of his head, revealing a beaten and injured Snai elemental. It and Iris immediately lock eyes. “Kael Voris is his name.”

Kael shakes, his breathing growing unsteady as his eyes shoot around the room. Iris shifts her gaze to Asli. “It was to my understanding that you declared it impossible to transport Imbra, correct?” 

Asli nods, her mind choking. “But this Snai—”

Iris throws her hand up. “What nation is this?”

“Irision, my Knison.”

“And who lives in Irision?”

“The Firen.” Asli’s voice lowers. 

 “Then, explain yourself, Grand Knight.” Iris stands up straight, her arms remaining crossed firmly over her chest. “Why did you bring a Snai to Irision’s court?”

“I wouldn’t dare return empty handed, my flame.” Asli places her hand upon Kael’s head, who flinches at the contact. “And this Snai has a high val bank. I’m sure you could do something with that.”

Iris’ gaze jumps between Asli and Kael. Then, she approaches, and kneels before Kael. She places her finger under his chin, moving his head around. His resistance is light, almost nullable compared to her. “Maybe I could.”
