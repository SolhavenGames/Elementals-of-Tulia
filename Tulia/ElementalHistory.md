History of Tulia
        Tulia was formed around the same time of the Sun, being the first terra-planet in the Solar System. Its original rate of revolution was around 786 earth days, and rotation around 12 earth days. Tulia was a very cold planet when it was first formed as well. However, around 5 million years after its formation, it was hit by an asteroid from the asteroid belt. The asteroid was a decent size, being just under a tenth of Tulia’s size. It hit Tulia with a low impact angle, but it still caused major damage. Its revolution around the sun sped up heavily, from 786 to 429 earth days, and its rotation went from 12 earth days to 28 hours. This became the new normal thanks to the gravitational pull of the asteroid belt and Jupiter, keeping its new speed. It has 3 moons that it pulled from the asteroid belt, two are present at night and one during the day. The names are “Kinnsi”, translating to “Little Sun”, and the two night moons are named “Orbinus”, translating to “Life”, and “Tublis”, which is a common name for elemental males. It’s life formed after Earth’s, however, as it’s on the very edge of the habitable zone, making it far colder than Earth. Life on Tulia evolved very slowly, until it eventually emerged out of the ocean around 2 billion years after its beginning. Then, the land life forms began. Grass, Trees, Flowers, all of it began to evolve and complicate, adapting to its harsher environment. The first and only intelligent life forms, Elementals, appeared around 10 million years ago. Elementals are a species who are heavily connected with the major elements. The first Elementals were primitive, being able to only control Fire and Ice. The first two elements— and the strongest known. As they evolved, however, the more elements they could control. Fire, Ice, Water, Air, Rock, Lightning, Nature, Light, Darkness, Snow, Wood, Metal, Poison, Sand, and Blood are all the elements that they have the ability to control in the present. Many Elementals attempt to learn more, but it’s very difficult. Elementals managed to become a collective civilization, with their own laws, countries, governments and more. Wars were fought by powerful Elementals who wished to conquer the major continents of Tulia, such as Hurion, Phoenix, North or South Unbion, The Far South, and Ingur. However, It’s been to no avail. Tulia still remains heavily threatened by the power-hungry. Tulia’s days are 28 hours long and its years are 429 days.  It doesn’t have a concept of weeks. All of its days are referred to by its calendar date. It has 13 calendar months, Kinner, Morsh, Hulmin, Yurni, Polus, Junoy, Zneff, Tilik, Unity, Retti, Tulip, Venos, and Stemo. 33 days in each month.

History/Culture of Elementals
        This only partakes in the history of  the species as a whole. Rarely will it have independent events from nations or elements involved. It excludes things that are considered to be myths and falsehoods, even if they are true (Forbidden Elements). 

* Early Elemental History

        Elementals started off in small groups & tribes based on their element. Elementals are usually only able to control one element, but in the case they control more, they are known as Daris. Those who only control one are called Karains. Daris were heavily discriminated against, as either of their elements would not want to house them. Daris were often seen as unholy, called broken and disloyal. In the very beginning, Fire and Ice Elementals heavily distrusted each other, splitting the planet into three, Firen Tribes, Icik Tribes, and the Daris tribes. On what's thought to be the 5th of Junoy, around the year -9,000,000, the third element, Water, was brought into the world. It broke the balance of Fire-Ice, and the Elemental segregation paused early there, as Fire, Ice, and Water began to live in harmony and use their elements together, and somewhat including the Daris, although most were still excluded from most events. Then, around 1.5 million years later, on the 18th of Tilik, Rock and Air were discovered, bringing more Elementals into the world of Tulia. This is when small cities began to form without governments. Millions of years later, with tribal settlements being harder to sustain due to the population rising, and more elementals preferring to settle than to travel, slowly switching their preference, around year -3,000 elementals discovered agriculture and the element, Nature, raising settlement and lowering mass-travel. Small cities began to grow, where elementals would settle with their own element, bringing back the segregation from before the discovery of Water. Major cities like Spaeual, a Firen city, Daarj, an Icik city, and Daon, a Naturine city, were founded in this time. These cities would be known as “Dua Taacl”, translating roughly to “A Cradle”. 

* First Civilizations, beginning of The Early Era.
  
  
  
  

        The Early Era is quite an important time in elemental history. It was when elementals learned to govern, live together, use their elements in combat, and wage war. It was also when the population of nations were split into three groups. The commonfolk, by far the most populous. They were usually poor and uneducated, without knowing combat, but always held the highest percentage of the population. The warriorfolk is much like the commonfolk, but they are trained in combat, and sometimes educated. They hold the second highest percentile in population, and sometimes surpass to be the first, which can cause economic problems for a nation if warriorfolk populations remain too high. Then, the last, the landowners, by definition the top 1%. They were rich, educated, and usually the ones governing in The Early Era. 

With tribal conflict growing over the newly discovered agriculture, the first elemental civilization to appear is Molano, a Fire Imperium on the continent North Unbion around year -2,998. An Imperium is a similar type of government to Earth’s monarchy, except the royal family is in council control instead of one elemental across one bloodline. It’s a government that mostly appeases the rich and landowners, and the commonfolk hate it. However, it was discovered by leaders that leading a nation alone was difficult. Rich elementals began to sprout up in near absolute control of land that was a part of larger nations, paying tributes to the nation to keep their land. This form of governing would happen throughout most of the primitive governments, and its style became known as “Dajalm”, diverging from the Frezzish word “Delemen”, meaning division. Dajalm allowed for heavy switches in leadership, as the most powerful across their region would rule over the rest, allowing them to be a “Nation”. However, maintaining the loyalty of the landowners and royal families below you was very important, as they could easily overthrow you. Hence, a delicate power balance was formed, where a “Nation” was made up of divided land between dajalms, and the strongest family ruled until toppled or overpowered by someone else. Molano was settled across the best-discovered lands for agriculture at the time, and as such, they didn’t allow other Elementals to live within their nation, paving the way for hundreds of thousands of years of segregation in nations based on your element.  This also brought back the heavy distrust from the primitive Fire-Ice-Daris days. Molano was a nation built on order and lawfulness, as well as warrior praising and glory hounds. The Dajalms were often warriors over bureaucrats. The Imperium adopted an economic system of Dassi, a system where the government had total control over everything, except for production, where the control was more 50/50, as government run businesses seize the commonfolk’s production. Commonfolk could do little businesses on their own in the form of bartering and the national currency, but the Dajalms and Royal family held over 99.9% control over the economy. The Dajalms would often return from raids of tribal villages with slaves, and the commonfolk enriched themselves through this, enslaving captured Naturine and Watrish elementals to farm. This caused Warish Elementals to flee North Unbion, to a new continent of Phoenix. Naturine elementals, however, weren’t so easily forced out. Wars and skirmishes began, and free & wealthy Naturine elementals who lived south of Molano had an idea. They met in a cradle city, Daon, and married each other, creating their own royal family, & causing the second civilization, Bino, to the south of Molano. Bino was led by a smaller royal family, which resulted in faster political and economic decisions. They also adopted Dassi, and their Dajalms were far richer on average as they took more control over production and trade, allowing for great cities to form, but also causing cultural regionalism in the country, that even spoke the Naturine language differently, arising with two prominent language types for them. Nartar, the more spoken and widespread, used in this time by commonfolk and warriorfolk, and Nattali less spoken, but mainly used by royals and the wealthy, making it far more prestigious to speak and learn. In the west, culture was far more prominent, with life more revolving around religion and morality. In the east, still quite religious, but they prided themselves on their better agricultural skills. In the north, religion wasn’t as prominent, but they were hard workers, and made of farmers and veterans from defending from Molano raids, but often poor due to exploitation. It was by far the most populous. However, without the south, the nation probably wouldn’t exist. The south was the formation of all of them together, forming what was back then the richest region in the world. Daon was in this region. The capital, Aagala, was in this region. The south was the backbone of the nation. The entire nation built itself around agriculture and giving to other elementals who lived outside of Molano, who they considered an enemy. This set the grounds for the first war with a formal declaration and occupations. During this, another nation popped up in The Far South, called “Folca”, under a new government style of Permia. Permism is a form of government where it’s centered around religion and listening to the consciousness of its element. It’s very popular among the clergy, who gain power in this government. Commonfolk, however, quite hate the government, despite being religious. The clergy often pass laws that make it harder for them to live, raising costs of goods. Folca was quite an odd nation. It was a new government, which proved difficult to run, but after church devotees seized power, they set the basis for the new style. Instead of a Royal Family, there was a Holy family, which was replaced by churches around the state. Everything surrounded and ran around religion, making the Icik elementals incredibly religious. A few years later, around the year -2,871,  the first war started between Molano and Bino. The war is called “The Million Years War”, as it lasted for hundreds of years, and created the first rivalry to last through all of history. The Firen-Naturine rivalry is very odd. It would be a lie to say that they absolutely avoided associating with each other. They often ally in the future, which makes many historians bring their rivalry into question. But, those who claim that the Firen-Naturine rivalry is true and real use The Million Years War and its events as a main source, as well as the general disdain between civilians and inhabitants of the nations. On the greater, international scale, you were either Anti-Firen and Pro-Naturine, or vice versa. Rarely was there ever an in between, with the future Airren nation, Jasu, being the exception.

* Inside The Million Years War
  
        The Million Years War is a series of wars between the first two nations. It was started by Molano in -2,871, who got a surprise attack and occupied 7 of the northern nature cities in a region called Yiam. The largest being a city called “Gronn”, a highly populated city with a grand trade center that was originally founded to house Naturine warriors who went to raid Molano. The start of the war brewed major distrust and hatred between the elementals as individuals, and Daris of Fire-Nature faced the worst of it, being murdered on sight. Gronn produced a lot of agricultural goods for the occupiers, against their will. Bino responded to this attack with many counter attacks which saw moderate success. The first 12 years of the war were small skirmishes across the border, as neither side really knew how to wage war. However, this changed on the 29th of Morsh, around the year -2,855, when Molano gathered a large portion of the Nature population in the cities it occupied, and forced them to work. When most of them refused, they were executed without a second thought. This event lasted many years, and is known as “Caalus Wigu”, translating to “The Wildfire Massacre". This is one of the first holidays. Celebrated by both Naturine and Firen, it’s named “Fogne don Jugule”, translating to “Fire and Nature”. It’s to represent the anger from Naturine elementals and victory from Firen. It is celebrated on the 15th of Tulip. This fueled the rivalry between the two, not only nations, but citizens, and even elements. Bino and its population was enraged, which caused the first large-scale training in combat methods and the first monument, as warriors banded together to form the first armies. Armies were formed in Bino’s kingdom. Molano heard about this and began to train their own. Bino’s most notable move was something called “Orkmin”, which uses the plants and grass on the ground as a weapon. Molano also had its own specialized attacks, such as “Iglena”, which was a pillar of fire launched in the direction of the victim, with a large explosion at the end. Before this, war was waged by independent warriors, who went to fight on their own, in a warfare system known as “Dival Sodi”. Warriors would report their victories and losses back to the government and media, farming glory for their names. There was never a major leader figure, instead, warriors teaming up with friends in small groups of 4-6 to invade, conquer, and plunder. After this, however, warriors would team up with surrounding warriors, forming armies of up to 10,000, being known as “Vidal Sodi”. A monument was built in Bino’s capital, Aagala, called “Ralm Dun”, with the names of all of those killed in Callus Wigu, with a statue of a leader of the resistance, Ugril Talanei. This monument still stands today and elementals love to visit, reminiscing of the violence in unorganized war. The first semi-organized battle took place in Gronn in -2,840, being named “The Battle of Seedlings”, as this is when war began to be properly waged. Molano’s defense proved to be tough, resisting every Bino attempt to break in, even with the help of Gronn resistance groups. Gronn remained in Firen control. 

        The next 50 years of the war was fairly peaceful, which is why The Million Years War is considered to be more of a series of wars instead of just one huge war. During this 50 year period, the occupation of the northern cities of Bino still continued, but trading and relations between the two nations increased. This time period ended when Bino demanded that Molano return their occupied territories, and when Molano refused, many battles began, such as “Yaad ilm”, A battle which saw heavy casualties for both sides. Molano’s occupation zone was forced further north, but they still occupied Gronn and another smaller city named “Yinyon”, and Bino led a battle to return these territories.

In the year -2,791, a major battle for the liberation of Yinyon began, a central city in Yiam. The casualties are still unknown, but with the help of modern technology, scientists have been able to determine that there was a loss of at least 2 thousand warriors on both sides, with about 641-753 MIA. Bino was desperate. In modern day, that isn't that much, but back then the casualties alone would’ve been nearly half of an army funded by a Dajalm. The battle was a victory for Molano, which would see the morale drop of both nations due to the mass loss of life. This battle itself is thought to have drastically shortened the war, as ancient writings speak of how horrifying the thought of more death was.
        Thanks to this morale drop, the population ordered a halting of the war. Molano’s elementals were suffering from the lack of attention that the monarchy was giving them, and Bino’s elementals had very little food, despite their agricultural power, due to the royalty demanding it all be sent to the frontlines. Both nations were facing heavy population drops, as with lack of professional armies, commonfolk were sent to the frontlines alongside the warriorfolk. This caused a 134 year period with peace. Very light trading commenced, mainly food and clothing, & the patriarchs, Spalkor of Molano and Jagual of Bino publically met and even shook hands. A nation on South Unbion, as an Air nation, also attended the meet. Peace seemed to be secured. Until Bino attacked once again, in -2,657. By this time, some of the royalty in rule of Molano passed, and they were in mourning. Nature decided this would be the best time to attack, and try to liberate their occupied cities. A major battle began here, known as “Valton Jilm”, as there was 1 casualty from each of the imperial families. The battle lasted for 3 Tulia days, and Molano came out on top due to their superiority in numbers. Bino fled the battle and there was a massive, nationwide celebration. This is now a holiday for all fire elementals, on the 3rd of Stemo, called “Inge dal Fonets”, which translates to “Day of Fire”.  This is to bring the pride of Fire Elementals to light. It’s celebrated by military and public parades.



Hundreds of thousands of years passed with the war continuing. The fighting got harsher and harsher as Bino desperately wanted to liberate its occupied lands, and Molano desperately wanted to keep a hold of them. The elementals were getting weary of all the deaths, and riots broke out in both nations demanding an end to it. Reluctantly, in -2,511, 360 years after the start of the war, a peace treaty was signed in the city that saw the most bloodshed, Gronn. Molano had quite the edge, still occupying the north. After 4 years of talks, the nations agreed that Molano would keep Gronn and Yinyon under an economic occupation, and would have to pay small war reparations for the other 5 northern cities that were damaged in the fighting. 





* Outside The Million Years War

During the war in North Unbion, a nation down in South Unbion began to form itself. It was the year -2,860 when an Imperium of Air called “Jasu” formed itself, which remained fairly peaceful as it was the only nation on the continent.  Airren elementals are known for the admirable peace they carry with them. Jasu was quite the peculiar nation. The nation was founded on pacifist dajalms and Royals, however still very nationalistic and xenophobic. Airren elementals were also often explorers, flying into space, and never returning. But they did discover The Asteroid Belt and Jupiter, naming them “Di Kajro” and “Zalka” respectively. Across an ocean in Phoenix, a small nation named “Shauson” formed in the year -3,801, inhabited exclusively by Ballad Elementals, and bringing along the discovery of the Blood element. Its founder, Duanel Iaakal was killed, as elementals believed that the element was fueled in hatred and evil, bringing the element to be deeply frowned upon. A new government system formed from this nation, named Incre. An Incre is a government which lacks a government & the Dajalm style, it’s more of a state with few laws and no leader. Owning land is impossible, as the state declares all land public, which is why there’s no Dajalmi. The laws the state does enact are always enacted at its creation, and they are incredibly finite. The government style mostly appeases to commonfolk, who now have total freedom. Warriorfolk and landowners hate this governing style, as warriorfolk love order, which this government lacks, and landowners become irrelevant. Shauson was founded on fear. Ballad elementals saw how the others viewed them, and knew they had to unite under something to defend themselves. This was supposed to be the unmarried Duanel, the first government run by one man. However, at his death, there was a power vacuum. Noone could fill it due to immense poverty, so it stayed that way throughout this entire period of Shauson history. Weak and unfunded law enforcement was the closest thing to a leader. Shauson slowly expanded through Ballad elementals settling surrounding land. They ran under an economic system of Aoaua. At first, Shauson was supposed to take on a Dassi economy with Duanel’s overtaking, but with him being killed, it was forced to make something else. WIth sloppy, disordered, and sometimes even unreadable writings, Aoaua was a form of economy where the elementals completely controlled everything, unregulated, leading to an unstable, unmanageable economy. It was near a nation that was founded only a few months before it, called “Sennas”, which rested on the coastline of the continent. It was led and inhabited by only Watrish elementals. Sennas was a nation that prided itself on happiness and divinity. The inhabitants of this nation were very peaceful, welcoming elementals. Xenophobia was unfortunately still widespread, as the Million Years War in North Unbion and their history of slavery had caused distrust. Sennas adopted an economic system of Dassi, with landowners and the Royal family holding most of the land, leading to absolute control over the economy. Despite their differences, Shauson and Sennas created an alliance to defend their continent against outsiders, as many elementals had interest in a resource they had called “Jaluma”, a rich, greenish resource that is used as jewelry and religious metal. In -2,741, Focla completely conquered The Far South, and The Ice Golden Age began. Back on the continent Phoenix, peace treaties and alliances were established between the two nations. On Hurion, the first nation of Rocian Elementals was established under a Permia, named Talkan. The nation was incredibly isolationist. Its population, Rocian elementals, were quite strong, but preferred their cultural development. It took a while for them to form their own nation due to them being spread, and being the only elemental without the ability to fly. Being the only nation on their continent, they had no friends, and no enemies. Hurion was seen as a fairly useless continent, but it won’t remain that way. Ingur remained habited by small groups and tribes of elementals, but no major nations. 

In -2,660 new war broke out in Phoenix, between Shauson and Sennas. Shauson completely stabbed Sennas in the back, as Shauson saw the economic power that the Jalamas could sustain, and they wanted it all for themselves. The war was organized with dajalm parties as Watrish slaves escaping from Molano spoke of the warfare they witnessed. The war lasted 3 years before Shauson defeated Sennas. Sennas was forced to adopt the government style of an Incre. The royal family was killed and Sennas, who changed their name to Shasson, was now a satellite state of Shauson. Another holiday came to light due to this celebrated by Watrish elementals on the 1st of Polus called “Wiko Hegien”, translating to “Free The Ocean”. Watrish Elementals celebrate this holiday by spending time with their loved ones and appreciating what they have. Resistance groups would spring up, but Ballad warriors would always seem to be able to stomp them out.



* The roads collide, Dajalm remains uncontrolled

        The Million years war ended, and now, in the year -2,100 total peace returned to Tulia. None of the nations approved of Shauson’s actions with the royal family of Sennas. However, most elementals tried to remain on their own continents and didn’t really bother to assist Watrish Elementals. They were busy building on their own economies and empires. Molano became Dal Iris, under a new government of True Knightsdale. A Knightsdale is a kingdom led by the military, and hellbent on expansion and making their race the strongest and most populous. There’s two  types of Knightsdales. One is led by one man, who shares his power with a close cabinet of advisors, named a Man’s Knightsdale. The other, and the more popular, is when the warrior population leads the state, called a True Knightsdale. Dal Iris was run under a True Knightsdale. This government has always appealed to warriorfolk, and always will. Commonfolk are quite neutral on the government, but the Dajalmi notoriously hate it, because even though they are still allowed to own land, warriorfolk come before them in the social hierarchy, and take governing rights. The warriorfolk also favor the commonfolk over landowners, leading to even more anger as the landowners watch as warriorfolk take all of their wealth through high taxes and ultimatums. This government derived from the warrior-dajalmi in the nation, and they were focused on expanding and uniting their elementals. They tried and failed to destroy the dajalm system, angering their dajalmi, but after promises of more land and money they regained their loyalty. This started “The Grand Race”, a race between Bino and Dal Iris to see who could climb the treacherous mountain range Kala’s Unbia and settle east of them, with both sides getting great agricultural land. The Imperial Family of Molano was still kept but as a figurehead, to try and keep the elementals behind them, as the family was still considered to be holy. Dal Iris also colonized Phoenix, causing tensions between them and Shauson. In the south, Folca’s golden age raged on. They became the most populated of all the nations, and became the most stable. The nation discovered mathematics, science, and built tools out of their element. When traders from other nations reported this to their leaders and elementals, this resulted in what’s called The Weaponry’s Awakening. This birthed the first widespread holiday called collectively “Polin Donn”, which translates to “Our Weapons”. It's from The 17th to the 20th of Polus. It’s celebrated by having everyone create their own weapons and present them in front of their governments. Although, unfortunately, it is rarely celebrated in modern Tulia due to the never-ending war, and it requires mass cooperation to be taken to its maximum potential. 
    
    
        Around a few hundred years later, in the year -1,991 Folca collapsed, and their golden age ended suddenly. The reasoning is still unknown, but the main theory is that the ideology of Knightsdale and Imperiums got popular with their population and dajalms, and they no longer wished to be a Permia. This caused a massive civil war on the 2nd of Hulmin. The civil war destabilized Tulia, as they were somewhat of peacekeepers in the physical world. The civil war raged and raged and The Icik Knightsdale named “Icralem” attempted to defeat The Icik Imperium named “Islem”. Dal Iris supported Icralem and Jasu attempted to bring Islem to victory, abandoning their peaceful style in fear of the Knightsdale ideology gaining too much strength. In the end, the war ended in a stalemate, as Dal Iris and Jasu demanded that peace be established and drew borders for the two. This improved relations between Dal Iris and Jasu, but both Icik nations hated the treaty. This is when Firen elementals began to seize the spot that Folca had left open, as the main superpower of the world. Unfortunately for them, a joint Bino and Jasu military alliance threw the power into Bino's favor. On Phoenix, The colony of Dal Iris was getting large. Shauson declared war and immediately got demolished in a 2 month war, known as “Qua Deend”, translating roughly to “The Blunder”, with some battles having Ballad elementals outnumbered 10 to 1. They were conquered and incorporated into Dal Iris, bringing lots of international negative attention to the nation. In -1,951, the first major empire was born, under the name “Eternal Flame”. It strayed away from a Knightsdale, seeing its power being eternal, and turned into what’s called a Junik. A Junik is a government run by multiple councils— the first government to ever include any. These councils aren’t elected, instead run and appointed by dajalmi, as they were still a major part of this governing system. There are 3 major councils with 3 different leaders. The councils had their own dedicated jobs— The National Council was to keep the nation in check and stable, usually controlling laws and media the most, The International Council to control foreign relations, trade, wars, and raids, and The Royal Council to approve of both of their actions, and to balance them out. Not to say the councils didn’t help each other from time to time, but they all had a main job. The matriarch usually had control over the royal council, being the first royal government to prefer one gender over the other. This new nation strayed away from its warrior-like past, and began to settle. It was clear that Firen elementals were tired of the constant fighting, and just wanted to rest. Bino adopted this form of government, and abandoning the alliance with Jasu, it and Eternal Flame started a very controversial alliance called “The Peacekeepers” in -1,901. Their main goal was to keep peace in the world. The colony in Phoenix was still held under Firen control, and major cracks in this “Great Nation” began to show. Mass murderers and forced relocations had leaked into the media of other nations, heavily damaging the public image of the Eternal Flame. Its International Council spoke with harsh words, saying that the Watrish and Ballad elementals were “Impure” and “Unholy”, and they claimed to be cleansing them. It was clear they were just subjugating and indoctrinating the elementals. Their ally, Bino, was notably silent on the situation. 

Now, the world would begin to swing between peace and war, in a delicate balance of power and betrayal. In -1,850, The Peacekeepers fell under major threat of collapse. In Bino, 2 of the 3 councils became heavily isolationist and anti-Firen. The Royal Council’s Matriarch, Hyakia Dakti was incredibly war-like and hated Eternal Flame, and wanted to intervene in its Phoenix colonies, along with the International Council of Bino becoming isolationist and anti-Firen. Together, they started a movement in Bino that became known as “Taakenna Firen ”, which also became the name of this period of time. The group gained a massive following with the commonfolk, which was still against Eternal Flame due to the history between the two nations. Until, both Hyakia and The International Council leader were “mysteriously” assassinated, and Taakenna Firen was dissolved. Fingers were pointed at Eternal Flame, more notably Draanek Toral, a prince of the Firen Royal Council who publicly spoke out against The Peacekeepers, but he resisted these accusations. It was only in the year -1,792 did things calm down. Jasu and Icralem teamed up to ease tensions, knowing that a war between the two would split the world.

Then, in -1,761, the Icik civil war continued. Icralem launched a massive attack into Islem, plundering cities and killing hundreds. A crime against its own kind. Jasu and Eternal Flame demanded an end to the war. Eternal Flame, despite abandoning its Knightsdale, wanted to keep the Icik elementals weak, so kept siding with Icralem, despite its crimes. When Eternal Flame called for the help of Bino, Bino refused, claiming that the war was none of their business, and if Eternal Flame intervened, Bino would end The Peacekeepers. Unfortunately for Icralem, this was enough to get Eternal Flame to ban warriors traveling to assist either nation in the war, allowing Islem and Jasu to crush them in 4 months. The Icik elementals, now under an Imperium, would now enter a process of rebuilding. 
        For 500 years, there was a period called “Dyra Kaan”. It was a period where all nations isolated and developed, thanks to isolationism gaining a following in royal councils, explaining why the period lasted for around a generation. The Peacekeepers were somewhat forgotten, as both nations began to simply develop. Whilst the alliance wasn’t dissolved officially, it faded into the past. Down in The Far South, Islem’s rebuilding was sped up with Jasu’s support, creating a friendship between the two nations. The occupied Watlan and Ballad elementals still went without help, and Talkan held their isolation policy strong , managing to stay out of every foreign affair, and out of trouble with every other power. They even strengthened their isolation policy, banning all foreign media from entering the country. Many nations had major cities sprout, with Eternal Flame seeing Haar grow to be the first city in the world to reach three quarters of a million population in -1,773. The next city to reach the milestone, against all odds, was the Watlan city of Yjana, still in the Eternal Flame’s colonial holding. Even with the efforts to eradicate the Watlan and Ballad elementals, they were persistent in resistance. So, Eternal Flame began to target their culture and languages, and still, no one intervened. The two groups of elementals were granted citizenship in the colonies on the terms that they spoke Firen and followed Firen cultural norms & ways of society. Some agreed to these terms, others didn’t, but it was working. Then, in -1,731, Bino’s then capital, Aagala, reached the milestone that Haar and Yjana had many years before.


        Around the year -1,688 is when a period within Drya Kaan called “Izalia” began. It’s named after the city it started in, an Airren city with a decent population. * End of The Unit Era, and the beginning of The Modern Era
  
  

The Modern Era is marked by the institution of education being first introduced in Kalan, and becoming the mark of the year 0. The Modern Era is a time of mostly pure prosperity and innovation as nations around the world worked together to improve the lives of everyone, Xenophobia died, and everyone, instead of being seen for their element, was seen as Elemental. Democratic governments began to arise as Unitism slowly died out, and power shifted from militarism to innovation and education.

In Kalan in year 0, commonfolk and warriorfolk marched on the capital. Unitism was losing popularity, and in a historic agreement, they agreed to create a new government style, named a Haligen. Haligen roughly translates from Firen to mean “Protective Cloud”. Haligenism would be a style of government run by 5 different elected leaders, who led different parts of the nation. One for international affairs, one for internal, one for international relations, one for economic management, and finally, one to manage corruption, and make it impossible for Dajalmi to rise again. Even after 1000+ years, elementals still feared the influence that Dajalmi had. Kalan changed its name to Rellan. The march on the capital is marked as the beginning of the Firen Golden Age II. It was a time of commercial innovation, making life easier. Historians argue that this was the richest a nation had ever been. Centralizing the government after years of separation under Unitism actually allowed for the nation to work together sufficiently, causing an economic boom like never seen before. They also adopted a new economic policy, named  Insal. It was the first economic system to unite central government and commonfolk production into a team instead of a rivalry. Although, commonfolk had more control over production and distribution. Insal also led to the creation of 3 new classes. As commonfolk split into 2 different social classes, a new class owning most of the business and oftentimes the country became known as the Elite. The two commonfolk split into became known as the Impoverished and the Middling. The impoverished were often poor workers who were working in horrible conditions, often under-educated, and the middling were those who were doing well enough to make ends meet, and could often afford some luxuries. The elite were the upper class, rich, higher citizens that really controlled everything. Insal was quite unregulated, allowing for the elite to do as they pleased with their impoverished employees. Warriorfolk still remained as their own separate class to all 3 of these, but there were subdivisions, where most warriorfolk were considered middling warriorfolk. 

The first innovation was the “Sinlek” in 3. It's quite similar to the lightbulb on Earth. By harnessing Light val, the genius mind of Tublis Kajria had managed to illuminate entire buildings. Minor things that later became a part of everyday life were invented, like Injek, the refrigerator in 8, harnessed by Ice val. Then, there’s “Kinnlao”, the watch, in 11. Tublis Kajria later invented what Earth calls the compass in 19, as it helped him fly around Tulia to show off his inventions. Theater was also invented in Rellan, as entertainment for elementals usually revolved around arenas or public battles. Theater would provide a peaceful alternative to entertainment. The first show was held in 21, and it was about an elemental during Tulia Dajiao fighting against Daris crime families, somewhat spreading anti-Daris sentiment. This is what’s thought to have prevented Daris from getting the right to vote. They were allowed to settle in Rellan, but they had no voting rights. 



Peace was pretty much total. With Tramalen adopting Haligenism in 32, and Aeyak in 36, changing their name to Yaoa, and Narjai in 40, changing their name to The Sacreds, Haligenism was on the rise, and the economic alliance, changing their name from Unbion’s Haiyao to Tulia’s Haiyao, the innovation of the species was soon to take full effect. Tulia’s Haiyao passed a law named “Elementas Yaki”, which basically ended elemental segregation in the member nations. The argument behind its ending was the striving for the best possible education.
