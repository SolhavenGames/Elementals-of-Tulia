# Elementals - The Species
## What are Elementals? 
Elementals are an intelligent _Valoran_ inhabitants of the planet Tulia. They happen to be the smartest and strongest of the Valoran animal family, as well have having the most val by far. Elementals come in 15 different unique elements: Fire, Nature, Wood, Air, Ice, Snow, Rock, Metal, Sand, Lightning, Poison, Darkness, Light, Blood, and Water- just like other Valoran creatures. They have drawn their borders based on this difference, creating 13 different sovereign nations. 
## Elemental Energies
Elementals are made up of 3 main energies: **Yal**, **Jal**, and **Val**. All three of the energies are extremely important to their survival, with imbalances in the first two causing pain and illness. Each energy serves its own important purpose.

### ⦿ **Yal**
Yal is a purely physical substance, being more of a group. It is also not unique to Elementals- as Humans also carry Yal. Yal happens to be things like blood, white blood, and any other substance the body could produce without the assistance of Jal or Val. This energy is incredibly necessary and often considered the most important of the three, as without it, Elementals would not be able to survive. Like Humans, Yal is produced within Elemental bones, and flows through them from their heart. Low Yal can cause anemia-like symptoms, but high Yal is much worse, as it struggles to make room for other energies, leading to other, far worse problems.

### ⦿ **Jal**
Jal is considered the child to Val, as it happens to produce a weaker, more harmless form of the element that the Valoran wields. All Valorans carry this energy, including all Elementals. This is also the element that determines what Element the user has, inherited from the mother. But in rare cases, the Valoran can have two Elements, with Jal being split. This phenomenon is known as "Daris". This energy is very important and is used for things like architecture, artisanry, goods production, and personal luxury. Jal is produced as a bi-product of Val and Yal, which is why it appears more when Val has yet to be unlocked. If an Elemental has too much Jal, they may suffer from Yal shortages, and it could cause anemia-like symptoms. Low Jal can cause the Valoran's element to behave differently, causing what's known as "Val Betrayal" as the user's Val begins to fight against their body. It is incredibly fatal and painful, only cured by purging most Val from the user's body. 

### ⦿ **Val**
The pride and joy of every Valoran creature. Val is an incredibly unstable and dangerous energy that Valoran bodies have tamed with the assistance of Jal. In some Valorans, including (but not limited to) Elementals, Val does not start at full production at birth. Instead, the Valoran is required to do what's called Unlocking, which looks different in every Valoran. For Elementals, unlocking Val is a moment of meditation, clarity, and quiet. When locked, Val is still produced, but far less than it could be. It reacts with Yal to produce Jal, which will dominate the Valoran's element, making it mostly harmless and tame. When Val is unlocked, however, it begins to dominate the energy trifecta. Unlike the others, having too much or too little val is not an issue, as long as the other two are balanced, despite it having increased reactions with Yal. The body begins to balance itself out, slowing production of Yal to keep everything stable. Unlocked Val is dangerous, often being made illegal to do so if the Elemental is not in a military position. Val is often unpredictable and lingers, which causes Tulia's current problems with Val Pollution, as generations and generations of families have been using their elements with little care for the environment. Val is produced in a special, small organ below the Valoran stomach called the Valex, which also happens to store the Valoran's Val Bank, which allows their body to store extra val for stronger, more potent Element use.

Val is a direct energy inheritance from the Elements, i.e. the Valoran Gods, which is why it's so unstable. Due to this, it is impossible to recreate artifically, with all attempts ending in major disasters. It does happen to be a wonderful energy source, with Lithil (Lightning) Elementals creating a large economy using massive power banks. 

It's also known to be the Valoran Universe's "God Particle", forming the basis of mass, energy, and everything in between. This means that humans do *contain* it, they simply do not have it built into their biological systems. 

As for what unlocked Val can do- it is known to act as its own structural reinforcement lattice that can withstand ~28.7 GJ of energy, a feat achieved when the Elemental flies at top speeds, due to its ability to eat and redirect momentum away from the Valoran's body as a whole, and use it for more speed. This also works for deceleration, allowing the Elemental to slow down within seconds. Without flight, it can withstand far less- only ~11.9 GJ of energy. While the lattice is best for flight, it also happens to be useful for combat, allowing them to take stronger punches and even resist piercing attacks. Even with this, the lattice can wear down after enough strikes. Val also is known to preserve organs, redirecting force away from them. This causes a lot more damage to the skin of the Elemental, which has evolved overtime to heal quicker in response, also growing stronger. Hinted to earlier, it also smooths the environment around the elemental, preventing their incredible speed from causing too much damage. This also translates to temperature (Warm Elements do not melt as they should unless prompted by the user, same with cold elements and freezing). 

### ⦿ **Divine Val**
Divine Val, formerly considered just a myth, is a version of Val that is simply direct Valoran God energy. Not inheritance, not a copy, just direct energy from the Gods. One ounce of Divine Val is equal to ~671,091 ounces of val- though the power does not scale linearly. This trait alone makes it multitudes more unstable and strong than normal Val, but normal Valoran bodies and minds struggle to handle it, causing unintended and often negative side affects with the energy. The energy is so strong that the Gods who created it fear it, and recently, it has caused inter-god conflicts. When Divine Val is unlocked, it completely replaces normal val- but not everybody can handle the energy. Divine Val is known to tear people apart from the inside if their val bank is too low. 

With Divine Val being equal to around 671,000 ounces of Val, it can immediately seem terrifying- which it is. Compared to val's peak of ~28.7 GJ, Foribdden Val peaks at 312 PJ- and that's not even in flight. That is sheer energy output. At peak preformance, A single ounce of Divine Val contains 312 petajoules of metaphysical energy. This sheer amount of energy allows the Forbidden Elements to pull the insane feats they do, like Hellfire burning concepts, and Warshine reaching beyond lightspeed. Alongside this, it also allows the Elemental to project effects onto others- which is a major part of Forbidden Elements. Divine Val also inherets much of the abilities from normal unlocked Val, with its reinforcement lattice abilities being increased with the val, becoming denser and harder to pierce. However, when it IS broken, it shatters like glass, often wounding the attacker and the wielder of the lattice, injuring them with unstable and untamed versions of the Element the Divine Val is apart of. Its momentum conversion also protects organs better, also growing to protect skin. Finally, its environmental smoothing is enhanced, allowing for perfect precision with their own element at mastery. 

Divine Val is supplied through _Forbidden Elements_. It's usually required for Valorans to seek the element out, but some are born in the user, and others choose the users. When the Forbidden Element is unlocked, their power is split into gates. As the user progresses through gates, their Divine Val increases exponentially. Divine Val is also known to prevent sicknesses, improve life length, prevent and even reverse aging, and lower sustenance requirements. 

### ⦿ **Val Banks**
To hold the previous two energies, these creatures have Val Banks. They store their val there, which also makes it their most protected organ by val- it rarely ever being harmed in battle. The average Elemental val bank can store around 26.5-33.1 Liters of val. Anything below that is widely considered unhealthy, and anything above considered a feat. However, neither of those can handle Divine Val. To handle Divine Val, there is a prerequesite of a val bank of at least 681.12 Liters. However, Val Banks this high are impossible, so what's happening? Val banks are known to not execute at their maximum potential. Most val banks can actually average around 101.7 Liters, but it's an incredibly inefficient organ- the only real issue with Val. Divine Val unlocks the entire organ, allowing massive val banks. The preqequisite only requires 681.12 Liters, but it massively increases val banks to upwards of tens of thousands of liters. Only ~300 Elementals out of the 10 billion are capable of reaching that.
