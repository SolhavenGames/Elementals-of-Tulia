# Tulia - The Planet
## Tulia's History
Tulia is the sister planet to Gaea, built by the Elements out of pity of the human gods. They pretty much copied exactly what the human gods did with Earth- making a rocky Terra planet tilted on an axis, even being similar in size. The only major difference they had in the beginning was having 3 moons- one at night (Orbinus), one during the day (Kinnsi), and one that hovers mostly unpredictably (Tubliss). The planet now rests in between Mars and the Asteroid Belt, but originally, it hovered close to its sister planet. After a schism between the Human Gods and the Elements, Tulia was moved to its modern location- but it retained its traits of 28 hours in its days with 429 days in its years split evenly between 13 months (33 days per month). The months are as follows: Kinner, Morsh, Hulmin, Yurni, Polus, Junoy, Zneff, Tilik, Unity, Retti, Tulip, Venos, and Stemo, in that order.
