# Tulia - The Planet
## Tulia's History
Tulia is the sister planet to Gaea, built by the Elements out of pity of the human gods. They pretty much copied exactly what the human gods did with Earth- making a rocky Terra planet tilted on an axis, even being similar in size. The only major difference they had in the beginning was having 3 moons- one at night (**Orbinus**), one during the day (**Kinnsi**), and one that hovers mostly unpredictably (**Tubliss**). 

⦿ **Orbinus**

Orbinus is the night time moon of Tulia, shining in the night sky when the sun and Kinnsi sets. The sattilite has become a symbol of truth and honesty due to myths that falsify stories of visiting Elementals. Its population is ~4,500, as its odd rotations make it an unpopular place to live. It remains more of a scientific hub that Elementals in the field visit to study and explore space with telescopes.

⦿ **Kinnsi**

Kinnsi is Tulia's day moon, residing with the sun. It's name roughly translates to "Little Sun", never blocking out the sun due to its orbital tilt of 11 degrees. The moon has become a symbol of good luck, loyalty, and persistance. Approximately 2.1 million Elementals reside on this moon, being mostly Mettai elementals living in the international space.

⦿ **Tubliss**

Tubliss, the smallest of the three, and most unpredictable. It has become a figure of youth and joy, with many calling its odd orbits and rotations a sign of innocence. Its population constantly fluctuates as disasters happen often, but it usually resides around ~61,000 Elementals. 

## Tulia's History (continued)
The planet now rests in between Mars and the Asteroid Belt, but originally, it hovered close to its sister planet. After a schism between the Human Gods and the Elements, Tulia was moved to its modern location- but it retained its traits of 28 hours in its days with 429 days in its years split evenly between 13 months (33 days per month). The months are as follows: Kinner, Morsh, Hulmin, Yurni, Polus, Junoy, Zneff, Tilik, Unity, Retti, Tulip, Venos, and Stemo, in that order. 

## Tulia's Physicality
The planet has six major continents, being **North Unbion**, **South Unbion**, **Phoneic**, **Izrum**, **Herios**, and **The Far South**. 

⦿ **North Unbion** 

North Unbion is the largest continent on the planet, and home to the Firen and Naturine Elementals. It's western and eastern halves are split by a massive mountain range known as the Kallus Unim, being home to the tallest mountains on the entire planet. The western half of the continent is flatter, with a desert in the south (a part of the Desert Trifecta) with a terraformed part built by Naturine settlers many generations ago, being home to the major city Tigri. On the other side of the mountains stretches two major rivers, with the Naturine inhabiting a jungle known as the Sarwili Jungle.
