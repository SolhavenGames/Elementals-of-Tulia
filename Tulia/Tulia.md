# Tulia - The Planet
## Tulia's History
Tulia is the sister planet to Gaea, built by the Elements out of pity of the human gods. They pretty much copied exactly what the human gods did with Earth- making a rocky Terra planet tilted on an axis, even being similar in size. The only major difference they had in the beginning was having 3 moons- one at night (**Orbinus**), one during the day (**Kinnsi**), and one that hovers mostly unpredictably (**Tubliss**). 

### ⦿ **Orbinus**
Orbinus is the night time moon of Tulia, shining in the night sky when the sun and Kinnsi sets. The sattilite has become a symbol of truth and honesty due to myths that falsify stories of visiting Elementals. Its population is ~4,500, as its odd rotations make it an unpopular place to live. It remains more of a scientific hub that Elementals in the field visit to study and explore space with telescopes.

### ⦿ **Kinnsi**
Kinnsi is Tulia's day moon, residing with the sun. It's name roughly translates to "Little Sun", never blocking out the sun due to its orbital tilt of 11 degrees. The moon has become a symbol of good luck, loyalty, and persistance. Approximately 2.1 million Elementals reside on this moon, being mostly Mettai elementals living in the international space.

### ⦿ **Tubliss**
Tubliss, the smallest of the three, and most unpredictable. It has become a figure of youth and joy, with many calling its odd orbits and rotations a sign of innocence. Its population constantly fluctuates as disasters happen often, but it usually resides around ~61,000 Elementals. 

## Tulia's History (continued)
The planet now rests in between Mars and the Asteroid Belt, but originally, it hovered close to its sister planet. After a schism between the Human Gods and the Elements, Tulia was moved to its modern location- but it retained its traits of 28 hours in its days with 429 days in its years split evenly between 13 months (33 days per month). The months are as follows: Kinner, Morsh, Hulmin, Yurni, Polus, Junoy, Zneff, Tilik, Unity, Retti, Tulip, Venos, and Stemo, in that order. 

## Tulia's Continents
The planet has six major continents, being **North Unbion**, **South Unbion**, **Phoneic**, **Izrum**, **Herios**, and **The Far South**. 

### ⦿ **North Unbion** 
North Unbion is the largest continent on the planet, and home to the Firen, Naturine, and Woai Elementals. It's western and eastern halves are split by a massive mountain range known as the Kallus Unim, being home to the tallest mountains on the entire planet. The western half of the continent is flatter, with a desert in the south (a part of the Desert Trifecta) with a terraformed part built by Naturine settlers many generations ago, being home to the major city Tigri. The border between the Firen and Naturine have been drawn along the river for many years, with the north being flatter, holding most of the major Firen cities- including its capital. On the other side of the mountains stretches two major rivers, with the Naturine inhabiting a jungle known as the Sarwili Jungle. Above the border, inhabited by the Firen, rests a more hilly terrain, with less dense verdure, allowing for even more population. Overall, the continent's population is about 1.5 billion Elementals.

### ⦿ **South Unbion** 
South Unbion has historically only been inhabited by the Airren Elementals. It happens to be one of the smallest continents on the planet, only being larger than The Far South. Physically, it's more of a large valley, allowing for winds to flow through the continent's plains easily. It has two major hill ranges, one in the North and the other in the South, where the Airren practice their religion. On the western most edge of the continent rests their own part of the Desert Trifecta, a small vertical deserct controlling the coast. Not many live there, and it has bad ties to histroy, being linked to Airren slaughters of Sakai Elementals early in the lifetime of their civilization. As for the rest of the continent, it is hardly much more than plains that allows the wind to flow through perfectly, breeding beautiful soft yet quick breezes every day. With all this considered, Wrenao has a population of 850 million Elementals.

### ⦿ **Herios** 
Herios is the third largest continent on the planet, along with being the most diverse terrain and culture wise. With a vast cluser of mountains and hills in the southwest, stormy and wet terrain in the north, and dry and dense desert in the southeast, Herios is often called The Heart of Tulia due to its location and its representation of almost all Tulian biomes. Beginning with its southwest- it holds densely packed mountains and hills with thin rivers falling from the peaks, flowing out to the coastline of the continent. The Roccian and Melai Elementals live in the nation Delekt together here- but it's mostly Roccian. In the North, the stormy and rainy flat lands with rivers flowing from the mountains and hills to its south. Other than that, it's mostly flat- but very fertile. Stalto and the Lithil Elementals live in this land, using the empty space to build power banks and sell energy. In the southwest rests the largest desert in the Desert Trifecta, which is inhabited by the Sakai Elementals in Bunnis. Sandy, dusty, dry, and hilly, the land is hard to sustain a nation on- but Bunnis has done so for many generations. All together, the continent has a population of 2.9 billion- the most populous continent on the planet.
 
### ⦿ **Phoneic** 
The second largest continent, Phoneic, is home to the Ballad and Watrish Elementals. It is home to the worlds largest Rainforest, known as the Kial Rainforest. It also happens to be the most dense, but has a lush river flowing through, fertilizing the land. Further east from the Kial Rainforest rests a steady plains with little to no hills. In the North, to the east of Kial, stretches a forest that climbs onto islands and hills in the area. It also stretches downwards, across a river. Its hills expand downward through the continent, building a natural barrier that has been used to dictate borders between the Ballad and Watrish rivals. At the southern tip rests a small swampy area. stick, musky, and dirty- mostly avoided, but some Watrish Elementals prefer to practice their religion there. Ralia, the nation of the Ballad, The continent has a population of 1.9 billion Elementals.

### ⦿ **Izrum** 
Home to the longest ongoing war in Tulian history, Izrum is a continent that's rarely seen peace. Being home to the Poisi, Litti, and Darrekin Elementals, none of them are known for their peaceful nature, which explains why the continent is riddled with war. It also has split the factions perfectly, with the Poisi border with the other two nations being drawn well on hills, rainforests, and swamps, making wars between the Poisi and the Litti or Darrekin gruesome and slow. However, the border between the Litti and Darrekin is almost entirely flat- allowing the two to run at each other endlessly, which they have been doing for generations. Other than a small desert in the south east of the continent, no other major terrain affects it. The Littl and Darrekin nations Sharlin and Dalino have been at war since they were founded, while the Poisi nation Tixolam has been dealing with their turmoil since it began. This creates an odd triangular rivalry, where they all wish to subdue the other. The population of Izrum is 2.1 billion. 

### ⦿ **The Far South** 
The coldest and smallest continent on the planet, The Far South is home to the sister cultures of the Icik and Snai. In its very center rests a massive range of icy and snowy mountains, almost impossible for any non-native to explore. Its rough terrain is unwelcoming to many, but some still come and attempt to conquer the heights by climbing to the peaks. Outside of the ranges, the continent is mostly flat- prone to many blizzards and storms. Though, the residents have grown used to the weather- it has become apart of their daily life. The culture molds around the cold weather and icy continent, allowing cities to extend deep into the snowy wasteland, where other cultures could not settle. Some even extend into the mountains, where many go to hide out, camp, grow closer to their element, or just explore. The Icik nation Fraklen, alongside the Snai nation Swalien, live on this continent together. They have never fought against each other in war, instead standing side-by-side throughout history. The population of this continent is 1.3 billion.

## Tulia - Val Pollution
Val Pollution is a growing issue that Tulia faces after many generations have been using their val unrestricted. Due to the nature of Val being unpredictable, hard to control, and quick to detach from its user, lots of val now floats around through the atmosphere, causing unpredictable and dangerous weather which will be listed below. 

### ⦿ **Ash Blizzards** 
Ash blizzards are exactly what they sound like- blizzards, but instead of ice, it's ash and char. This disaster is dangerous, as when they happen, an animal caught in them can struggle to breath as the ash chokes them of oxygen and fills their lungs. With the sharp ash flying at high speeds, it also happens to do major damage to structures and can cut animals who manage to survive the suffocation. 

### ⦿ **Poison Fog**
Incredibly deadly, sneaky, and sometimes invisible fog of pure toxins falling directly from the sky. Poison Fog is a major issue that pushed for innovations to ventilation to prevent the fog from sneaking into buildings. It's exactly as it sounds- a fog that is deadly to breathe in. Some are more merciful, and some are incredibly deadly. This disaster has its own measurement of danger, called the PF range (1-6, 1 is mostly harmless, 6 is deadly). The average Poison Fog is around 4.3, which is deadly after about 5.5 minutes of breathing it in.

### ⦿ **Blood Rain** 
A harmless rain of blood from the sky, replacing the water. This disaster is more inconvenient than anything as it stains fabrics and sometimes buildings. 

### ⦿ **Wind Tsunami** 
A terrible invasion of incredibly fast winds that can sometimes even topple buildings. Winds roll in at upwards of 250mph, striking through valleys, streets, and tunnels with unrelenting strength, knocking anyone who it catches off-guard down to their feet. Luckily, these storms are somewhat predictable.

### ⦿ **Fire Tornado** 
A tornado of flames that loves to target cold and wooded areas. The Fire Tornado appears without warning, slowly swirling before growing to its maximum size, travelling for a few minutes to a few hours, then dispersing. It can cause burns, destroy buildings, and ravage countrysides. It's often followed by Ash Blizzards.

### ⦿ **Light / Darkness Destabilization**
This disaster will ruin the lightwaves in the area for a few minutes. Light bounces oddly, sometimes not bouncing at all, causing dark patches. The worst of all is that this disaster is completely impossible to predict. Sometimes, it can just prevent light from bouncing at all, causing moments of total pitch black darkness in an area. Other times, it can change colors of light, causing everything to appear brighter and even blinding.

### ⦿ **Tectonic Falters**
Val in the air seeps into the ground often, causing tremors, quakes, and sinkholes where they usually wouldn't be- and being much stronger than usual. Though the tremors and quakes only last for a few seconds, the sheer force of them can cause concussions. 

### ⦿ **Extreme Overgrowth**
Within a few seconds, years of verdure grows. Vines curl up buildings. Trees sprout on roads. Grass and dirt breaks through concrete. Foundations of buildings age and rust. There have been rare cases of animals being caught by the rapid growth, getting wrapped in vines or stuck in trees that sprouted right below them.

### ⦿ **Moisture Attraction**
Water seems to pull closer to other water sources. This can create weird moments where massive drops of floating water rests in the middle of roads. While it's usually harmless, it sometimes can trap animals within its water bubbles, and the water bubbles have been reported to chase those who happen to have chase Valorans and wrap around their heads to suffocate them.

### ⦿ **Flash Desert**
Out of nowhere, the ground becomes covered in sand and dust. It all rushes in within a few minutes, and it can take days to clean. Many have drowned in the sudden influx of sand, causing some cities to build anti-Flash Desert walls.
