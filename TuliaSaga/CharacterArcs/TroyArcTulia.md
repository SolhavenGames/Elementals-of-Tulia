# Troy Talona | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_We'd die for Earth, but they will die first._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A relatively popular Left-Wing Technocratic Unionist (someone believing in the unity of Earth/Gaea under one state, similar to the days of the Terra Union).

**Ending State**: A man who seized more power than sanctioned, but is now out of arms reach of anyone who could bureaucratically stop him.  

**Inciting Losses**: Elementals arriving on Earth/Gaea was all he needed to hear to be radicalized against them. 

**Irreversable Actions**: Multiple: Rebranding over the Elemental Integration Initiative, and funding the Knights of Gaea, showed he was radically anti-elemental. 

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Being the fuel behind Humanity's side of the Burning of Gaea.
## Summary 
Troy Talona, a name that believed in human supremacy over everything. A loud Unionist before Elementals arrived, he'd speak proudly of the prosperity of humanity under the Terra Union and call for its return, using fiery language against those who disagreed. These early actions allowed him to pursue a career in politics, where he first became a regional leader in his nation's (Military Democracy of Oceania) council. Then, he went larger- shooting directly for the Global Government- the ADS, joining as a representative for his nation. When the first Elementals arrived, he was outraged. Immediately, he called it an invasion, working with people like Samae to begin rapidly spreading anti-Elemental propaganda. In fact, he helped her found the Knights of Gaea, supplying the name among other things, choosing the name because he knew of the significance of "Knights" (Knightsdales the Elementals were fleeing from) and how they called the planet "Gaea", making a name directly pointed at the species. Though he did not join openly, he was a very proud supporter of the cause. When more Elementals arrived, and he noticed the global government only kneeling, he decided to take matters into his own hands. Using his charisma, he quickly climbed the ranks and became the leader of the Elemental Integration Initiative, rebranding it to the Elemental Research Initiative. There, he began to prosecute, study, and even kill thousands of Elementals. This indirectly influences Imbra to start her crusade, which begins the Burning of Gaea. Troy begins to deploy robotic units known as H.U.M.A.N. units (Humanoid Unit for Mitigation, Armor, and Nullification) to combat them. His final action would be the mass killing of hundreds of Elementals from Zezri's refugee group, solidifying him as a staunch anti-Elemental figure.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **E-Year 2010**
- An eleven year old child, Troy lives in a quite peaceful continent which prides itself on the fact.
- Troy grows up in a staunch Unionist household, causing him to also align with the ideology himself.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2014**
- Troy becomes a semi-popular figure for his outspoken and unfiltered mindset, attracting Unionists and those adjacent to his ideology.
- He begins focusing on Political Science and History in school, joining a specialization program in his first year of highschool.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2015**
- Troy wins a seat in his school's Student Government, holding that seat throughout his entire highschool career.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2018**
- Troy graduates highschool.
- He was voted "Most likely to conquer the world", voted by his peers.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2019**
- Supported by his faimly, who have their own political careers, Troy Talona immediately gets into politics. Using his popularity among Unionist groups, he immediately dominates the party.
- The Unionist party starts relatively unpopular in his country, but his speeches and demeanor give them much needed attention.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2020** 
- The first Elementals arrive on the planet- Troy is suspicious of them, but it is not a political issue, so he does not speak out publicly.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2021**
- Troy's Unionist Party begins winning seats in his country's parliament- the first time it has happened since the parliament was founded.
- Troy wants to run for the executive office of his country, but cannot due to lacking military experience.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2023**
- Someone jokingly suggests Troy join the ADS as Oceania's representative, but he takes it seriously.
- Troy joins the race later than the other candidates.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2024**
- Despite pollings, Troy wins the elections with 58% of the popular vote. Opposing parties accuse the Unionists of electoral fraud, but accusations stop after investigations prove otherwise.
- Troy, eager to join, arrives a few days early to meet his new peers.
- Troy takes interest in the EII. Though he still hasn't spoken publicly about Elementals, he does still heavily distrust them.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2025**
- Troy speaks out against publicly for the first time. The language he uses is modest, though he hints at stronger emotions. This speech generates light backlash.
- Despite this, Troy is still re-elected by his people.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2026**
- Troy is officially assigned to the EII, making him a permanent member of the ADS. He no longer requires re-election to keep his power.
- Immediately, he notices weaknesses within the power structures and bureaucracy. He immediately aims to climb and rise the ranks.
- Troy meets Is'ale | This interaction further convinced Troy that Elementals did not belong on the planet, as he felt Is'ale was being silently rude and passive aggressive.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2027**
- Troy continues to rise quickly, now speaking about Elementals very often. Though, he hardly gets much screentime outside of his home country and small rural villages.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2028**
- Troy uses a new approach, blaming Elementals for spikes in unexplained crime and climate changes.
- This approach gives him viewership in coastal and urban areas, though he's still mostly seen as an extremist, with supporters of co-existance calling him crazy.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2030**
- Troy rises to a powerful role in the EII, giving him his first batch of power above independent nations. Though, these powers heavily rely on the legitimacy of the ADS.
- Troy plays his cards carefully- listening to Elemental Specialists, agreeing with moderate refuge policies, and befriending his employees.
- Using his connections, he slowly builds legitimacy for himself to take a stronger role in the Institution.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2031**
- Troy gets another promotion, becoming the second most powerful person in the EII. Elections for the strongest position are not up for another 2 years. 
- He begins building support, appealing to moderates who only support temporary refuge, and normalizing anti-Elemental sentiment within the EII.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2033**
- Troy becomes the head of the EII, immediately renaming it from the Elemental Integration Initiative to the Elemental Research Initiative.
- He immediately begins prosecuting Elementals in nations that will allow him, though he does it behind the scenes and in the shadows.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- When the Burning of Wuhan happens, he immediately rallies the world behind him.
- The ADS grants him emergency powers, giving him near total control over all military and defense spendings of the Global Government.
- Troy meets Samae | Him and Samae get along well, and he calls her and her organization wonderful people. He also grants them funds using his newly granted powers.
- Troy pulls his funding back once he finds out the Knights of Gaea have begun attacking humans.
- Troy immediately begins investing in robotic soldiers to fight against Elementals, alongside a new weapon called "Spear Cannons", the only known thing to pierce the Elemental Val Lattice.
- Spear Cannons are quickly rolled out and mass-produced, being the first genuine resistance Humanity puts up.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Troy, making patterns through attacks of Imbra and Adva, correctly predicts that New York Super City is the next target for the duo.
- He sends a pre-emptive force to help defend the city. They all know they may die, but they know the power of the message they will send.
- After NYSC, Troy gives a speech encouraging humanity to stay united and strong.
- Due to his prediction, the ADS gives him even more power, giving him near total legislative control over the organization.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- The first android prototypes come off of the assembly lines- though they are total failures.
- Troy begins technologically augmenting his own soldiers, allowing them to fight Elementals- though their success rate is ~1% among Low Val Bank Elementals, and 0% with anything above.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Elections are suspended in the ERI as Troy is seen as the only person who can save humanity.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- When Zezri's Underground Society arrives, he immediately imprisons most of them. Some escape.
- When he sees Kael, he gets an idea- realizing that Kael was with Imbra and Adva from data of them being the only trio with a Firen, Airren, and Snai.
- He concludes that something happened to Kael causing the other two to commit the crimes they did.
- Troy decides to try to lure the two to Berlin-A by using Kael as bait, hoping to capture one of them.
