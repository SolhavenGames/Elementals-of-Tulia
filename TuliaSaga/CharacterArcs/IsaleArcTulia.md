# Is’ale Minsi | Tulia Arc Journey
<sub> **_Freedom for my people, at any cost._** <sub>
## Structure 
**Starting State**: A staunch anti-Knightsdale protester in Rutt (Naturine Nation). 

**Ending State**: Joining Terkou to make Earth/Gaea the perfect Elemental Pluralist Democracy.

**Inciting Losses**: Taking a major injury to his abdomen to save many of his fellow fighters. This causes him to be honorably discharged, but also increases his val bank substantially.

**Irreversable Actions**: Becoming as outspoken as he was caused him to be a target for Knightsdales later.

**Biggest Arc Influence**: Giving insight to fighters in Dal yon Tulia.
## Summary 
Is'ale is a strong believer in the democratic processes of Tulia. When the Knightsdale movements first began, he quit his job and began a campaign in his nation to subdue any such movement before it could even begin. This worked beautifully, causing little to no Knightsdale action in Rutt, the nation of the Naturine. Unfortunately for him, neighboring nations like New Iris and Wor, changing their names to Irision and Wrenao respectively, now seeing him as a major target for removing a potential ally. His pleas to the Rutt Government to intervene in International Knightsdale Movements were quickly ignored, though videos of them grew rapidly popular around the free world. When Dal yon Tulia broke out, he immediately enrolled in the military, fighting on the frontlines as a brave soldier to hold back the evils of Knightsdale from taking his home. Being so popular and on the front lines was a downside, though, as many of his enemies would aim for him. This worked one day, brutally wounding him and causing him to be honorably discharged by Rutt. The Knightsdales were not done with him, though. They began sending assassins after him, seeing his mere existance as an insult. Due to this, Rutt began plotting to send him to Earth/Gaea for safety. This plan eventually succeeded, where Is'ale initially cooperated with the authority. When Earth/Gaea grew hostile, he went his own ways- and eventually ran into Terkou, who began preaching his ideology to him, wrapping him slowly into his ideology.
## Timeline
### ⦿ **T-Year 900**
- Is'ale is a part of a government advisory group that has noticed the recent rise of the Knightsdale ideology, and is urging the government of Rutt to do something about it. Him and his group are ignored.
- When the Firen fall to Knightsdale, his group becomes more "I told you so" and begins pushing harder. The government of Rutt still mostly ignores them, though some representatives begin to hear them out.
  
### ⦿ **T-Year 901**
- Once the horrors of Irision begin being leaked to the rest of the world, Is'ale pushes harder, desperate to free the Firen people. His government support is still weak.

### ⦿ **T-Year 902**
- Knightsdale Sympathisers get elected to Rutt's government, though they have very few seats, leading to relatively no power.
- Though, the new Knightsdale Sympathisers manage to have all Anti-Knightsdale groups locked out of government. Is'ale's group begins to plead with the public instead.

### ⦿ **T-Year 904**
- Thanks to Is'ale's campaigning, the public turns against Knightsdales and radicalizes to fight against them. Though, no elections will be held for another few years, leaving them stuck with the static, non-interventionalist government.

### ⦿ **T-Year 905**
- Trying to hold onto their power, representatives in Rutt approve of an Anti-Knightsdale alliance with remaining Democratic nations.
- Is'ale and his group, now turned political party, still yells for more.

### ⦿ **T-Year 908**
- A new election secures plenty of seats for Is'ale's party, though he does not run himself, remaining more of a spokesperson for the public eye.
- Dal yon Tulia breaks out shortly after, and Is'ale immediately enlists in the military. He fights on the border of Irision, defending Naturine cities, and becoming a figure of Naturine Resolve.

### ⦿ **T-Year 910**
- Is'ale is a part of an invasion into Irision, which boosts public perception of the war, formerly growing hopeless.

### ⦿ **T-Year 912**
- Is'ale is brutally wounded, causing him to be honorably discharged by Rutt. The injury notably increases his val bank heavily. 
- He retakes his job as an anti-Knightsdale speaker.
- While giving a speech, he narrowly dodges an assassination attempt. This causes the government to begin plots to get him off the planet, to protect him.

### ⦿ **T-Year 913**
- Is'ale begins his journey to Earth/Gaea, guarded and led by Naturine Soldiers through the vacuum of space.
- The people of Rutt have a celebration for him before he leaves.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2026**
- Is'ale arrives to Earth/Gaea.
- The Naturine Soldiers explain his importance to their nation, and the Global Government on Earth/Gaea (ADS) decides to treat him with luxury due to this.
- He is given a nice home in the rainforests of the Republican Federal State of Sector Iota on the island of Java.
- Some humans come to visit him, his story spreading among journalists and government officials. Though, he considers them irritating, as they treat him as if he doesn't understand anything.
- Is'ale meets Troy | At this time, Troy had moderate amounts of power, but wasn't strong enough to push his anti-elemental agenda. This meeting with him alone pushed Is'ale to distrust humanity more.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2034**
- When humanity grows hostile towards Elementals, Is'ale secretly unlocks Biota.
- The Burning of Gaea alongside the very public prosecution of Elementals causes him to flee his home to Antarctica, where he believes he will be safer.
- He brings rations with him, hoping to hide there for a while before he has to return.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2035**
- Is'ale meets Terkou | Terkou approaches Is'ale, first offering shelter and food. Then he begins to indoctrinate him against Humanity.
- Is'ale begins to listen to and align with Terkou, agreeing with his end goal of a united Elemental Democracy on Earth/Gaea.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2036**
- Is'ale fully integrates in Terkou's 'colony', hiding out and waiting for their time to strike.
- Is'ale meets Teira | He assists Terkou in radicalizing the young Forbidden User, preaching community and love.
### ⦿ **E-Year 2037**
- Is'ale meets Aeri | Is'ale doesn't like Aeri immediately, but he tries to tolerate them.

### ⦿ **E-Year 2038**
- Is'ale meets Zero | The lost soul makes him uncomfortable, but he tries to give Zero some company and affection.
- Is'ale, having spent so much time alongside Terkou, now believes wholeheartedly in the idea of an Elemental Democracy on Earth/Gaea, and is willing to kill to get there.
