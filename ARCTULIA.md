# TULIA
## ⦿ Collapse of the Free World
As radical movements promoting nationalism and xenophobia conquer powerful nations, the entire world of Tulia is shaken by a drastic shift in power, leading to fears of losing freedoms and mass loss of life.
## ⦿ Dal yon Tulia 
The inevitable war breaking out, leading to mass death, destruction, and terror. Millions are killed or displaced by the war, including major characters. The conflict will drag through the entire arc, though it’s not the main focus.
## ⦿ Rise of Forbidden Users
Combatants in the war (Specifically Tixolam, the Knightsdale Nation of Poison Elementals) will begin experimenting with the power of Forbidden  Elements, leading to a special task force of Bane users. Forbidden Elements like Warshine (in Aeri) and Hellfire (in Imbra) will not be caused by this, but will still be counted as a part of this event.
## ⦿ Great God Conflict
Directly caused by the rise of Forbidden Users, the Gods fall into conflict. Discovery of two users- Hellfire and Warshine (in Aeri and Imbra alike),
will lead to exploding tensions causing Gods to scramble to ensure their people survive Dal yon Tulia. 
## ⦿ Zezri’s Underground Society
Unity in difference and fear, fighting to survive together. External threats (Tixolam) will push more to join her in her underground resistance, until
they eventually lead a full-blown revolt.
## ⦿ Tixolam’s Forbidden Accident
Tixolam, growing ambitious with their Forbidden Element studies, slip up- and hundreds of Forbidden Users escape. The worst of which being test subjects of Death, who end up spreading the element, causing a global pandemic.
## ⦿ Dal yon Tulia: Forbidden
Nations all around the world shift focus to the massive problem of roaming Forbidden users on Tulia. Commanded by their gods, they hunt and kill any they can find, causing alliances and rivalries alike to blur temporarily as all focus on the new enemy.
## ⦿ Mass Refugees Flee to Earth
Running from war, many will flee the planet to Earth (what they call Gaea) for safety, only to be met with growing hostilities on the planet. Though there are three massive groups who flee, the last one (led by Zezri) happens to end the Tulia Arc.

# EARTH
## ⦿ First Refugee Arrivals
In reaction to Dal yon Tulia, many fled the planet as soon as possible. They mostly arrived in waves, surprising Earth each time. In response, Earth’s global government created an institution to help ensure Elementals were processed, documented, and watched.
## ⦿ Troy’s Resistance
Taking over established institutions, Troy will lead a massive anti-elemental campaign on Earth, contributing to the radicalizations of Elementals and Humans alike, further widening the canyon between the two species. 
## ⦿ Imbra’s Crusade
The start of the Human-Elemental Conflict (name WIP lol, i’ll pick a better one later). After blaming humanity for the loss of her love (Kael) to Knightsdale soldiers, she attacks major cities on Earth using her Forbidden Element, Hellfire, causing massive damage and gaining the alias “Phoenix” due to her sometimes visible wings of fire and ranged attack tactics. Her friend Adva will join her in this conflict, gaining the alias “Tempest” due to her massive wind speeds and incredible mobility & combat. 
## ⦿ Terkou’s Radicalizations 
Pushes the narrative to Humanity that Elementals are inherently evil, radicalizing more against them. However, it also pushes Elementals to follow the narrative, as humans will only declare them aligned. 
## ⦿ Samae Unlocks Pure
Using self-experimentation, Samae becomes the first and only Human to unlock Pure. She extracts val from dead elementals to implant in herself, causing massive amounts of pain and illness as her body rejected it, but cybernetic augments kept her alive.
## ⦿ Deployment of HUMAN Units
Troy, utilizing his growing power, spends hundreds of billions on an experimental android campaign, directly built to counter Elementals. Early deployment of these units are subpar, allowing Elementals to defeat them easily, but proving both Troy’s and Terkou’s points. (Troy = “Elementals are too strong”, Terkou = “Humans are out to get us”.)
## ⦿ Forbidden Refugee Arrival
Led by Zezri, hundreds of Forbidden Users will arrive on Earth, including most of the major character cast. When they arrive, they cooperate with Troy’s ERI, leading to most of them dying under his watch.
