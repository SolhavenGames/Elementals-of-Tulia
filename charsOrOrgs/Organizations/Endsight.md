# ENDSIGHT - RADICAL DEMOCRATIC ELEMENTAL SUPREMACISTS
<sub>_"No elemental should kneel to any other creature. Ever."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
Endsight is an extremist Elemental Psuedo-Cult that believes in Elemental supremacy to humanity. Still retaining the cultural democratic stance to many Elementals, it aims to colonize/"claim" Gaea for the species and kick out, subjugate, or even kill Humans to make it a second Elemental home, with the end goal of one large united Elemental nation under a strong, powerful Democracy for Elementals to thrive under. However it currently is far from that goal, being a small radical terrorist organization living deep in the shadows, but with connections that can be found nearly everywhere. It's deeply entrenched in society, with many radical Elementals actually following its ideology in secret.

Endsight often sees spikes in membership when Valorankind on Gaea comes under threat that seems impossible to address conventionally. Many of the members of Endsight happen to look up to its leader as if he is some sort of figure of divinity, with them refusing to use his name, simply referring to him as "The One". In fact, he is considered to be so divine, saying his name in public risks eyes being cast upon you...

## STRUCTURE
Endsight lacks a definite structure. Instead, its leader, [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md), directly commands its four Forbidden User members. Then, due to isolation of the leader, the rest often command themselves in self-inspired terrorist attacks. However, it is known that there are a few "high ranking" non-forbidden user elementals that do deliver commands when the leader demands what's called a "Terrorism Surge", or forges plans that exploit weaknesses. This structure, while not intentional, is what allowed for the cult-like culture to form in the organization. It also allows for those less connected Elementals to radicalize further (or the opposite), splintering into different, unique groups...

The structure of its Forbidden User task force, however, is strong. [Aeri Glem](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/AeriArcTulia.md) is its damage-dealer. Its hardest striker. Its quickest soldier.  [Isale Minsi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/IsaleArcTulia.md) is its leader and damage sponge, also creating lots of ground on the battlefield. [Teira Kni-Soa](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TeiraArcTulia.md) is its logistics and positional domination. She sometimes doubles as a scout as her Forbidden Element allows her to see what her eyes cannot. [Zero](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/ZeroArcTulia.md) is its silent, loyal assassin. These three together build a dangerous, terrifying force to be reckoned with, only matched by groups of other Forbidden Users.
## GOALS
Early Tulia Arc Goals - House Elementals, Represent Elementals

In this time, Endsight was not a terrorist organization. It was even run through the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) as it paved the way for Elementals to live on the planet. While Elemental populations on the planet were low at this time, the few who were present were quickly given homes. 

Middle Tulia Arc Goals - Reorganize, Repurpose

Endsight, after [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md) essentially took the organization and bent it to his own, new will, was now reorganizing and repurposing itself to fit his new ideology. Since he's fled to Antarctica, a barren continent, he struggles to find members, but some who share his vision stay close. 

Late Tulia Arc / Early Gaea Arc Goals - Spread Terror, Inspire Uprisings, Conquer Humanity

After recruiting [Adva Anil](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/AdvaArcTulia.md) and [Imbra Hayes](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/ImbraArcTulia.md) when he found them both distressed and full of hatred, Terkou points them as weapons to begin the Burning of Gaea. When he defects from them, however, he's forced to find his four new members, training them and being sure to not make the same mistake he did with the last two...

Middle Gaea Arc Goals - Spread Terror, Solidify Elemental Gains, Get the Val Bomb

Endsight has solidified itself as a terrifying power with four willing Forbidden Users under its wing. However, they could be scarier. They want to do more. They want to push more. They need more fear in both Humans and Elementals so that Elementals can finally push to conquer the entire planet. When they hear about the Val Bomb, they decide they absolutely need it at any costs. It could be the key to destroying Humanity once and for all.

Late Gaea Arc / Early Xalii Arc Goals - Spread Terror, Weaken Humanity, Buy Time

With the Val Bomb not being secured, Endsight falls back to the possibility that conventional warfare may have to be enough. They begin to weaken humanity through the same tactics they always used: Terrorist attacks on large major cities, which went well during the Terror Plan, strategically attacking when their strongest enemies were occupied with destroying the death cult, [The Whispers](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/TheWhispers.md). When their Forbidden Users disappear into the Spirit Realm, they switch towards buying time for them to return. They can't let their enemies know they're weakened. 
## BACKSTORY
Endsight was originally founded as an extention of the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) and its intentions to assist Elementals finding a home on the planet. Being led by both [Kterla Naomi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md) and [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md), the two became the official faces of Elementals on the planet.

However, when [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md) eventually decided that Humanity was insultingly weak, and that Gaea belonged to Elementals, he distorted Endsight into the terroristic and hateful organization it's known as today.
