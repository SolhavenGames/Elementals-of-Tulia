# ENDSIGHT - RADICAL DEMOCRATIC ELEMENTAL SUPREMACISTS
<sub>_"No elemental should kneel to any other creature. Ever."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
Endsight is an extremist Elemental Psuedo-Cult that believes in Elemental supremacy to humanity. Still retaining the cultural democratic stance to many Elementals, it aims to colonize/"claim" Gaea for the species and kick out, subjugate, or even kill Humans to make it a second Elemental home, with the end goal of one large united Elemental nation under a strong, powerful Democracy for Elementals to thrive under. However it currently is far from that goal, being a small radical terrorist organization living deep in the shadows, but with connections that can be found nearly everywhere. It's deeply entrenched in society, with many radical Elementals actually following its ideology in secret.

Endsight often sees spikes in membership when Valorankind on Gaea comes under threat that seems impossible to address conventionally. Many of the members of Endsight happen to look up to its leader as if he is some sort of figure of divinity, with them refusing to use his name, simply referring to him as "The One". In fact, he is considered to be so divine, saying his name in public risks eyes being cast upon you...

## STRUCTURE
Endsight lacks a definite structure. Instead, its leader, [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TerkouArcTulia.md), directly commands its four Forbidden User members. Then, due to isolation of the leader, the rest often command themselves in self-inspired terrorist attacks. However, it is known that there are a few "high ranking" non-forbidden user elementals that do deliver commands when the leader demands what's called a "Terrorism Surge", or forges plans that exploit weaknesses. This structure, while not intentional, is what allowed for the cult-like culture to form in the organization. It also allows for those less connected Elementals to radicalize further (or the opposite), splintering into different, unique groups...

The structure of its Forbidden User task force, however, is strong. [Aeri Glem](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/AeriArcTulia.md) is its damage-dealer. Its hardest striker. Its quickest soldier.  [Isale Minsi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/IsaleArcTulia.md) is its leader and damage sponge, also creating lots of ground on the battlefield. [Teira Kni-Soa](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/TeiraArcTulia.md) is its logistics and positional domination. She sometimes doubles as a scout as her Forbidden Element allows her to see what her eyes cannot. [Zero](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/ZeroArcTulia.md) is its silent, loyal assassin. These three together build a dangerous, terrifying force to be reckoned with, only matched by groups of other Forbidden Users.
