# KNIGHTS OF GAEA - STRIKING FEAR INTO EVERY ENEMY... AND ALLY
<sub>_"Earth is for humans."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
The Knights of Gaea is a heavily militarized group of humans with technological augmentations. It's led solely by one person, [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md), the only Human Forbidden Element user and the most mechanically augmented human on the planet. After being recruited by and eventually orchestrating a coup of the [Association of Democratic States](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md), the Knights of Gaea was thrown into a position to unleash its ideological will across the entire globe, something they wasted no time doing.

In such a position of power, [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) wasted no time in declaring war on every single Elemental on the planet, while silently killing and subduing humans that didn't agree with her ideology. Her end goal? Total, unrelenting control over Humanity and Earth as its sole dictator. In her future, the people don't just bow to her. They worship her, with no other option. For now, she has other obstacles to go through...

## STRUCTURE
Much like her desired ideology for the rest of the planet, the Knights of Gaea is run like a strict dictatorship with [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) and her closer, higher ranking members issuing strict orders that have deadly consequences if they're not followed. Its structure is incredibly unfair, with only about 20 higher-ups that still answer to [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md), and everyone else is forced to follow thoughtlessly, or face immediate execution. This, obviously, has caused a few dissidents in her lower ranks, but they, like some of her puppets in the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md), practice a silent obedience. 

Among those higher-ups, they act almost like warlords. Not all of them are based with [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) in Wuhan-Nanjing, many are elsewhere, like New York Super City. This is mainly to extend the grip of the entire organization to be globally present and threatening forces, though it does happen to spread their forces thin at times. It is also important to note that the Knights are not some professional force, and mostly operate using recruits as many of its soldiers are thrown into losing battles as meat shields to overwhelm the enemies, which is why their numbers are so low. 
## GOALS
Middle Tulia Arc Goals - Grow, Secure Power, Prevent Elemental-Friendly Nations

When it was founded, the Knights of Gaea had a simple goal: Prevent Elementals from finding homes on the planet to force them to go somewhere else. [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) had long been outspoken about the species, and when she was 17, she quickly took up the mantle of preventing them from staying by any means necessary. The Knights of Gaea quickly became the most funded underground organization in the world.

Late Tulia Arc / Early Gaea Arc Goals - Terrorize Elemental Nations, Hunt other Forbidden Users

While the Burning of Gaea is in full swing, the organization begins to operate through terrorism to frame Elementals and begin conflicts. Many nations were already hateful to Elementals, and it was easy to push them to try and end them all. However, this usually just led to stronger Elemental uprisings, and recruitment opportunities for rival organizations. Likewise, hunting other Forbidden Users went poorly as their powers were on par with [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md), so it was often sending millions of soldiers to their deaths. 

Middle Gaea Arc Goals - Expand into Political Sector, Secure their power

After the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) made them their official army, the KoG saw a great opportunity to push to turn the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) into its political arm. Wasting little time, [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) and her lackeys immediately began subjugating representatives and employees, before eventually killing the organization's leader, [Troy Talona](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaSaga/CharacterArcs/TroyArcTulia.md). Now, slowly descending into a paranoid madness, [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) begins solidifying her position.

Late Gaea Arc / Early Xalii Arc Goals - Expand their power, Eliminate All Elementals, Hunt other Forbidden Users

Using the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md), [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md) declares war on every single Elemental on the planet, preparing a mass offensive into currently standing Elemental nations on the planet. They wouldn't take it sitting down, however, and those same Elemental nations banded together in an alliance. 
## BACKSTORY
The Knights of Gaea was an underground organization started by a 17 year old [Samae Herba](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SamaeArcTulia.md). Her beginning goal was simple, not very militaristic. Convince nations that Elementals are a scourge not to be trusted through lobbying and fabricated data. When the Knights of Gaea consistently grew in funding, however, she opportunistically expanded to become militaristic, spreading terrorism and executing weaker Elementals.

Later, she began to augment herself and her soldiers with technology as they grew into a more Transhumanist stance, seeing the rapid and unsupervised growth and advancement of technology as the only way to defend humanity against the growing Elemental threat. Together, her and her fighting force, with their swarming and overwhelming tactics, became the best non-Elemental Elemental fighting force.
