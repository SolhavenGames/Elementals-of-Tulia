# PEACE & COOPERATION GUILD - THE FORCE FOR INNOCENTS
<sub>_"Enforced peace is the only way."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
The Peace & Cooperation Guild (PAC for short) is a specialized task force of four Forbidden Users working under the [Strategists for Peace](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/StrategistsForPeace.md). As a specialized task force, they usually end up taking on what's known as High or Extreme Risk operations, including fighting other Forbidden Element user task forces. 

The organization prides itself on its coexistence stance, fighting for a future where Humans and Elementals can live side-by-side safely. They operate under a doctrine known as "Enforced Peace", which some criticize and claim is nothing more than a veil for authoritarianism. Members of the group (and its parent organization), however, have a history of supporting freedom and suppressing authoritarian regimes, so those fears seem to be far-fetched attempts at defaming them.

## STRUCTURE
Despite having an official leader, PAC operates mostly leaderless, as the leader [Kterla Naomi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md) simply gives the task force directives and targets. They usually deliberate among themselves on how to approach and target them, with the role of field leader often shifting based on who seems to have the most knowledge on the operation they were given.

The dynamic among the actual fighters, the Forbidden Users/Task Force, is often leaderless. They operate under a simple yet effective set structure where eat of the members are given set roles to fill during combat. However, this often leaves them very rigid, and if one is temporarily removed from the fight, vulnerable. This is something they've addressed constantly, but have decided to leave alone for the time being.
## GOALS
Middle Gaea Arc Goals - Defend Innocents, Hunt & Destroy harmful organizations

Since it was founded, PAC has only had one main goal: Prevent the deaths of innocents across the globe, as they are caught in the bloody barbaric conflicts of the Burning of Gaea. However, its leader is well-versed in politics and understands that fighting the conflict directly will not simply rid of the problem. Thus, they also hunt to fight the organizations that allow for the fighting to continue.

Late Gaea Arc / Early Xalii Arc Goals - Prevent [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) aggression, Buy Time

The sudden switch in [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) ideology is terrifying for PAC. What they have long been fighting for was now under direct threat by the global government, and the fight would certainly not be easy. Furthermore, as the Xalii Arc began, all four of its Forbidden Users vanished into the Spirit Realm, leaving the [Strategists for Peace](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/StrategistsForPeace.md) to fend for itself and buy time for them to return...
## BACKSTORY
PAC is a very young organization founded by [Kterla Naomi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md), desperate to find some Forbidden Users who shared her end goal of peace and coexistence on the planet of Gaea. She had come from Tulia, but she had been livning on Gaea for a while now. She knew it was possible, she just needed help to achieve it.

The young organization wasn't one to take injustice sitting down. While it originally began like the official Armed Forces for the [Strategists for Peace](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/StrategistsForPeace.md), needing to be sanctioned by nation-states to partake in organizations, they slowly began to stray away from that as it became increasingly clear that they were only subjects to the wills of human nations...
