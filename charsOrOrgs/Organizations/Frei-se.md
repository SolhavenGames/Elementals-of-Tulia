# FREI-SE - TRI-UNION OF THREE CULTURES
<sub>_"We would've been peaceful. Now we will protect every Elemental."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
Frei-Se is a nation in Antarctica led by a royal family, currently just a King and Queen, known simply as King Dei-Sor and Queen Dei-Sor. Formerly peaceful, it was provoked into conflict after an Aerial attack from human nations which began what's known as the Frei-Se War. King Dei-Sor led armies of Watrish Elementals into battle, flooding entire Mega and Super Cities in search of the politicians responsible for the attacks. Notably, the soldiers by his side, including himself, opted to save innocent civilians that had nothing to do with the greater conflict. But they still did destroy the cities, leaving those same civilians homeless.

With growing tensions in the late Gaea & early Xalii arcs, Frei-Se organizes a global alliance of every Elemental nation, promising to protect them from the evils of Samae. But when their King and Queen disappear, they are sure to hide that information, but quickly arm themselves as they will not back down on what they've promised. They will hold the line until their Queen and King return.

## GOALS
Early Gaea Arc Goals - Organize, Stabilize

Before [Spara Dei-Sor](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SparaArcTulia.md) arrived, Frei-Se was a struggling nation which could barely hold on to stability due to outside forces repeatedly assassinating popular political figures. It seemed like the nation would go nowhere, and if it failed, it would set a dangerous precedent for future Elemental nations, forever tarnishing their legitimacy. 

Middle Gaea Arc Goals - Grow, Develop, Defend Themselves

[Spara Dei-Sor](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SparaArcTulia.md) does eventually arrive. She's elected Queen three times. The first two times, she rejects. She tries to set up democratic institutions, but the elected people constantly dismantle them to give her total power. She eventually concedes and agrees to become Queen of Frei-Se. Now, Frei-Se was a proven force, a powerful one. Under their new Queen (whom they built a palace for despite her begging them not to), finally began to develop with a city, Darsk, being built as its capital, and massive walls of ice being built around the entire continent of Antarctica. 

Late Gaea Arc / Early Xalii Arc Goals - Avenge their fallen, protect Elementals across the globe, Wait for their Royals

[Rai Ronal/Dei-Sor](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/RaiArcTulia.md) arrives with a mass Watrish Migration, and they're eventually allowed to stay. Eventually, he marries [Spara Dei-Sor](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SparaArcTulia.md), becoming King Dei-Sor. During their wedding, Frei-Se is the victim of an aerial attack, pushing the nation to war. The new King leads millions of Watrish Elementals on a revenge campaign where they hunt the politicians responsible, and return them to their Queen, who executes them. When the Xalii Arc begins, their monarchs mysteriously disappear. However they choose to stand by their oath to Elemental nations elsewhere, and wait for their monarchs to return...

## BACKSTORY
Frei-Se was started as a wishful nation. Democratic, but disorganized. Elections were often rigged. Leaders came and went. Structures changed too quickly for the citizens to keep track. Popular leaders were executed by outside forces that wanted to keep Frei-Se weak. In fact, Frei-Se was considered so unthreatening that the [ADS](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/AssociationOfDemocraticStates.md) was overjoyed when its population grew, as that was one less fighting against them, instead being trapped in some wishful democratic limbo.

Yet, that all changed when [Spara Dei-Sor](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/SparaArcTulia.md) arrived. She arrived with the intention to help. To assist in building Democratic institutions. Imagine her horror when those same Democratic institutions she helped build elected her leader, and those elected to govern with her instead dismantled them to make her the sole leader, declaring her Queen. Then, when she built more, it happened all over again. Then one more time. They wanted her as Queen, so they would get her as Queen. Queen Dei-Sor led Frei-Se into a prosperity it could not find on its own...
