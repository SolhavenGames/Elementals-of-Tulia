# FREI-SE - TRI-UNION OF THREE CULTURES
<sub>_"We would've been peaceful. Now we will protect every Elemental."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
Frei-Se is a nation in Antarctica led by a royal family, currently just a King and Queen, known simply as King Dei-Sor and Queen Dei-Sor. Formerly peaceful, it was provoked into conflict after an Aerial attack from human nations which began what's known as the Frei-Se War. King Dei-Sor led armies of Watrish Elementals into battle, flooding entire Mega and Super Cities in search of the politicians responsible for the attacks. Notably, the soldiers by his side, including himself, opted to save innocent civilians that had nothing to do with the greater conflict. But they still did destroy the cities, leaving those same civilians homeless.

With growing tensions in the late Gaea & early Xalii arcs, Frei-Se organizes a global alliance of every Elemental nation, promising to protect them from the evils of Samae. But when their King and Queen disappear, they are sure to hide that information, but quickly arm themselves as they will not back down on what they've promised. They will hold the line until their Queen and King return.
