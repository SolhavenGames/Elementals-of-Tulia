# THE WHISPERS - YET ANOTHER REASON TO FIGHT
<sub>_"No eyes deserve the beauty of these planets."_</sub>
## SUMMARY
The Whispers is a highly secretive extinctionist death cult that's actually a break away from [Endsight](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs%2FOrganizations%2FEndsight.md). Yes, its original members were members and staunch followers of [Terkou Lynqo](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc%2FCharacterArcs%2FTerkouArcTulia.md) and his ideology. However, these elementals, after extended exposure to Gaea and memory of Tulia, felt like they were on the wrong side. They were fighting for a planet to belong to them, but deep down, they believed the planet should belong to nobody. Not humans, nor Elementals. Then in came humans who agreed, and The Whispers was born.

Originally founded by a father and daughter, expanded into a global terrorist organization, the Whispers was deeply paranoid to its core. It knew eventually that it would fall, and that led it to be reckless at times, to the point where they were the reason their base of operations was discovered.
