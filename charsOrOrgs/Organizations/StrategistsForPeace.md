# STRATEGISTS FOR PEACE - TREATIES FAILED... TRYING AGAIN
<sub>_"We built a world together. How could you forget so soon?"_</sub>
## SUMMARY
The Strategists for Peace is an organization that centers itself on Peacekeeping operations, for both species. Likewise, they also employ both species, being one of the few Hybrid-Armies on the planet. With this army, they send the soldiers into frontline battles with the end goal of protecting civilians over anything else. They do not fight for specific or particular governments, and they make that clear when they arrive. They are even documented switching sides when nations suddenly switch their attitude towards innocents. 

Like its daughter organization the [Peace and Cooperation Guild](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/PeaceAndCooperationGuild.md), it heavily prides itself on its pro-coexistence stance and beliefs that coexistence could work if supremacist groups were given small voices. While they don't exactly explain how that coexistence should look, they explain that its because they believe it could take multiple unique faces. For now, they continue armed peacekeeping, mediation, and political lobbying to prevent armed conflicts.

## STRUCTURE
The Strategists for Peace operate under a hierarchy much like a business. There's managers of certain sectors, planners, employees, recruiters, all of whom are not directly answering to [Kterla Naomi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md)  or [Wykoi Maita](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/WykoiArcTulia.md) themselves, instead answering to trusted employees who represent their sectors. Not even all information reaches the two, as they really trust their hired higher-ups that much.

The two at the top do cover vast organization wide things. For example, [Kterla Naomi](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/KterlaArcTulia.md), similarly to how she runs the [Peace and Cooperation Guild](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/charsOrOrgs/Organizations/PeaceAndCooperationGuild.md), will issue directives that peacekeepers/treatymakers/deliberators/generals will follow, with [Wykoi Maita](https://github.com/SolhavenGames/Elementals-of-Tulia/blob/main/TuliaArc/CharacterArcs/WykoiArcTulia.md)
doing very similar for the organizations political and economic side.
