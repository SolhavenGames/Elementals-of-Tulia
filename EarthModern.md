# Overview
The Great Terra War has mostly ended as governments collect and organize themselves, creating alliances and rivalries across the globe. Some states aim to reunify Earth under the Terra Union, 
and some aim to prevent this. Nonetheless, the world is still em- broiled in tensions and conflicts, near famines from over urbanization, and confusion and instability as people and land 
frequently switch flags every other month due to war and occupation. Many populations wish for a return to the simpler times under the Terra Union, as not even most people called for its diss-
olution. With each passing day, this dream seems to drift further, as Terra Union loyalists lose land and power.

# Asia & Australia
The current “Frozen Asia” as it’s called is split between two major factions. One led by India, and the other led by China, despite their similar ideologies. The sphere of influence dominated by China holds themself, Korea’s Empire, The Collective of Persia, and Mongolia. Together they form the Legion of Freedom, despite none of them having democratic elections. They wish to build an Asia dominated by them, holding their own ambitions together carefully. Then, the sphere of India, which holds themselves, the Republican Federal State of Sector Iota, the Military Democracy of Oceania, and Japan’s Empire (AKA South Japan). Unlike the Legion, the cohesion of this alliance is merely held together by the desire for joint defense against Legionary expansion. Oceania and Iota often have clashing interests, and Japan despises the alliance’s unwillingness to assist in its ongoing division (more on that later). Together, they created the Tokyo Treaty Organization. Other Asian nations, such as the State of Japan (AKA North Japan), Central Asia, Novikov’s Corporate Empire, South East Asia, and other  disorganized territories do not participate in the cold war, but use the tensions to their leverage to gain beneficial treaties and concessions from both sides. Specifically, nations like the Levant have gained heavy recognition in the Peninsula of Arabia following multiple generous treaties from the Legion. War is handled differently in both alliances. For example, in the Legion, they have a large emphasis on Infantry. There are tens of millions of light infantry with small arms ready to storm across borders if war was to ever be declared. Within these divisions, there is some light armor like IFVs or Armored Cars, but it is very light. They gamble on pure boots with equipment other than large investments. 
In the TTO, they innovate and adapt. They have infantry paired with robotic advance- ments to make them stronger in battle. However, TTO infantry have way less weaponry in their arsenal. This is because they heavily believe in their robotic augmentations to carry the necessary weight for their soldiers. In all simulations they have run, it has done such. They have successfully deployed approximately 4.5 million infantry with full cyber-augmentations which is the highest out of any alliance. Apart from their infantry, they invest in armor, including advanced IFVs and speedy tanks with heavy anti-infantry weaponry after spies discovered Legionary plans. 
Navally, both do the same thing. There has been a naval arms race for the longest time, which the Legion has been losing from the start. TTO imports many ships from outside nations, including Terra Union Naval Commands. But the Legion moves too slow, makes lackluster deals, and is often unwilling to negotiate. It seems they care little for the naval front and have already surrendered it to the TTO.
The airforce is very different. Every nation in both alliances imports stockpiles of thousands of planes from the Airforce Conglomerate in Central Europe, building massive air forces which guarantees a slaughter when the war begins. Massive heavy high-flying bombers with deep striking missiles also guarantee maximum damage, which has encouraged both alliances to build massive anti-air defenses in the super and mega cities.
The situation in Asia is tense, but some see hope. Some populations in oppressed states like India and China flee to Oceania, where laws are looser and there is protected speech, assembly, and press. They then spread massive propaganda of how horrible life is in both countries, attempting to de-legitimize these nations and tear apart the alliances they’ve built. It doesn’t seem to be working yet, and Oceania has not commented on them, despite India demanding action.

	Africa
— — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — 
Africa is a much different continent to Asia. It is not currently frozen in a cold war, and is heavily controlled by loyalists to the former Terra Union. There are only 4 free states that do 
not identify as territories of the Terra Union, those being North West Africa, The Nile Corporation, State Alpha, and State Beta. The Terra Union loyalist states are the Terra Sahara Command 
and the Terra Naval Command in Africa. The Sahara Command, usually  referred to as “Greater Sahara”, commands a large force of loyalists and defenders. The Naval Command, usually referred to 
as “Southern Atlantic Naval East (SAN-E)”, commands a massive naval force, which is used to keep the still chaotic global trade in an even tighter chokehold. Both are based in their own portions 
of Lagos, one of the five super cities in the continent. Over the years, they have been working together to restore Terra Union control over Africa. 
As for other nations: State Alpha and State Beta rest on the southern half of the continent. They named themselves after the zones they were secretly called way before the Terra Union when the 
Silent Eye/Defenders of Democracy were still running rampant, fighting to build it. They also sit in a strong alliance, holding off both the SAN-E and Nile Corporation from their territories, 
while holding their own version of Democratic Socialism strong. 
North West Africa mostly aligns with their European allies to the north of them, mainly to counterbalance the Nile Corporation’s increasingly greedy demands. They hold a similar ideology to 
Democratic Socialism but leaning much further left with more government control, and they are one of the 4 nations on the planet who utilize humanoid robots called “Frontline Bots” for military 
purposes. Uniquely, they are the only nation who has banned them from civilian use. It’s believed that they are banned to prevent current global monopolies from reaching into their country attempting 
to lobby their politicians. 
Finally, the Nile Corporation. They are a large corporate empire with influence across the globe due to their powerful aerial trade economy. They hold the strongest economy globally despite their 
infamy due to expansionist and aggressive ideals. They are led by an executive who has tightly tied their corporations into the success of the nation, making themselves the unwavering head of state 
and demanding total loyalty to their companies. The leader, Royal El-Karim, is arguably the richest man on the planet. He utilizes his corporations and global influence to demand lots, and hold an 
alliance with a different corporation in Eastern Europe. They also use robots for their military, mainly due to their ongoing tensions with North West Africa. 
Africa is arguably the most democratic continent in the world currently, and many flock to State Alpha or State Beta due to the high quality of living in those states. Many refer to the continent 
as the “Bastion of Hope”.

	Europe/Europa
— — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — 
Europe currently sits as a continent constantly at war. Unlike the rest of the world, the Terra Union initially managed to maintain control over major European cities, even after the Declaration
of Deunification. However as time went on, control slipped. The state that came after the Terra Union, named Europa, began to lose major ground as hundreds of small and disorganized rebellions
happened all at once. Years of conflict ensued, and suddenly Europa was pushed back to its most loyal region: The Balkans and Anatolia. What rests where it used to be are now multiple states, 
some still loyal to Europa’s cause, but split off from the nation itself.
Those loyal to Europa rest together in an alliance with them, called the Europa Restoration Army. Firstly, the Atlantic Command, a Terra Union loyalist state with large swathes of navy. It resides
on the British Isles and Iceland, using these islands and robotic naval fleets to hold total control over the Northern Atlantic. Since the Atlantic Command is aligned with the Terra Union, they 
work with their counterparts in Africa and South America to establish near total control of the entire Atlantic Ocean. This nation consolidated power over its land after a massive secessionist
crisis in the British Isles and Iceland led to a bloody civil war that they ended up coming out on top in. Then there is the Republic of The North, which peacefully split from Europa after admin-
istration from the Europa capital became too focused on other things. The Northern Republic succeeded in a peaceful manner, inheriting all assets of Europa in their new territory. It’s considered
the closest nation to the Terra Union while not being a direct Terra Union child state, having nearly every political theory that was used by the Terra Union implemented. Finally, there is Europa
themselves. Based in Instantinople, they lead the drive to reunite Europe under the Terra Union. 
Outside of this alliance rests more states. Reborn State of France, Technocratic Republic of Germany, Democratic Republic of Iberia, The Rus in Kyiv, Mediterranean Command, and Airforce Conglomerate
in Central Europe. In Western Europe, France and Iberia have formed their own alliance to defend themselves against common enemies with North West Africa, called the Northern Atlantic Military
Alliance, NAMA for short. They defend each other from war with pro-TU states or Corporatists/Technocrats on their borders. Their alliance is formidable, despite ideological differences. They sometimes
rely on each other for military assistance, and North West Africa’s robotic infantry troops to defend their frontlines from Germany-Mediterranean Invasion. 
Speaking of, the Germany-Mediterranean Command’s Alliance stands as their main rivals. Two technocratic nations rose from the ashes of the long gone Green Party that formerly held immense power in 
the Terra Union. The Technocratic Republic of Germany and Mediterranean Command are two nations big on innovation. They do not use humans for war, but what they use are not considered robots: they
use human-controlled mech suits. These suits are expensive to build but do their job well, especially against the human forces of France and Iberia. To their east rests the Airforce Conglomerate,
who they continually buy planes from in an uneasy economic web built on blood. 
Central Europe, which is realistically further east than their name would suggest, is a strong military dictatorship meets corporatocracy. The air force controls everything in the nation in a 
council-totalitarian government, and loyalty to the military is demanded. However they rarely ever go to war, and do not have any rivals. They consider themselves to be a neutral nation who have
little ambition beyond their borders, and insights into their country are rare. All that is known is that they somehow produce thousands of top tier planes monthly, and everyone is desperate to buy 
them…
Finally, The Rus in Kyiv, sometimes called simply “Rus” or “Kyiv”. This nation is an odd one, as they call themselves Neo-Communists, but do not show any signs of either. The ADS officially recognizes
them as a decentralized confederation. In the general public’s eyes, Kyiv is renowned for being mysterious, but mostly referred to as a confederation based in Kyiv. Political Scientists agree with this,
and claim the Neo-Communist claim is false and far-off. Their military is often ridiculed for its incompetence, shown in recent wars with its southern neighbor, the Caucasus State. Despite a much larger 
military force, they failed to secure even basic military objectives, and left the war with an ADS enforced ceasefire, giving them minimal land.
Overall, Europe is split between 3 strong alliances who constantly will fight each other, causing major land swaps and instability. The borders of Europe change year by year, and many people flee the
continent due to conscription laws and military oversight.

	North America
— — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — 
North America is home to the second most powerful nation in the world, the Thirteen Republics. It is also home to the largest, most expansive Super and Mega cities, thanks to massive urbanization
projects under the Terra Union. The largest of these would be New York City, which has grown to become comparable to the size of West Virginia. Another major example would be Toronto, which has
grown so large that the country that owns it (Defenders of Toronto) is entirely just Toronto. No rural land, purely urban. 
This is good, and bad. For one, nations with these large cities have massively booming economies, and a heavy influx of migrants. For example, New York City and Miami alike have been getting
hundreds of thousands of European immigrants over the years, despite ongoing wars. However an unfortunate effect of mass urbanization is the loss of farmland. Countries in North America together
make up 45% of the world’s food imports, and commonly fight wars with each other over rural land. People in massively urban countries like the 13 Republics, Toronto, or the Democratic State of 
Central America have commonly been on the verge of famine. If it weren’t for ADS intervention, it’s likely millions would have died by now. 
The Thirteen Republics is an economic powerhouse resting on the East of the continent. Their name has a sprinkle of nostalgia in it, as they actually have 19 Republics in their confederation.
This confederation demands strong political ties but allows heavy economic freedoms, allowing for different republics to develop how they see fit. For example, the New York Republic is heavily 
industrialized, forming massive corporations for consumerism, while the Tennessee Republic tries to take on most of the agricultural demands of the nation. It’s a delicate balance that has been 
holding strong since the nation was founded. As for their military, they hold a strong alliance with their northern neighbor Quebec, who frequently battles with the Empire of Alaska. Quebec itself
is much like the 13 Republics, being an economically decentralized confederation with strong political ties between territories. The alliance between the two holds strong due to these similarities.
In the north rests the Empire of Alaska, a military state focused on expansion and conquest. While it claims to have an emperor, the emperor has never come out in public themself, and thus it is
highly believed that the Emperor of Alaska is a made up figure. What is known about the government of Alaska is that it’s a massively military-dominated unelected parliament, and political
scientists assign them a similar ideology to that of China, as an Imperial Military State. It is quite the fitting title, as Alaska has arguably one of the most impressive armies, despite its low
population. Soldiers of Alaska are trained to fight in the winter, and notoriously do so very well. In fact, the winter of North America always sees heavy raids and invasions from the military of
Alaska due to their unwavering advantage in the winter. All soldiers wear full white and use white equipment, making them hard to spot. Thus, these forces have been named “Death in White”, and
every enemy soldier fears running into them.
There is a militia loyal to the Terra Union near the Great Lakes and what used to be Central Canada. Some of them are even defectors from neighboring nations, including Alaska, allowing them to 
hold somewhat better against Alaska’s army. To their southwest rests a nation that is not exactly Terra Union, but leans to them for defense. Known as the Defenders of Nature, they are a radical
anti-urban group that wish to demolish all super and mega cities globally, returning to a simpler, more rural time. Some mark them as terrorists for so willingly destroying cities, and they only
secured their alliance with the Terra Union militia by agreeing to halt this destruction. To the Militia State’s Northeast rests the Defenders of Toronto, another small Terra Union leaning state.
They are not nearly as radical as the DoN, but they do wish to defend Toronto from invasion, keeping it free from all foreign influence. Together, they make up militaries strong enough to hold off
all that comes their way, but not strong enough to take back anything they’ve lost. They are mostly left alone due to this, as invasions into their lands are costly and unrewarding, leaving the
invader in a weakened state. 
Finally, to the south rests two nations: The Socialist Federal States of The Pacific East, and the Democratic State of Central America. These two are uneasy allies, aiming to strike the Terra Union
militias down. Both nations vehemently hate the Terra Union, and enjoy their new-found freedom. They claim that the union did nothing but harm to the world, and those who want to return it are 
“not only fools, but should be purged from the Earth to prevent their disease from spreading”. Terra Union resolve within their own borders holds strong, as medium portions of both populations 
still call for some pro-Terra Union reforms. Demands like this are usually met with force, and these two countries are not known for being free or fair. They keep their powerful militaries on
constant watch in big cities, ensuring loyalty to the cause and sometimes even conscripting. The military is present in everyday life, and nobody can escape them for even a second. Only during
wartime do the people get some breathing room, and that’s when unrest seems to rise the most. These two nations are unstable, but only time will tell if they stand.

	South America
— — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — — 
South America, like Africa, is incredibly Terra-Union controlled. Unfortunately for its loyalists, it’s also home to an incredibly rich and powerful nation: The Inca Empire. The Inca returned 
themselves to power through blood, sweat, and tears, and now claim to be isolationists on the western half of the continent, simply exporting their resources through airlift to the highest 
buyers. They have incredibly luxurious exports, and are often on high demand. Some like to call them Corporatocratic for this, but they do have democratic elections, which seem to be free and 
fair. The army of Inca is made up mostly of special ops who fight in mountains and jungles. However they are rarely on active  duty, simply waiting for the country to be invaded. Their biggest
threat are usually the neighboring anarchists who frequently invade their territory, but are always sent away.
To the south of the Inca rest the Republican Imperial Federation of Patagonia. They are the only known nation to have a dynasty that serves for life alongside democratic elections. They are a
quite peaceful nation, and only mobilize to keep peace in South America. They also have openly stated their willingness to reintegrate into the Terra Union if the nation was ever restored as 
it was before, but do not wish to mobilize their people for the horrors of war. Paired with their pacifist Inca neighbors, they have little need for an army. The Terra Union forces in Brazil 
have even agreed to economic alliances, which they rarely do when not provided with military alliances as well. Patagonia has clearly won the jackpot, and many move there due to freedoms and 
quality of life. 
The Terra Union holdings in South America have little to fear. First, there’s the Naval Command in South America, commonly referred to as NASA. They currently hold the strongest non-robotic 
naval force in the world, and when paired with other naval Terra Union states, their naval dominance is unmatched. Inland into South America rests a militia state similar to the ones in North 
America, named Terra Union South America Militia, or TU-SAM for short. They mainly fight off Anarchists entrenched in the Amazon Rainforest after agreements with the Reborn Inca Empire. Due to
their proximity with NASA, most of their forces are marines or sailors who became foot soldiers. 
Overall, not much happens in South America. It’s a pretty peaceful continent and a strong base for Terra Union states that currently reside there, as they have no enemies on their land. In fact,
all four nations in South America have signed multiple economic agreements to even further secure the peace in their continent.
